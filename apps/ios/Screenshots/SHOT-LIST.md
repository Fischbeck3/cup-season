# The App Store shot list (IOS-035, 2026-09-05)

**This supersedes the order in `spec/appstore-launch-kit.md` §3.** That list
led with Standings and the league wizard and put the composer third — a
stranger's first two frames were a table and a settings screen, which is the
same defect IOS-035 found in the description. The captures themselves live in
`6.9/` (the 6.9" set, taken from the simulator through the dev hatches).

The rule is unchanged and is the reason this file is short: **real artifacts
only.** No mockup chrome, no invented golfers, no fabricated faces. The dusk
system, champagne only on earned elements, and the ledger pane may appear but
never as the hero shot — the story is the season, not the money.

**Consent:** every screenshot with a real name in it needs the golfer's okay
(the launch kit's own rule). If any member has not okayed store use, capture a
neutralised league instead.

## What came off the front

The retired list opened on **Standings with a story line** and the **league
wizard summary**. Both are still true screens and both stay available — the
standings shot is the natural understudy for #3 until the season page exists —
but neither leads. Nobody downloads an app because its settings screen is
tidy, and the wizard is a screen a golfer meets once, if ever: twelve of
thirteen members of a league never open it at all.

The **"Draft the crew"** caption is deleted outright and not reassigned. All 13
production leagues draw at random and no captain drafts anything (TM-22,
2026-08-29); a caption for a mechanic the engine does not run is the store
listing lying, which is the whole reason IOS-035 exists.
