# Cup Season

Cup Season turns rounds golfers already play into a shared season and a lasting record. These terms are shared across the phone, web, and competition rules.

## Language

**Round receipt**:
The explanation of what a posted round was worth under the competition rules the viewer may see. It connects scoring figures to their source round and can sit alongside that round's photograph, scorecard, and conversation.

**Round scorecard**:
The hole-by-hole strokes recorded for a round. It describes what was played; the round receipt explains how that round counted.

**Course book**:
A saved record of a course and its tees, ratings, slopes, and known hole data. It makes those facts available to a golfer without a signal.

**Tee card**:
The ordered pars and known stroke indexes for a particular set of tees and hole layout. It describes the course being played, rather than a golfer's strokes.
