# CUP SEASON — COPY PASS

## 1. THE ANSWER

The voice is real and it holds. Across seven surfaces and both clients, the character the canon describes is actually on screen — "you can't hurt your squad by playing badly, only by not playing," "84 · beat your number by 0.6 → 7 pts," "Your rounds stay on your card — all of them." Nothing in this audit is hype, nothing is urgency, and only two lines in the whole product are shame. The owner is right that it mostly is in good voice, and he is right about the class of defect he found: **the same thing said twice on one screen is the dominant failure in this app by a wide margin**, and it is already fixed in the one place he found it (`index.html:11312–11320` — the reasoning is logged in the file and the sub-CTA now reads plain "Post a round"). But he is wrong that he found the worst one. The worst one is the sentence the canon marks *verbatim*: **"Cup Season keeps the ledger; the money moves between friends" does not appear verbatim anywhere in either client.** It appears in twenty-two places, in about a dozen different wordings, with three verbs for the noun (books / tab / ledger), three destinations (friend-to-friend / between you / never the money), and two names for the company (Cup Season / "the app") — and on four of those surfaces the sentence is the retired promise D39 killed, wearing a new coat: "never through us" (mailed to every member of every finished league), "never the money" (on the You tab, under a dollar figure), "takes no fee or cut of any prize pool" (on the live legal page). That is not drift at the edges. That is the one law written as a fixed phrase behaving like free copy, on the surface the canon calls the anti-"betting app" vaccine.

## 2. THE PATTERN

**Shape 1 — the second telling.** The owner's own catch, and it generalizes into four sub-forms he can spot on sight:

- *A summary counting rows already on screen with their answers on them.* `index.html:12233` pushes a "Needs you · 3 invites" chip; the invites are sitting above it in `#notifBanner` with an Accept on each (`14339–14346`), and the buddy requests below it with Accept/Decline (`12329–12333`). The chip walks the golfer to a third screen to do what the rows already do. Same shape at `12222` (a "Next round" chip beside the Next tile that owns the fact and has a tee time) and `12241` (a "Month closes in 2 days" chip under a lead card that just said "The month closes in 2 days").
- *A control re-narrating the line above it.* `index.html:17329` fires `toast('Code sent')` in the same tick as `17328` writes "Sent to you@x.com. Type the sign-in code from the newest email." into a live region that stays on screen. `LiveRoundHost.swift:181` labels a button "Enter your email to keep it" four points under a sentence ending "…Enter your email to keep it."
- *Two components painting the same empty state into one scroll.* `index.html:4734` ("THE RACE STARTS WITH THE FIRST POSTED ROUND") and `5034` ("STANDINGS START AT THE FIRST POSTED ROUND") fire off the same `!teams.length` and land ~40pt apart. `CareerRecord.swift:24` and `TrophyMeta.swift:101` both tell a new golfer the case is empty, under one heading.
- *The sharpest version — the same control twice.* `index.html:11013` and `11165` both render `Run it back — Season 2` under a "Season wrapped" heading, about fifteen words apart, for any golfer whose only league has finished. `renderHomeStart` (12370) and `renderHomeHero` (12360) are called in the same function and nothing gates one against the other.

**Shape 2 — the one line that had to be one line is the only line with no home.** Every other law in §3 is enforceable by grep: "commissioner," "PvI," "differential," "unlock" are *tokens*, and a token can be found. A **verbatim** law has no violating token — it has only near-misses, and near-misses are invisible to search. So the ledger sentence was retyped once per money surface by whoever wrote that surface, each retyping slightly better prose than the last, and nothing ever failed. The named bands survived the same twelve months intact because `CSBands` / `GuideCopy.swift:79–83` hold them in one place and everyone calls them. The ledger line has no `CSBands`.

**Shape 3 — the copy that lives outside the copy file is the copy that's wrong.** This one is diagnostic and tells the owner where to look next. Almost none of these defects are in the `*Copy.swift` files the architecture set aside for copy. They are inline strings in view files (`StandingsPane.swift:41/90`, `YouSections.swift:85/86`, `IndividualRaceView.swift:52`, `TourCardSheet.swift:73/92/103`, the two event setup sheets) and, on the web, strings hand-assembled at their call site next to a producer that already exists. The cleanest case: `STAGE_LABEL` is six words in one object at `index.html:6154`, with a comment above it saying the labels "could not agree" when they were hand-derived — and `14417` hand-derives them anyway, emitting two strings outside the six, including "Squad formation," the exact word the comment records as retired. Same story on the phone: `BoardText.easeCaps` (`BoardText.swift:38`) is a faithful port of the web's name-restoring easer, and `HomeStream.swift:131` is a nine-line lowercase-everything knockoff — so one server post reads "Wes Tucker takes August" on the Board and "Wes tucker takes august" on Home. **Where a producer exists and is called, the copy is right. Where a producer exists and is bypassed, the copy is wrong.** There is not one exception in this audit.

## 3. THE FIXES, RANKED

`W` = web (`index.html` / `legal.html`), `i` = iOS, `S` = server. **TWIN** = must change on both clients in the same commit or the pair drifts.

### First: rule on the ledger line, then sweep it

The canon sentence is **"Cup Season keeps the ledger; the money moves between friends."** The house has been drifting toward "…between you," which is worse and worth rejecting deliberately: "between you" addresses the reader as a party to a transaction; "between friends" names what the transaction *is*, which is the whole point of the vaccine. Rule once, put it in one constant per client (`CS.LEDGER` / `MoneyCopy.ledger`), and make every row below a call, not a retyping.

## 5. THE ONE RULE TO ADD

They do share a cause, and §3 does not name it. §3 is entirely a vocabulary code — say "the Pro", never say "PvI", the verb is "run it back". Every one of those laws is enforceable because a violation is a *token* you can grep for. But the two biggest failures here have no banned token in them. "Cup Season keeps the tab" contains no forbidden word. "Setup — not open yet" contains no forbidden word. And the owner's own catch — a line and the button under it both explaining the same thing — contains no forbidden word either. Each of them is a *placement* failure: a fact stated by a surface that does not own it, or a fixed phrase retyped by a surface that should have called it.

The law, phrased the way §3's are:

> **One fact, one place.** Every fact on a screen belongs to exactly one element — the one closest to the action. A control names the press; the line above it carries the why; a summary never counts rows that are already on screen with their answers on them. If two elements can both say it, the one further from the action goes silent.

That covers the worked example, all of Shape 1, and most of Shape 3's symptoms. It does not cover the ledger line, and it should not pretend to — that failure is not a voice problem but a sourcing one, and the honest fix is an amendment to §3's existing ledger bullet rather than a new law:

> *…verbatim everywhere money appears — **from one constant per client. A retyped copy is the defect the day it is written, because it is already the version that will drift.***

Add that clause and the reason this happened stops being invisible. The named bands survived twelve months because `CSBands` and `GuideCopy` hold them and everyone calls them. The stage vocabulary survived on the web because `STAGE_LABEL` holds it — except at the one call site that hand-rolled it. The ledger line has twenty-two wordings because nothing holds it. **A law written as a fixed phrase needs a fixed home, or it is only a wish.**
