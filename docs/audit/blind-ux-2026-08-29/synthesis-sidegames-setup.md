# Synthesis — Side games & League setup

Blind UX audit, 2026-08-29 · web client at http://127.0.0.1:8791/ (unstamped `v23 · __CS_VERSION__` build on branch `native/m0-foundation`), plus the iOS screen survey.

## 0. How to read this

Three labels are kept strictly apart throughout:

- **OBS** — what a tester saw or did. Every OBS cites the raw report, the screenshot, and the exact UI copy. Nothing in an OBS line comes from the spec or the code.
- **INT** — the synthesizer's interpretation of *why*, including code reading. Where an INT rests on a code path it cites the line.
- **INTENT** — what the product means to do, from `spec/spec-v1.0.md` (§), `spec/decision-log.md` (D-numbers), `spec/gameplay-modes-working.md` (modes §), `Cup-Season-Guide.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, or the UI copy in `index.html` (line numbers).

The rule the brief gave, applied without exception: **if the spec explains it and the UI didn't, that is the finding.** Several things below are "working as designed" and are still failures, because the design lives in a document the golfer never sees.

Raw reports (all read in full): `raw/agent5-organizer.md` (org), `raw/agent3-league-novice.md` (nov), `raw/agent6-new-joiner.md` (join), `raw/agent1-casual.md` (cas), `raw/agent2-competitive.md` (comp), `raw/agent4-skeptic.md` (skep), `raw/agent7-retention-observer.md` (obs), `raw/ios-screen-survey.md` (ios). Screenshots live under `screenshots/<session>/`; below they are written `session/file.png`.

**Coverage caveats that bound every claim here.** No tester ran a Ryder, a Major, a Wolf round, or a Sunningdale round; nobody reached a Cup Final; every Papago Grind tester was pre-season (first tee Sat Sep 5, audit Aug 29), so nothing here observes a side game or a setup decision *inside* a live season. The install banner overlapping the FAB is the single most damaging discovery bug because it silences the only entrance.

### 1.3 Understanding — do users know what each game is and how it works?

**OBS.** Every game's explanation is a one-line blurb that appears only after the game is selected, and the player-count rule appears only on tapping "Tee off" (org ORG-39: "the Wolf/Match-play player-count rules only appear after selecting"; index.html:9070–9080 shows the count messages fire on the tee-off click). Blurbs verbatim from index.html:9061–9065, as seen by all who reached the screen:

- Match play: "Singles (2) or 2v2 net best ball (4). Keep scoring; we tally the match as you go." + "Stake per side · $0 = bragging rights".
- Wolf: "A round of Wolf — needs four. We run the rotation and the side tally; scores still post." + "Dollars per point".
- Skins: "Low net takes the hole's skin; ties carry the pot. Two to four players; scores still post." + "Dollars per skin".
- Sunningdale Rules: "Match play, no handicaps — go 2 down and you get a stroke until you climb out. Singles or 2v2. Win a hole while ahead to bank a unit." + "Bank unit".

Per game:

**Match play — understood, and the strokes line is the best copy in the flow (OBS).** org: "'Casey vs Marco · Strokes off the low man (Casey) · estimated card — no real stroke index: Marco gets 4: holes 4, 9, 13, 18.' — this is exactly the sentence golfers argue about on the first tee, and the app writes it for you." (`87-live-matchplay-setup.jpg`). comp: "the setup blurb is excellent and specific ('Strokes off the low man (Priya): Casey gets 9 — the 9 hardest holes · Marcus gets 6: holes 3, 6, 9, 16, 17, 18')." Undefined at point of use: "net best ball", "strokes off the low man", "SI" (cas A10 glossary: "SI ✱ … Undefined"; org: "the phrase *stroke index* is jargon for most weekend golfers"). cas, who has no index, was silently given "EST 18.0 IDX" and "the match strokes derive from that estimate; no explanation of where 18.0 comes from or how to change it" (cas A21, `70-I03-setup-done.jpg`) — the sentence "Leave index blank for an estimated 18" is on the setup screen (index.html:3030) but under the guest row, not next to his own chip.

**Wolf — not understood by anyone who did not already know it (OBS).** nov N-23: "**Wolf is never explained.** If you don't know Wolf, this line tells you nothing (who is the wolf, what's a rotation, what's a point)." cas: "I don't know how Wolf is played and the app doesn't say." obs glossary: "live-game formats (Sunningdale, Wolf, Skins), 'bank', 'units'" listed as confusion debt. **But comp, with four players seated, saw a fuller rule:** "wolf tees last, picks a partner after any drive — or goes lone for 3. Last two holes (17–18): last place is the wolf" (comp timeline 15:47). **INT:** the Wolf explanation exists in the product and is shown only once four are seated — i.e. the rules are gated behind the precondition ("needs four") they would help a group decide to meet. **INTENT:** modes §3.2 says the app's job is "a banner that says whose turn it is and what they're deciding" — during play, not before it; nothing in spec or decisions covers teaching the game at setup. D9's stated reason for pulling skins forward was "zero rules teaching for most foursomes" — an admission that Wolf needs teaching, which was then not designed.

**Skins — understood, the live ledger loved (OBS).** nov: "The live ledger is instant and readable — this is the best-feeling part of the app." comp ran 18: "HOLE 10 WORTH 3 SKINS · THRU 9 … JORDAN → MARCUS $35" and the finish "PRIYA 7 · MARCUS 6 · CASEY 3 … DONE · 2 SKINS DIED CARRIED" (`56-live-hole18.jpg`). Residual jargon: "'2 SKINS DIED CARRIED' is *confusing* wording" (comp glossary); "'Riding' = skins carried; guessable from context, never defined" (nov). nov also puzzled over "0 STK" for two 18-index players ("strokes off the low man" with equal indexes — correct, unexplained).

**Sunningdale Rules — a name and a sentence, no (i) (OBS).** org ORG-39: "'Sunningdale Rules' — I have no idea what that is; no (i)." nov: "I have never heard of this; the one-liner is OK but 'bank a unit' is opaque." cas: "Never heard of it; the sentence helps a little." obs, reading a finished board row "Sunningdale: Jerecho Fischbeck & Jade def. Will & Isaak 3&2 · no handicaps · bank: Jerecho Fischbeck & Jade 6 units", listed "Sunningdale", "bank", "6 units" as undefined. **INTENT:** D74 chose this game for "groups without handicaps" and its amendment explains the "Rules" suffix at length (the bare word read as the venue; iOS data detectors mangled "Sunningdale:" into a URI). D74's own tradeoff line says "the bank's owner semantics are subtle — the status card must always name who holds it and what a win does." The setup blurb does not; the obs board row ("bank: … 6 units") does not say what a unit is worth or who pays.

**The stepper (OBS, three testers).** "the FIRST tap of + sets the hole to par, every further tap adds one. I tapped + six times on a par 5 expecting 6 and got 10; Mike got 9" (nov N-22, `91-live-h1.jpg`, `92-live-h2.jpg`); org ORG-38 "the first tap surprised me"; comp "first tap opens at par; then ± (fast)". **INTENT:** §13.1 "card found → stepper on par" — designed, and the setup card says "The stepper opens on each hole's par" (index.html:3025), a sentence the iOS survey flagged as "a UI widget name leaking into copy." nov's 10-on-a-par-5 is the exact "wrong score on hole 1" this design invites when unannounced at the moment of the first tap.

**Nassau (OBS).** nov: "Nassau — the most common buddy bet in America — is absent." **INTENT:** §13.2 roadmap. Not a defect; a known gap the picker does not acknowledge.

### 1.4 How they affect the competition — and whether users can tell

**INTENT.** Side games "never touch cup points" (§13.2; modes §5). Every complete member card posts as an ordinary round, "✓ attested by construction" (§13.1, §6). The result is a parallel ledger and a settlement card. Guests have "zero contact with the season" (§13.3).

**OBS.** All four testers who teed off inferred the right model and none found it stated. The only sentences that carry it are "scores still post" (Wolf/Skins blurbs), "League members post to the season; guests play every game, post nothing" (setup), and "Only league members' rounds post to the season" (the attest paragraph). cas A36: "Nothing in one place states that side games don't affect season points while the gross score does … I couldn't answer 'does winning the match matter for the Cup'." org: "They do not affect the season standings as far as any copy says." comp: "Nothing on the board card links the side game to standings." nov built the model from the Pot tab: "integrated at the ledger level, not at the points level."

**OBS — the pre-season lie repeats here.** "Finish round & post to season" (index.html:3075) was shown to nov and cas a week before first tee: "I'm a week before the first tee, so per the Clubhouse that would NOT post to the season. The button says it will. Same contradiction as the basic post." (nov). comp's four cards posted "4 CARDS TO THE SEASON" (`58-live-posted.jpg`) and then "Standings still all zeros for everyone (five posted rounds today between us)." org's settlement read "**Round posted** · 0 CARDS TO THE SEASON · … Casey — NOT POSTED · INCOMPLETE CARD" (ORG-27, `92-settlement.jpg`) — a headline contradicting its own body.

**OBS — the finish sheet's two buttons.** cas A20, `75-I08-finish-attempt.jpg`: "'Finish — no complete member card to post' and 'This one was casual — post nothing' — I genuinely cannot tell the difference." **INT (code):** index.html:9318–9322 — the primary reads "Post N cards to the season" when any card is complete and degrades to "Finish — no complete member card to post" when none is; the secondary always sends `p_casual: true`. When no card is complete, both post nothing; the difference (the game result and settlement still land on the board for "Finish") is not on the sheet. org saw the same sheet and called it "the clearest 'what happens if something is wrong' moment in the app" — correct about the missing-holes list, wrong about the buttons, which is the point: the sheet explains the cards and not the choice.

**OBS — "Attested" is defined only here.** org ORG-07: "This paragraph is where 'Attested' (the mystery word from the bylaws review) is finally explained — on the live scoring screen, after the round has started. It should be on the wizard." nov N-11 same. **INTENT vs shipped copy — CONFLICT with D13.** D13 (2026-07-15): "'Vouch' is the only user-facing word, everywhere." Shipped: "VERIFICATION Attested" (bylaws), "auto-attested" (live), "✓ ATTESTED" (settlement), "verified + attested" (Cutthroat card), "Attested PLAYED WITH THE GROUP" (receipt). The decision was never applied and the word it retired is now load-bearing in four places.

**OBS — a round written to your card by someone else's game.** cas A7: "'You · Played to your number · 103 gross · Papago · Blue' appeared on my card from another member's skins game ('Attested · PLAYED WITH THE GROUP · Played with Casey, Marcus, Priya'); no notification, no confirmation; my index count moved. … to a normal golfer, someone else just wrote a 103 on my record." (`86-K03-103-receipt.jpg`, `87-K04-scorecard.jpg`). **INTENT:** §13.1 "Finish → every round posts at once"; D85 "any phone can fix any score; the newest edit wins"; D86 sends a push nudge to rostered members at tee-off (the "doorbell"). **OBS on the doorbell:** the promised "Continue your round banner is waiting on Home" never appeared for cas (A8), and every tester's console carried `[live-resume] server query failed: Could not embed because more than one relationship was found for 'live_rounds' and 'live_round_players'` on every load (org, nov, cas, skep, join, obs). **INT:** D86's invitation path is broken server-side in this build, so the one signal that would have told Jordan "you're on Priya's card" did not fire; what remains is a 103 with no consent step, and the only recourse is the unlabeled ✕ on the You tab. comp separately noted the flip side: "one phone can post + attest four cards (I did)."

**OBS — scrap.** cas A6: "'Scrap this round' (`#discardBtn`, enabled) does nothing on click — no confirm, no toast, no state change, no console error; tested via UI click and JS click." (`93-L03-scrap-test.jpg`). nov, on a different round: "'Scrap this round' is a two-tap confirm: first tap turns the button into red 'Tap again to scrap — nothing posts, for anyone'. Good pattern, no modal." (`93-scrap-confirm.jpg`). Two observations, opposite results; cas's round had "Group phones" opened and a league mate seated, nov's had a guest. Not resolved here — flagged for reproduction.

### 1.5 Money and "the books"

**INTENT.** "App tracks, Venmo moves, nothing held" (§13.2); "Running ledger, always visible … Finish → settlement card: minimized transfer set" (modes §3.3); D77 (the result leads every settlement string), D78 (hole strip + card), D89/D90 (a settlement card that can travel). The season pot is a separate ledger: "The pot is what the roster owes; payouts are made from what was collected" (§7, D106). Pride stakes are "unmistakably NOT money: no dollar rendering … no conversion to the pot, ever" (D64). **No decision says whether side-game cash appears on the Pot tab.** `game_results` holds the ledger server-side (D107 §5 mentions "the wolf/skins ledger (game_results)"); the web client never reads it for the Pot tab (no `game_results` reference in index.html).

**OBS — side-game cash is not on the books.** nov N-24: "live settlement 'DANA → MIKE $2' disappears after the round; Pot › Post a stake says 'Pride, on the books — never money' … the settle-up is the thing buddies argue about at the bar, and the app that 'keeps the books' doesn't keep these." comp C-28: "Pot tab unchanged ($250 · $0 collected), 'THE OTHER STAKES' lists only 'Loser buys the beers'; the $60 skins settlement is NOT on the books anywhere in the Pot tab" (`60-pot-after-skins.jpg`). org: "the $5 match is not a 'stake'." skep, without running one: "side money ($5) is on the board the same day the real pot is '$0 collected'."

**OBS — four money nouns.** org ORG-24: "buy-in, pot, stake-per-side, stake — with different rules each, and the Pot tab's 'Post a stake' explicitly *cannot* be money while the side game's 'stake per side' explicitly *is*." join glossary: "Pot / pot sheet / the tab / the books — four names." nov: "USER ASSUMPTION (before): side-game dollars go on the Pot ledger. ACTUAL: only the buy-in pot is money."

**OBS — pride stakes work and confuse.** cas posted one: "'Loser buys the beers · Jordan vs Casey · **Loser hosts the cookout** · rides on First round of the season · [Settle] [✕]' because the prefilled terms stayed … 'Settle' is a button I can press by myself on a bet with Casey — who wins?" (A30, `40-E06-stake-posted.jpg`). comp: posted "Low net of September · Priya vs Casey · Loser gives 2 a side next time", it appeared on the board — "Fun, social, low-stakes." skep: "the stake card ('Loser buys the beers') — the one object in the app that reads like our group text." **INTENT:** D64 — "either PARTY confirms a settle"; templates prefill terms (index.html:11189). The "who wins?" ambiguity is D64's own "v1 trim": "every settle is a party tap — the auto-hook onto duel/match/callout results is the fast-follow." cas found the trim.

**OBS — settlement artifacts are the app's best objects, and they travel badly.** org: "The settlement card is the artifact I'd actually screenshot into the group chat — 'MARCO PAYS CASEY $5' is the whole point." obs: the match-play and Sunningdale scorecard sheets are "the best-looking objects in the app — and they have no share/export control" (`42-matchplay.jpg`, `43-sunningdale.jpg`). skep SK-25: "'Match play: Priya def. Casey 2 UP THRU 3 · $5 on the line' … the app flags it in red as incomplete while still calling it 'def.' — so it reads as a test artifact rather than a result" (`45-matchplay2.jpg`). org: "Three share buttons plus a revoke on one sheet; I still could not see any link text in this browser"; "Copy link" for the guest failed with "Copy failed — try again" (clipboard denied). comp: "'Share the card' produced no visible feedback." **INTENT:** D77/D78/D89 were all about making this artifact travel; the headless harness cannot see a native share sheet, so the share verdict is bounded — but the *fallback* (no visible link, no copied state) is what every tester saw.

### 1.6 Social interaction and encouragement

**OBS — what works.** Board stories post automatically and read well: "Board tab: 'Sat · Aug 29 · ◆ Match play: Casey def. Marco 1 UP THRU 3 · $5 on the line' … Side games do post to the league board — this is the social hook, and it works without configuration." (org `93-board-after-live.jpg`). comp: results "were visible on the league board within seconds ('Priya took 7 skins and $60 · Priya 7, Casey 3, Marcus 6 · $5 a skin · 2 carried died · SCORECARD ›')" (`59-home-after-skins.jpg`). Guests: "'Marco · No account needed — the link is their pencil now and their recap after' … Clear and social." (org, `90-group-phones.jpg`). nov, from an empty league: "the LIVE skins game with a guest, and the guest's promised 'recap text with scorecard and an invite' — that is the one mechanic that works with zero members and could pull people in." comp: "Setup with league members: trivial — chips for Jordan/Sam/Casey/Marcus are right there."

**OBS — what does not.** Encouragement to *create* one: "none — no prompt, no 'try a match this Saturday'" (org); "Weakly — the ⊕ door's LIVE card is first and orange-dotted; the tee-sheet form has no 'game' field" (comp, `68-plan-tee.jpg`); "Mildly … No nudge from Home or the Clubhouse" (nov). The tee sheet — the product's own "before" door — cannot carry a game, so a foursome planned in the app arrives at the tee with no game set. Rivalry: "the app itself never picks a rival for me" (comp); obs: "no taunt/nudge/share control exists" on any rivalry surface.

**OBS — the weekly clash is invisible.** obs, a member of two live leagues at week 6 and week 4, saw no clash chip on Home, Standings, or the board (`04-home-first.jpg`, `07-league-full.jpg`, `19-sub-Board.jpg`); his verdict was "It never gives me a rival, a gap to the leader, or a 'this round matters because' line." comp: "'Next round matters because…' signal: none." **INTENT:** D52 decided the clash "for LAUNCH, not gated on month-1 proof" as "the Tue–Sun appointment beat"; D108 (2026-08-28) is the build packet and both clients carry the chip (`renderClash()` index.html:4613–4640: "THE CLASH · A v B · THROUGH SAT"; `WeekClash.swift`). The client comment at index.html:3484 reads "without week_clashes yet all render nothing." **INT:** either the D108 migration has not been pushed to the linked project or the daily tick has not yet opened a week — verify with `supabase db query --linked "select * from week_clashes"`. Either way, the single mechanic designed to answer the retention persona's top complaint was not observable in the audit, one day after its build commit.

### 1.7 Integrated or bolted on?

**OBS, unanimous shape.** Integrated *at the moment of play* (one door, the strokes line, cards post, the board hears it); bolted on *relative to the season* (nothing on Standings/Pot/League/Home/wizard/welcome refers to them; money settles off the books; the live setup has "its own course search (that ALSO asks for rating/slope by hand), its own 'foursome' concept, no mention of Desert Dogs anywhere on it. Bolted on, well made." — nov). cas: "Half and half." comp: "Integrated in the flow, but the money is NOT on the 'books'." org: "integrated in the ⊕ and board, bolted on relative to the league room."

### 2.1 The root cause, and what it took down with it (INT, verified in source)

`lockBylaws()` (index.html:15122–15219) performs, in order: `league_settings.update(...)` → `seasons` insert (or reuse) → `form_squads` RPC (non-solo) → `leagues.update({phase, name})` → `renderPhase()` → **`return { emails: emails.length, invited: staged.length, nextPhase }`** (line 15218). `staged` was deleted by D97 ("removed: … `state.wizInvitees` and its three constant-zero readers, the staged-invitee loop in `lockBylaws`") on 2026-08-04; this fourth reader survived. Every server write completes, the function throws on its last line, and the click handler (index.html:15471–15507) lands in `catch` → `toast(humanError(err,'Lock failed.'))` (line 15504). `humanError` (index.html:4106) has no pattern for a ReferenceError, so the user sees the generic sentence.

The consequences are the setup audit's spine:

1. **Six false failures for both organizers** (OBS above). The server state was "locked" from the first tap.
2. **The invite link never appeared for anyone.** The `try` block's success path is `switchView('home'); toast('Bylaws locked'); openLockShare(nextPhase, …)` — and `openLockShare` (index.html:14135–14170) is the **only** surface that renders the URL: "You're invited to **{name}** on Cup Season · `cupseason.app/?join={CODE}` · [Share the invite link] · [Later — it lives in the league room]". Because the throw precedes it, the sheet the wizard promises ("Lock opens the invite link — one link fills the league") was never shown. Every "Share the invite link → toast 'Invite code: X', no link" finding (org ORG-03, nov N-05, cas A22, join J-27, skep SK-17) is downstream of this one line: the room's share buttons fall back to a code toast when `navigator.share` and the clipboard are unavailable, and the one screen that prints the link in text was unreachable.
3. **Retries re-ran the writes and produced a chimera.** nov's fifth/sixth attempts had Teams switched to Solo. `lockBylaws` re-ran: settings rewritten with `structure:'solo'`, the existing season reused, `form_squads` skipped, `nextPhase = 'season'`, `leagues.phase` set to `season`. The two squads from attempt one still existed. Result (OBS): Home "SEASON LIVE — Rounds count from today" beside Clubhouse "BEFORE FIRST TEE … SQUADS LOCKED" with "Squad 1 / Squad 2" (`nov/34-reload-home.jpg`, `35-clubhouse-1.jpg`) — nov N-03, N-10.
4. **"Minimum four to tee off" was bypassed by construction.** D58 made `start_season` refuse <4 golfers so that "'minimum four to tee off' stops being copy and becomes a gate." The solo branch of `lockBylaws` never calls `start_season`; it writes `phase:'season'` directly (line 15208–15211). A one-person Solo league is live the moment it locks. nov N-06: "the league locked with 1 player and Home says SEASON LIVE." This is a client-side direct write with game consequences — the class CLAUDE.md says should be an RPC.
5. **The organizer could re-lock indefinitely.** The Home hero (index.html:10078–10115) computes `solo = n<=1` and, for any league with one member, renders "Just you so far. Lock the bylaws and the invite link is yours. [Lock it in and invite your crew]" regardless of `phase`. After a *successful* lock (phase `draft`), org's Home still said "FORMING · … [Lock it in and invite your crew]" (`org/35-home-after-failed-lock.jpg`) and led back to step 3. D96 (which added this CTA) says its tradeoff is "a league that is named and crewed but deliberately unlocked will be told to lock every time" — it did not anticipate telling an already-locked league to lock.

**INTENT worth naming here.** D40 (2026-07-20): "The public share link/code moves to AFTER lock … `openLockShare` … is the single share moment." D96/D97 (2026-08-04) reaffirmed "the wizard's remaining invite story is one story: lock, then share." The design has one invite moment and this build cannot reach it.
