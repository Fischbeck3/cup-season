# Blind UX audit 2026-08-29 — Gameplay loop, information hierarchy, competition visibility, side games

**For:** the product owner. **Build audited:** `34d20b6` — production cupseason.app serves a byte-identical `index.html`, so every client defect below is live today, not "on a branch". **iOS:** `apps/ios` on the same branch, static landing screens only.

**Method in one line.** Seven blind personas drove the real product in a phone-viewport headless browser on real prod accounts (organizer, league novice, new joiner, casual, competitive, skeptic, and a read-only mid-season observer on the owner's own account), plus an iOS screen survey; synthesis agents then read everything with the spec open; the top five findings were adversarially validated by 15 independent agents (none refuted). This document writes up the **loop** — what a golfer does from one round to the next and whether the product carries them — the **information hierarchy** of every screen, whether **competition is visible**, and whether the **side games** earn their place. The setup wizard, terminology, rules mental model, retention/monetization and the master issue list have their own documents in this folder.

## 0. The verdict in one paragraph

The loop has one genuinely strong beat — **enter the result** (course search → tee → rating/slope autofill → live points preview → a receipt that shows the differential arithmetic) — and one strong side-loop (live skins / match play with strokes off the low man and a netted settlement card). Everything downstream of the posted round leaks energy: the form promises points, the ceremony withholds them, the standings show zero, and nothing on any of six screens says why (6 of 6 posting testers; 4 of 6 wrote a verbatim "I think it's because the season doesn't start till the 5th, but nothing on the screen said that"). The sign on "vs your index" is read backwards by golfers on five surfaces (6 of 7). Upstream, every member's Home leads with the organizer's lock button and a guilt bar. The endgame — the thing the product is named after — exists as one bylaws row four taps deep behind a collapsed panel; 8 of 8 personas could not explain how the Cup is won. Competition is legible on exactly two surfaces (a mid-season Home hero for the last-opened league, and every hole of a live side game) and nowhere pre-season. The product already knows most of this: D4, D24, D26, D51, D52/D108, D81, D105 each name a missing beat; several are built server-side (`season_scenarios`, `week_clashes`, `cup_final_race`) or in the client (`renderClash`, `index.html:4589`) and were visible in no league any tester touched. Persona scores: rulesClear 3–5 across all eight; stakesMeaningful 3–6; wouldInvite 3–6; wouldPay 2–5. The highest single scores in the audit are sideGamesCompelling 8 (ORG, COMP) — from the two who ran a game.

## Part 1 — The micro loop, transition by transition

*Before round → Choose / see competition → Play golf → Enter result → See outcome → See season impact → Compare against friends → Anticipate next round.* Walked with a posting account by ORG, NOV, JOIN, CAS, COMP, SKEP; read-only in-season by OBS; first-screen only on iOS.

Scoring key (1–10): **Clarity** — does the golfer know what is happening · **Motivation** — does it make them want the next step · **Friction** — 10 = frictionless · **Emotional payoff** — does it land · **Information quality** — is what is shown true, complete and consistent with the other screens.

### 1.2 T1 · Before the round → choose / see the competition

**OBSERVATION.** Pre-season member Home (JOIN, CAS, COMP, SKEP; `screenshots/join/21-P-home-member-full.jpg`, `screenshots/skep/09-home-full.jpg`): hero "THE PAPAGO GRIND · FORMING · 7 days to first tee. 5 golfers in. The bylaws lock at the tee." with a single full-width orange **"Lock it in and invite your crew"**; below it "NEXT · Open · PLAN A ROUND", "MONTH CLOSES in 2 days", "Monthly floor · 2 rounds a month. Miss it and your squad loses 5 points…". Four of four players read the lock button as their instruction (JOIN §5, CAS §4, COMP 15:32, SKEP probe 1); it opens "CREATE YOUR LEAGUE · REVIEW THE BYLAWS, THEN LOCK IT IN" with a live **"Lock the bylaws & form the squads"** and a "Cancel" that offers "Cancel this league? … discards it completely" — the server refused (`commissioner only`, JOIN). "What round am I supposed to play?" — every poster answered "there is no round". NOV read "NEXT · Open" for twenty minutes as "the next league round is open" (it is the tee-sheet calendar, N-20). Mid-season Home (OBS `screenshots/obs/05-home-full.jpg`): "FELLAS · WEEK 6 OF 26 · **1st** · — HELD · You lead by 22 points over Jade. · AUG FLOOR 1/2 ▬▬ 1 MORE · 2D" — the only surface in the audit where a golfer sees a named opponent and a gap before playing. The second league, where he is losing, is invisible until opened in the Clubhouse (OBS #4). No screen anywhere says what the next round is *worth*. No tester saw a weekly clash chip.

**INTERPRETATION.** Before a round the app answers "what is the state of the league" (for the Pro) or "what are you late on" (for a member), never "what is at stake for you today". The forming hero (`index.html:10072–10116`, CTA at `:10102`) tests `n<=1` and the league name, never `CS.member.role` — D96 added the CTA reasoning only about a solo Pro, silently reopening the door D40 closed at `enterLeague` (validated TOP-3, 3/3 verdicts). `heroMyRung()` (`index.html:10155–10198`) renders position + gap and a forward line **only in the tie case** ("Level with X. Your next round breaks the tie."); D81's "on quiet days points forward" was never built for the common case. D51 (the stake line — "what your next round is worth", decided 2026-07-21) has no client code. D52/D108's clash is built (`renderClash`, `index.html:4589`; hidden "until a row exists", `:3482`) and no audited league had a `week_clashes` row.

**IMPACT.** The member's biggest button is one they are afraid to touch and that nearly deleted a friend's league; "what do I do now?" — the vision's "App Should Feel Alive" question — is answered with an admin tool and a guilt bar. Motivation to play the next round has to come from outside the app.

**RECOMMENDATION.** Gate the forming hero on role (the phone already does: `HomeView.swift:567` checks `isPro` and shows members "The bylaws lock at the tee." with no button). Members get "First tee Sat Sep 5 · Casey draws the squads at the tee · practice rounds build your number until then" plus a CTA they can act on (plan a round / post a practice round labelled as such). Role-gate `switchView('wizard')` so no route can reach the wizard uninvited (the You-tab symptom itself was a harness artifact, M-031; the gate is still right). Build D51 on Home and the composer; put the clash chip on Home, not only Standings, once `week_clashes` has rows in prod.

### 1.3 T2 · Choose the competition → play golf

**OBSERVATION.** The ⊕ door (`screenshots/org/61-post-sheet.jpg`): three cards — "● LIVE Play now — score the group live", "Post a round — after you play · Gross + tee, 20 seconds", "Plan a tee time — before". All seven web testers found it and called the before/during/after model the first thing that clicked; side games were discovered here without instruction by 7/7 and nowhere else upstream (Part 5). Reaching the ⊕: the "Get the full-screen app" banner sat exactly on the FAB — two taps did nothing until "Not now" (ORG-09; NOV's first guest "Add" tap hit the banner's "Add", N-27). Plan a tee time: "Put a round on the tee sheet — BUDDIES & LEAGUE MATES SEE IT THE MOMENT YOU POST — Day [2026-09-05] · Tee time · Course · Note"; it defaults the day to first tee ("a small, smart nudge", NOV) and has **no game field** (COMP `screenshots/comp/68-plan-tee.jpg`). Live setup (`screenshots/org/85-live-door3.jpg`): course → tee/rating/slope → 18/9 → "Enter the pars" → THE FOURSOME 1/4 → guests → "GAME FOR THIS ROUND · PICK ONE" last on the page; each game explains itself only after selection. On iOS the live screen has no title, no exit and no game named (`screenshots/ios/11-live.jpg`, IOS 1.9; the D110 addendum added Close after the survey). "Group phones — everyone can score" promises league mates "a *Continue your round* banner is waiting on Home" — it never appeared for CAS (A8) and every boot in five sessions logged `[live-resume] server query failed: Could not embed because more than one relationship was found for 'live_rounds' and 'live_round_players'`. iOS lists five different "do a round" entry points (Post tab · + on Home · Play now · Live round · Put a round on the tee sheet), none explained (IOS §2.6).

**INTERPRETATION.** The door is the cleanest screen in the product (D82 three tenses, D110 LIVE leads) and the only place the three verbs are explained; every other entry point ("Live round" inside a NEXT UP card, "Play now" in the Post nav bar, the NEXT tile that is a calendar) borrows a verb without the explanation. The "before" door cannot carry a game, so a foursome planned in the app arrives at the tee with nothing set. The doorbell (D86) is broken server-side: PostgREST cannot resolve the embed because `live_scores` and `game_results` both carry FKs to `live_rounds` and `live_round_players` (verified fact 3); the fix is an explicit hint at `index.html:7800`. The install banner overlapping the FAB silences the only entrance to all three doors.

**IMPACT.** A new user who ignores the banner cannot reach Post / Live / Plan at all. A league mate seated in someone else's live game gets no signal that they are on a card (CAS A7: "someone else just wrote a 103 on my record"). Wolf's rules are gated behind the precondition ("needs four") they would help a group meet.

**RECOMMENDATION.** Never overlay the FAB with the banner (position the banner above the nav, or suppress it on screens with the ⊕). Add the FK hint at `index.html:7800` and confirm the resume banner renders. Add a "game" field to the tee-sheet form ("Saturday's foursome — put skins on it"). Move the game picker to the top of live setup with an (i) per game. One verb per concept across web and iOS: *Post a score / Play live / Plan a round*.

### 1.4 T3 · Play golf → enter the result

**OBSERVATION.** The composer (`screenshots/org/62-post-round-form.jpg`, `screenshots/nov/65-post-form.jpg`): course search → tee list with rating/slope → autofill; praised by NOV, SKEP, COMP. A blank date produced "Post failed. That didn't go through — please try again." — the real cause is a null `played_on` violating NOT NULL; no field is flagged; "Start over" clears the date, so the recovery path re-creates the fault (verified fact 4). "HOW THIS ROUND SCORES · No league yet? The round still counts on your card…" (`index.html:3198`) is shown to members of a league (6/7). Live scoring (`screenshots/org/88-live-scoring.jpg`, `screenshots/nov/91-live-h1.jpg`, `screenshots/comp/56-live-hole18.jpg`): "ALL SQUARE" / "MIKE 1 · 2 RIDING", "HOLE 1 · PAR 5 · SI 15", steppers; the first tap lands on par unannounced — "I tapped + six times on a par 5 expecting 6 and got 10" (NOV N-22; ORG-38 "the first tap surprised me"); "Scrap this round" was dead for CAS (A6, `screenshots/cas/93-L03-scrap-test.jpg`) and a two-tap confirm for NOV (`screenshots/nov/93-scrap-confirm.jpg`) — unresolved, M-090; "0 STK" for two 18-index players puzzled NOV (correct under strokes-off-the-low-man, unexplained); "attested" is defined here, after tee-off, for the first time (ORG-07, NOV N-11).

**INTERPRETATION.** This is the strongest transition in the product. Vision principle 2 ("posting a round in under 60 seconds") is met when the course picker cooperates. The failures are mechanical, not conceptual: placeholder-as-value, an unguarded blank rating, duplicate course rows, a NOT NULL error narrated as "try again", an unannounced par-on-first-tap (§13.1 "card found → stepper on par" — designed, and the only hint is a sentence on the setup card that names a UI widget: "The stepper opens on each hole's par"). The `courses` edge function's 502s are the third-party path (GolfCourseAPI) failing before the cache; cached courses work.

**IMPACT.** Taps and trust, not belief: a golfer who posts "-79.0" or a 10 on a par 5 has a wrong round on an immutable card (§16) whose only correction is an unlabeled ✕ on You › Recent rounds with a native `confirm()` and no undo (ORG-35, NOV N-12, CAS A26).

**RECOMMENDATION.** Hide rating/slope until a tee is picked and block Post when they are blank; use empty inputs with a caption instead of numeric placeholders; dedupe course rows; validate the date client-side and name the field; announce "first tap = par" in the stepper itself; make Scrap deterministic; add "Fix / delete this round" to the receipt with an in-app confirm and undo; gate `index.html:3198` on `!window.CS?.league`; surface a 502 as "course lookup is down — type your own" rather than an empty list.

In-season receipts do carry points: "Points 9 · This month COUNTING #1" (`screenshots/obs/37-round-card.jpg`).

**INTERPRETATION.** §2.1 defines PvI as Playing Index − Differential with positive = beat your number; the app is faithful to the spec's sign and golfers read golf's. D1 chose "beat your number by 1.4" as the display precisely to avoid a bare signed number; `vsPhrase()` (`index.html:5709`) does that on the ceremony while `calcVs` (`:6339`), the receipt, You, standings and board chips print the raw signed number. D2 said two numbers survive on the card — *gross and points*; `finishCeremony` (`:6187–6192`) deliberately falls to "COUNTS ON YOUR CARD" when `counts` is false (round outside the season window, `:6584–6596`) — the comment says this "matches the app's own 'practice rounds hit your card, not the season' promise", but the ceremony never *says* that. Validation (TOP-4, 3/3) found PvI computed in four places under three rules (client `recalc` at 100% with no window; `home_feed` at 100%; `v_rounds_ranked` with allowance and window; `score_round`'s fallback sets `index_at_post` to the round's own differential when no index exists — CAS's "+0.0"). The receipt prints the raw index beside the allowance-applied verdict, so its arithmetic does not close (§16 asks for the allowance snapshot).

**IMPACT.** The moment of maximum attention is spent on a number whose sign reads backwards, a promise that vanishes on the next screen, and three stacked celebrations for milestones that do not exist yet. A golfer who reads minus as good either mis-celebrates or distrusts the engine — and the engine is the product's only real differentiator (SKEP: "the scoring engine… is the part the app hides").

**RECOMMENDATION.** Never print a bare signed PvI: route every site through `vsPhrase()` ("beat it by 1.7" / "1.7 over"), colour secondary. Ceremony = gross · band · points · consequence ("+6 pts · counts for Squad 2 · #3 this month" or "practice — season starts Sat Sep 5"). One PvI producer (server view) feeding preview, feed, receipt; show "Index 14.2 × 95% = 13.5" on the form and as a receipt row so the arithmetic reproduces. Collapse the three post-tap sheets to one; threshold badges only for rounds after a baseline exists; the link-revoke control only after a link has been made.
