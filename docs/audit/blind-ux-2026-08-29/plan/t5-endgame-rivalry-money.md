### D111 · The endgame is a sentence you can always see — the Final line stops competing with the week
*(2026-08-29, from the blind audit TOP-5 (M-055, M-059, M-124; hard defect A-12). UI + IA level on a decided mechanic (§14.0/§14.3, D105). PROPOSED — talk first. Sources: `#statFinal` index.html:9605–9637, `LeagueCopy.deadline` LeagueCopy.swift:224–247, hero 10041–10066, climb note 4478–4480 / StandingsMath.swift:399–407, `season_scenarios` meta, `cup_final_race`.)*
- **Current:** D4 (2026-07-15) ordered a season-long foreshadow ("Season crowns 4 Cup seeds · Cup starts fresh Aug 30") and accepted "persistent banner real estate" as the cost. What shipped is one line in the Season tile that competes with "Week closes Sun" and "Month closes" under a nearest-deadline sort, so it renders on one day in 155 (Fellas, reproduced with the code's arithmetic). The hero's season branch carries the gap and the floor, never the endgame; the standings caption speaks D24's engine vocabulary ("EVERYONE ADVANCES — 2 CONTENDERS, 2 SEATS", "PROJECTED UNDER A GENEROUS CEILING", "LOCKED"); the only plain-English sentence is the Pro-only wizard tooltip. Both clients are identical in this.
- **Problem:** 8/8 blind testers could not say how the Cup is won; the only mid-season tester scored stakes 3/10 and found the Cup Final in a bylaw row after four taps. D105 itself records "foreshadow shipped, the race itself never did" — and the foreshadow did not ship either, in effect. A leader can coast on a lead that decides only the seed and learn what it was worth on the day the slate resets: exactly the rug-pull D4 named.
- **Recommendation:**
  1. **One producer, `endgameLine()`** (web classic block; iOS `LeagueCopy.endgame(_:b:scenarios:)`), derived from `finish`, `structure`, `climbCut(meta).K`, roster n, `cupFinalStart()`/`seasonE()` and `cup_final_race.status`. Copy by structure (dates and counts real, never hardcoded weekdays — §14.0):
     - squads2 · cup_final: **"Both squads reach the Cup Final — 4 weeks from Tue Dec 22, scored fresh. The season decides who starts +10."**
     - squads3/4 · cup_final: **"Top 2 squads reach the Cup Final — 4 weeks from {date}, scored fresh. The season decides the seeds."**
     - solo · cup_final: **"Top 2 golfers meet in the Cup Final — 4 weeks from {date}, scored fresh. The season decides the seeds."**
     - points_table: **"The points table crowns it — the season ends {date}. No reset."**
     - during `cup_final`: **"The Cup Final is on — {N} days left. Only rounds since {date} count; {seed 1} started +10."** (reads `cup_final_race`).
     - n ≤ K: the D112 sentence instead.
  2. **Three homes, always.** (a) The hero's season branch renders it as the line under `gapLine` (10063–10065; iOS `HomeHero.foot` gains a second line). (b) The Season tile's sub-line is the endgame with a countdown ("Cup Final · Tue Dec 22 · 115d"); the week/month closes keep the slot only within 1 day of firing ("Week closes tonight", "Month closes tomorrow · floors assessed") — the press meter and the hero foot already carry the month. The `opts.sort` stays for those two; the Final line is pinned, not sorted. (c) The climb note (`#climbNote` / `ClimbMath.note`) becomes the structure sentence; the D24 clinch/elimination facts move to the badges and the scenario line where they belong.
  3. **Every rendering taps.** Pre-window → the ENDGAME sheet (D113's help section: dates, seeds, +10, ties, what the day after looks like). During the window → the race receipt (`renderCupRace` / `FinalistReceiptSheet`). Instrumented (`endgame_tap {src}`).
  4. **Vocabulary defined or retired.** `LOCKED` → **`IN`** (a seat in the Final is clinched; aria "clinched a seat in the Final"), `OUT` stays; "PROJECTED UNDER A GENEROUS CEILING" is D24's honesty-rule *name* and leaves the screen (D24 says it is an engine property); "HAS LOCKED THE TOP SEED · +10" → "**{name} has the top seed and the +10 — nobody can catch them before the Final**"; clinch captions render only when at least one row has points (empty squads say nothing, M-055's "TOP SEED · +10 over two empty squads").
  5. **Points King ties get the ladder.** `close_season` picks the king by `points desc` alone (20260828170100:385–386). The same §14.3 rungs (months won → best single month → fewest rounds → logged coin) decide a King tie, and the rung is stored (`seasons.king_rung`) so the ceremony can say it. One re-creation of `close_season`, byte-identical elsewhere. Until built, the TIES copy says "the Cup and the seeds"; it never promises a King ladder that does not exist.
- **Principle served:** #5 The App Should Feel Alive (anticipation is drama — D4's own line) · success metric "understand standings in ten seconds" · §16 (the sentence taps to its receipt) · D24's honesty rule kept as an engine property, not a caption.
- **Benefit:** the Final becomes an anticipated event with a date and a prize on every screen where a standing is, from week 1; the lead converts into a seed you can see coming (D4: "a playoff, not a theft"); a member can answer "how do we win" without the Pro.
- **Tradeoffs:** one more line on the hero and the climb (D4 accepted this). The Season tile loses its "week closes Sunday" beat on most days — the press meter carries the month, and the hero foot the floor deadline, so nothing is lost, only moved. Copy must be structure-aware in five variants; a producer with app-tests cases makes drift visible.
- **CONFLICT:** none upward. Executes D4 as written and D105 §3 ("foreshadow unchanged" — this *repairs* the foreshadow, it does not touch the race). Retires the D24-era caption strings as UI copy; D24's engine is untouched. Amends the C4 "nearest deadline wins" rule for `#statFinal` (a code comment, never a logged decision).

### D112 · The hollow Final — when the roster cannot fill more seats than it has contenders, say so
*(2026-08-29, blind audit TOP-5 corner case (M-124, M-055). Mechanic-adjacent: no scoring change; a warning and honest copy. PROPOSED — talk first. Sources: `season_scenarios` meta.k, `climbCut`, both audited live leagues are 2-player / 2-seat; `STRUCT_NOTES` 12002–12007; `openLockShare` seat math.)*
- **Current:** at roster n ≤ K (K = seats: 2 for squads3/4 and solo, 1 for squads2 and points_table where the race is the +10 / the crown) every contender reaches the Final by construction. The engine still runs — seeds lock, +10 applies, the window scores fresh — and the standings say "EVERYONE ADVANCES — 2 CONTENDERS, 2 SEATS". Nothing tells the Pro at lock, nothing tells a member what is actually at stake.
- **Problem:** the flagship moment is structurally trivial for the owner's own two real leagues and for every founding league that starts small, and the product describes that as a feature ("everyone advances"). A member reads it as "the season doesn't matter"; the Pro never learns that two more golfers would make it real.
- **Recommendation:** no mechanic change. (1) `endgameLine()` gets an n ≤ K variant: squads2/solo **"With 2 of you, both reach the Final — the season only decides who starts +10 (the seed)."** / K=2 **"With {n} contenders and 2 seats, everyone reaches the Final — the season decides the seeds."** (2) At lock (wizard review step and `openLockShare`) and on the Pro's League pane while `phase ∈ {setup, draft}` or at the starter: **"{n} golfers · everyone would reach the Final. Add golfers to make the seed race real — 4+ for two squads, 3+ for solo."** (3) **Open for the owner (⚑):** whether a season whose roster is still ≤ K at `ends_on − 27` should auto-fall-back to `points_table` (a mechanic change: the tick would flip `league_settings.finish` and post it) — recommended *no* for v1; the honest sentence is enough and the +10 race is still a race.
- **Principle served:** golf-honest voice (say what is true now) · §16 · #1 Golf First (a two-man league is still a real league; it just needs the truth).
- **Benefit:** the Pro learns the fix at the moment they can still act; the member stops reading "everyone advances" as "nothing matters".
- **Tradeoffs:** one more Pro-facing line at lock (theme 1 owns the lock share sheet — coordinate so the sentence lands once). If ⚑ resolves to auto-fallback, that is its own entry.
- **CONFLICT:** none. §14.3 and D105 are unchanged; D24's K is read, not redefined.

### D114 · The pot gets a payment path — a Pro-set note and due date on the bylaws, never a transfer
