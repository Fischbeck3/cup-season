### D111 · Noun sweep III — the collisions get one owner each (card · stake · tee sheet · cup · the books)
*(2026-08-29, from the blind UX audit (terminology §5 items 3, 9, runners-up; M-061, M-062, M-074). UI-copy level (5) with one IA-level (3) assignment — "tee sheet". PROPOSED — talk first. Sources: D47, D64, D86, D107, §13.2, Guide `:37–40`.)*

- **Problem:** eight of eight testers listed "card" or "stake" or "tee sheet" as a term they had to re-derive; the worst instance is the post-round verdict "COUNTS ON YOUR CARD", which every tester read as "counted" when it meant "not in the season" (§5 item 3). - **Recommendation (assignments, copy-only except one route label):**
  - **card** = the golfer card (the profile), used only on You / Card & settings / onboarding. Recap / settlement / Tour Card are named artifacts ("Share the recap", "Share the settlement", "Tour Card"). The record-of-rounds sense retires: "counts on your card" → **"posts to your rounds — every league you're in reads it"**; the post-round verdict becomes a season-state sentence (T4 owns the words: "PRACTICE · SEASON STARTS SAT SEP 5" / "+9 PTS · COUNTS FOR SQUAD 1").
  - **stake** = money on a live game only (§13.2). The Pot tab's pride bets take D64's own noun: **forfeits** — "The forfeits · pride, never money", "Post a forfeit", "Put it on the record"; the D64 sheet subtitle "Pride, on the books — never money" loses "on the books". D51's line is "what's on the line", never "stake".
  - **the books** = money, full stop (D47 restated). Pride copy never says it.
  - **tee sheet** = the shared calendar of planned rounds (D47 restated). The live scorer is **a live round** ("Play now" is its door; "Live round" its screen) — D12's one noun, three states (planned / playing / posted). D86's invitation face reads "Marcus put you on a live round" and the plan bridge "On your tee sheet today · load it into the live round"; the Guide's "The tee sheet — one round" heading becomes "Live rounds — one round, scored together".   - **cup** = the season title. "Cup points" → "points" / "season points"; "Cups & events" → "Leagues & events"; "Cup champs" → "the winning squad". "Cup Final" keeps its name and gets its sentence (T5).
- **Principle:** #2 Low Friction — the no-tutorial metric; D11/D12's law that each noun means one thing; D47, restated with teeth (see T6-11's lint).
- **Benefit:** the post-round verdict stops lying by accident; the Pot tab and the live setup stop using one word for opposite things; the Guide, the door and the app agree on what a tee sheet is.
- **Tradeoffs:** "forfeit" reads a shade more legal than "stake" — accepted, it is D64's word and unmistakably not money (D64's own legibility rule). "Live round" for the D86 banner loses D86's charm ("the tee sheet calls you to it") — copy, not mechanic. ~90 strings across two clients; no schema (`settle_forfeit`, `forfeits` already carry the noun).
- **CONFLICT (named):** D86's and D107's *titles* use "tee sheet" for the live round — amended by assignment, neither reversed. D64's title "stakes past money" stays as history; its object noun ("forfeit") wins the UI. D47 "books = money" is upheld against the shipped pride copy. Nothing upward.
