# Critical findings — blind UX / gameplay / retention audit, 2026-08-29

**Product under test:** cupseason.app, production build `34d20b6` (byte-identical `index.html` to this branch); native iPhone app in `apps/ios` (static screen survey only).
**Method in one line:** seven blind personas drove the real product on real prod accounts through a headless iPhone-viewport browser, plus a look-only iOS survey; five synthesis agents read everything against the spec; the five biggest findings were then attacked by fifteen independent validators (three lenses each: unfamiliarity-vs-interface, reproduces-and-intended, root-cause-in-hierarchy). Every one of the fifteen returned **Confirmed UX problem**; none refuted a headline. Sub-claims they trimmed are listed under each finding — read those, they change the fix.
**Numbers:** 425 raw items (424 in `raw/persona-results.json` over 12 persona runs + the joiner attempt-1 harness item) → **135 deduplicated master issues** (`issues.json`; counts in `issues-counts.json`): **P0 7 · P1 44 · P2 65 · P3 19.** Persona verdicts (1–10, each family's latest run): organizer 3 · novice 4 · mid-season observer 4 · joiner 3 · casual 4 · competitive 5 · skeptic 3 · iOS 4 — **mean 3.75, median 4**. Executive-verdict medians across all 12 result rows (the method is stated once in `blind-ux-audit.md` §1): `rulesClear` **4** · `wouldInvite` **4** · `wouldPay` **3.5**.
**How to read:** OBSERVATION is what a tester saw (quoted copy, screenshot). INTERPRETATION is our reading. IMPACT is the funnel stage it costs. ROOT CAUSE names the hierarchy level (vision → principles → IA → mechanics → UI → implementation) and the file:line the validators verified. Nothing below was softened because the spec explains it — where the spec explains it and the screen does not, that *is* the finding. Screenshot paths are written `<session>/<frame>.png` as the testers named them; the frames live in this folder as `screenshots/<session>/<frame>.jpg` (validator frames `screenshots/v-TOP-*/` stay `.png`).

The two things every persona praised — the post-a-round form with its live bands, and the live side-game scorer with its stroke line and settlement card — are exactly the two things the season layer then contradicts. That is the shape of this report.

## Part 1 — The five biggest problems

The ids **TOP-1 … TOP-5** and the titles are the same in `blind-ux-audit.md` §2, in `issues.json` (`validation` field) and in `raw/synthesis-and-validation-results.json`.

### TOP-1 — The organizer cannot finish: "Lock" reports failure after the server has already committed, and the invite never appears

**PROBLEM.** The single tap that turns an account into a league — `Lock the bylaws & form the squads` — throws a client-side `ReferenceError` *after* four server writes have committed. The Pro sees "Lock failed", retries, and the post-lock share sheet (the only surface that prints the join URL as text) never opens. There is no contact-based invite; every other share control degrades to a code in a 2.4-second toast. The buy-in the Pro never chose ($75) is what every invitee is asked to accept.

**OBSERVATION.**
- Organizer persona (`raw/agent5-organizer.md` 13:32:14 UTC): tapped the lock; "Nothing visible changed" (`screenshots/org/31-after-lock.jpg`); accessibility status "Lock failed. Something went wrong — please try again."; console `[cs] error: Lock failed. staged is not defined`. **Six attempts over ~3 minutes** across two entry points (wizard step 3; Home hero "Lock it in and invite your crew") and a full reload (`32-lock-retry-toast.jpg`, `36-lock-from-home.jpg`). After the "failed" lock, the Clubhouse tab showed *no league at all* while Home still said "Just you so far. Lock the bylaws and the invite link is yours" (`34-clubhouse-after-failed-lock.jpg`, `35-home-after-failed-lock.jpg`).
- Novice persona (`raw/agent3-league-novice.md` 13:31:21): same toast, visible ~1.5 s (`screenshots/nov/33-lock-fail-toast.jpg`); ×3 more; switched Teams to Solo and tried ×2 more — **six attempts, ~2 minutes**; reloaded at 13:33:37 and found "DESERT DOGS · SEASON LIVE — The season's on. Rounds count from today." (`34-reload-home.jpg`). The first tap had succeeded; the later Solo choice was silently discarded — the Clubhouse showed two "Empty" squads and "SQUADS LOCKED" (`36-clubhouse-full.jpg`).
- Invite: "Add golfers" accepts only name/@handle; a typed email returns "No golfers found. Invite links still work for everyone else." (`screenshots/org/42-add-golfers-email.jpg`). Every "Share the invite link" / `Code · THEPTCQ5` chip / "Share an invite link instead" produced the toast "Invite code: THEPTCQ5" and never a link (`org/38-share-invite.jpg`, `org/40-code-chip.jpg`, `nov/45-share-link.jpg`). No invitation email reached any of the four invitee accounts (three independent Gmail searches).
- "Draw squads" → "Draw failed. Something went wrong — please try again." while the console carried the server's own sentence "Not enough golfers to cover every squad — 1 in, 2 squads. Share the invite link first." (`nov/53-after-draw.jpg`).
- Money: the Standard card and "Use these defaults →" never mention money; behind "Customize" the buy-in reads `$75` (`nov/19-wizard-customize-full.jpg`); the novice needed ~7 minutes of reading every (i) to find it.

**INTERPRETATION.** From the user's chair the big orange button does nothing, six times. Both testers did what people do — hammer it, back out, reload — and one of them mutated the league further by doing so. The novice's line is the whole finding: "Journey C is technically complete but the user experience of completing it is a failure message." The share sheet that D40 promised "fires the instant you lock" is unreachable, so the organizer is left to text friends a code by hand; the Guide's claim of invite-by-email is false.

**IMPACT.** Activation, at the root of every other funnel: 0 of 2 organizers saw a success state; no joiner, round, or pot exists until this tap works. Organizer verdict 3/10: "Fix the lock bug and the invite-by-contact gap and this becomes a 7." Prod telemetry (read-only query by a validator): `lock_ok` = 1 all-time (2026-07-27, pre-D97); `lock_fail` = 11, all on 2026-08-29, all `staged is not defined`. **No client-side lock has succeeded in production since D97 shipped on 2026-08-04.** Prod holds seven setup-phase leagues, all carrying `buyin_cents = 7500` that nobody chose (orchestrator check: all seven were created 2026-07-17 → 07-21, before D97 shipped — July pilot/test leagues, not Pros stalled by this bug; `client_events` holds no lock attempt of any kind between 08-04 and this audit).

**LIKELY ROOT CAUSE.**
- *Implementation (the crash):* `index.html:15218` — `return { emails: emails.length, invited: staged.length, nextPhase };` in `lockBylaws()` (15124–15219). Commit `1fd47e1` (D97) deleted `const staged = state.wizInvitees || []` and its loop but left the return. The throw happens after the `league_settings` update with `locked_at` (15127–15149), the `seasons` insert (15189–15196), the `form_squads` RPC (15203) and the `leagues.phase` update (15211). The handler at 15494–15505 catches it, `humanError()` (4117) has no branch for a ReferenceError → "Something went wrong — please try again.", and `openLockShare()` at 15501 never runs. `tests/preflight.mjs` runs `node --check`, which cannot see a free identifier (this is the second such crash after F-007 `memName`).
- *Mechanics (why it was catastrophic):* "lock" is not a server transaction — four client writes and one RPC, no `lock_league` function. RLS `settings_write` (`is_commissioner AND locked_at IS NULL`) silently matches zero rows on a retap, while `leagues_update` has no lock guard and the client derives `nextPhase` from *local* `state.structure` — so the novice's Solo retry wrote `phase='season'` against bylaws locked at `squads2`. Prod now holds Desert Dogs as `structure=squads2 · phase=season · 2 squads with 0 players · 1 member`, a state spec §15 forbids. `apps/ios/Packages/CupSeasonKit/.../Wizard/WizardService.swift lock()` ports the same five-write sequence.
- *IA (the invite):* A-W2/D97 made invites link-only and put the one link-as-text surface on the lock's success path; `shareInvite()` (14113–14128) tries `navigator.share`, then clipboard, then `toast('Invite code: ' + code)` (14127). `#emailSlots` does not exist in the markup; `state.emails` is always `['']`; the `invites` table has no sender. `Cup-Season-Guide.md:107` still says "or invite by email".
- *Spec conflict (money):* spec §7 "default $75" and the schema (`league_settings.buyin_cents DEFAULT 7500 NOT NULL`, baseline:1082; `create_league` inserts bare defaults) vs D46 "$0 bragging rights is first-class and the default". S2-03 zeroed `state.stake` in `resetWizard()` (14094) but never re-rendered the static `<div id="stakeVal">$75</div>` (3263), and any reload before lock reloads 7500 via `applyBylaws()` (14351). The novice's "one tap of − from $75 goes straight to None" is the signature of state 0 behind a stale label.
- *Home hero (persists after the one-line fix):* `renderHomeHero`'s forming branch (10072–10116, CTA at 10102) only distinguishes `phase==='season'`, so a LOCKED-but-alone league (phase `draft`) still says "Lock it in and invite your crew" — that fed the organizer's attempts 4–6.

**RECOMMENDATION.**
1. *Today, client only:* `index.html:15218` → `invited: 0` (or drop the field). Split commit from celebration in the handler (15494–15505): after `lockBylaws()` resolves, run `loadMemberships → switchView('home') → openLockShare` in their own try; in the outer catch re-read `league_settings.locked_at` / `leagues.phase` and, if committed, render success — never toast "try again" once the phase has moved. Failure feedback goes inline under `#lockBtn`, not a toast. Pass user-written RPC messages (form_squads' "Not enough golfers…", `commissioner only`) through `humanError` untouched (M-017). Fix `Cup-Season-Guide.md:107`.
2. *This week, mechanics (decision-log entry first, rule 5):* one SECURITY DEFINER `lock_league(p_league, p_bylaws jsonb)` RPC — settings + `locked_at` + season upsert + `form_squads` + phase from the *stored* structure, in one transaction, idempotent on `locked_at`, D37 grant/revoke pair; web `lockBylaws` and Swift `WizardService.lock` both call it. Tighten `leagues_update` so phase is written only by RPCs. Encode §8 (minimum four) and §15 (no unassigned members) as state, not sentences.
3. *Invite surface:* promote `openLockShare` into an `openInviteSheet(L)` reachable from the room's "Share the invite link", the Code chip, Add golfers' footer and `#draftShare`; it always shows `cupseason.app/?join=CODE` as selectable text with **Copy link** and **Copy message** (prewritten: league, Pro, first tee, buy-in or "bragging rights", link, three things to know). `navigator.share` stays first; the bare-code toast becomes the last fallback only. Home hero: branch on `phase` — `draft` and alone → "Just you so far. Share the invite link."
4. *Money (log the §7-vs-D46 CONFLICT; recommend D46 wins):* migration `alter table league_settings alter column buyin_cents set default 0` (fixes the seven stalled prod leagues and the re-entry path); `resetWizard()` calls `renderSetup()`; lift the buy-in row out of Customize to one visible line; restate money on the lock/invite sheet.
5. *Verify:* an app-tests case create → lock → assert the share sheet rendered and `leagues.phase` matches stored structure, retap → no row changed; a preflight lint (acorn + eslint-scope) that fails on any unresolved global; alert when `lock_fail` > `lock_ok` in `client_events`.

**EXPECTED IMPACT.** Restores 100% of client-side league locks (today 0 can succeed) for exactly the cohort D96's CTA is funnelling in; organizer's own estimate 3 → 7, `setupClear` 4 → 7, `wouldInvite` 4 → 6–7; ends the "$75 nobody chose" covenant before it reaches friends; removes the invalid-league-state hazard on two clients.

**SECOND-PASS CLASSIFICATION — Confirmed UX problem (3/3).**
- *Unfamiliarity vs interface:* "The headline defect is a client ReferenceError that fires AFTER the server has committed four writes, and no familiarity with the product changes what the user sees."
- *Reproduces / intended:* "The crash is a refactor remnant, not a chosen behavior … live on cupseason.app for 25 days; the phone does not carry this bug but ports the same non-atomic lock."
- *Root cause in hierarchy:* "The crash is a one-line implementation bug; the reason it was catastrophic rather than cosmetic is a mechanics gap (lock is four client writes with no server transaction) and an IA decision (link-only invites) whose secondary surfaces were never built."
- *Refuted / trimmed sub-claims:* "the product's only invite moment" is overstated — the room hero, Members sheet, Add-golfers footer and Home hero all carry a share control; `openLockShare` is only the one that prints the URL as text. "A code in a vanishing toast" is partly a harness artifact — a real phone gets the OS share sheet first (the tester conceded `navigator.share` was undefined in headless). "No way to invite friends without accounts" is wrong: signed-out `/?join=THEPTCQ5` renders "You're invited to The Papago Grind. Enter your email and you're in." — the gap is email/SMS, not account-less joining. "$75 hidden from the lock decision" is wrong (the step-3 review shows BUY-IN; both testers changed it) — but $75 *is* the effective default via the static label and DB default. "Seven pilot Pros stalled here" is misattributed: the `index.html:10080` comment records D96's measurement of a CTA-less hero on 2026-08-04, *before* D97 introduced this bug the same evening.

### TOP-2 — Nobody can tell what they are joining until after they have committed to $50

**PROBLEM.** The cold door is a slogan; the invite landing is one line; the consent sheet — reached only after email, code, golfer card and orientation — shows four bylaw rows and a "Join — I'm in for $50" button with no roster, no organizer, no dates, no scoring and no payment path. "Not now" destroys the invite. On the phone, eleven screens never define a point or say how a season is won.

**OBSERVATION.**
- Cold door (`screenshots/skep/02-cold-door.jpg`, `join/01-A-cold-door.jpg`): wordmark, "Rally your crew. Post real rounds. Take the cup.", `Continue with email`, `I have an invite code`, Terms. Nothing below the fold on a phone.
- Invite landing (`join/02-B-join-link-landing.jpg`): "You're invited to The Papago Grind. Enter your email and you're in." Actual path: email → 8-digit code → golfer card → D82 orientation → consent sheet. Joiner: **5 min 42 s, 11 taps + 4 text fields + one trip to email** (~4 min without a deliberate detour).
- Consent sheet (`join/11-J-after-take-me-in.jpg`): "Before you join The Papago Grind · THE FINE PRINT, UP FRONT · BUY-IN $50 / player · on the pot sheet · PRESET Standard · PARTICIPATION FLOOR 2 rounds / mo · FINISH Cup Final · final 4 weeks · Joining puts you on the pot sheet for $50. Cup Season keeps the tab; money moves between you. [Join — I'm in for $50] [Not now]". Tapping "Standard" does nothing (skeptic SK-03).
- "Not now" (`join/12-K-not-now.jpg`, `13-L-join-a-league.jpg`): Home reads "LEAGUE · None yet · JOIN OR START"; the Join sheet's code box is empty; the joiner re-typed THEPTCQ5 (3 taps + typing). The casual persona hit the same wall: "Had to retype the code from the text message" (3 attempts).
- iOS (`screenshots/ios/01-door.jpg`): "WHO'S THE BITCH? · WEEK 4 OF 14 · 2nd · — held · 10 points back of the lead. · Partial month · floors waived" — no tap route to any explanation of a point; the leader is unnamed and "2nd" of an unstated two reads as a podium when it is last.
- Skeptic (`raw/agent4-skeptic.md:80`): "The first thing the product tells me after sign-up is that I owe $50. Casey's text did not mention money. The door did not mention money … this is the single most likely bail point for my persona — 'wait, fifty bucks for what?' — and the app cannot answer."

**INTERPRETATION.** The teaching in this product is good and all of it happens *after* the commitment: the post-join "THREE THINGS TO KNOW" sheet, `How scoring works`, the bylaws disclosure. Every fact a joiner lacks at the $50 decision already exists in the database and is deliberately not fetched for the screen where the decision is made. The invite was built as plumbing (`?join=` consumed to `localStorage`, URL rewritten to `/`, only the league *name* resolved) rather than as the artifact the growth model says it must be.

**IMPACT.** Activation, and a monetization-trust cost at the point of highest bail risk. Joiner personas' `conceptClear`: 5 / 6 / 4 / 5 (three of four ≤ 5); `wouldPay`: 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 (all ≤ 5). Joiner's own question — "Can you understand what you are joining before accepting?" — **3/10**. Growth is foursome-by-foursome with no paid acquisition (decision-log:722), so the invite must sell itself; today it depends entirely on the friend's nagging.

**LIKELY ROOT CAUSE.** IA + onboarding sequencing, with a data-layer sub-cause and a copy sub-cause.
- `covenantGate()` (`index.html:15422–15440`) renders exactly what `join_covenant_info` returns, and that anon RPC (`supabase/migrations/20260722211500_covenant_pulse_pairings.sql:29–44`) selects only `name, buyin_cents, preset, floor, finish, structure` from `leagues` + `league_settings` — no `seasons` row (dates), no `league_members` (count, Pro), no bands, no split. `structure` is returned and unused. iOS `CovenantSheet` (`JoinLeagueFlow.swift:131–138`) mirrors it field-for-field. The covenant was scoped to setup-QA S3-01 ("money never surprises anybody"), not to the joiner's decision; D50 explicitly parked the covenant's explanatory paragraph "for a future UX pass"; `spec/gtm-year1.md:97–99` says "make the invite carry a preview of the league (name, crew, pot)" and was never built.
- Pre-auth, `league_by_code` (`20260714040000_join_polish.sql:14–17`) returns only the name; the landing copy at `index.html:17914`/`17832` was written for the pre-covenant instant-join flow.
- `resumeAfterProfile` removes `cs_code`/`cs_code_name` (17565) *before* `covenantGate` runs (17568) as an anti-loop guard, so a decline is indistinguishable from a failed join; the only trace is the toast at 17572.
- **New, found while tracing:** `boot()`'s signed-in join path (17506–17512) calls `join_league` with **no `covenantGate` at all** — a returning user who taps a `/?join=` link is put on the pot sheet with zero disclosure. That is a direct S3-01 violation.
- The door lost its explanatory layer when D83 retired the demo; the desktop "wings" are `display:none` below 1100px (`index.html:1818–1819`), so the phone door is slogan-only. The one-breath definition ("Season-long golf with the people you already play with — points, pot, pressure.") ships only in the meta description (line 22).
- iOS `HomeView.swift:553–591`: the rank card's line/foot are plain strings with no `NavigationLink`/`Button`; scoring help lives only on `PostRoundScreen.swift:320`, You › How it works, and `BylawsCard`.

**RECOMMENDATION.**
1. New migration extending `join_covenant_info` (same signature, anon+authenticated grant, revoke from public; every new key nullable for skew): `pro_name`, `pro_marker`, `member_count`, first ~6–8 members (display_name + marker only, never handles/emails; honour `discoverable='nobody'`), `starts_on`, `ends_on`, `weeks`, season status, `counting_cap`, `allowance_pct`, `pot_split`, owed pot (stake × roster, D106's number). Fail-closed on unknown code, same law as D57. Log it as a decision-log entry (IA level, extends S3-01 + D57) before building.
2. Render a league card *above the email box* on the invite door (`safeBoot` pending branch, 17910–17918): "Casey (THE PRO) invited you · The Papago Grind · 5 in · Sat Sep 5 → Sat Jan 2 · 17 wks · 2 squads · $50 buy-in → $250 pot, 60/25/15 · every round you post, anywhere, scores against your own number · Cup Final: last 4 weeks, scored fresh · [How scoring works →]" (`openScoringHelp` is classic-side and callable signed out). Landing line becomes "Sign in to join — you'll confirm the $50 before anything's final." Enrich `shareInvite`'s text (14118) with stake + dates so the group-text preview sells.
3. Turn `covenantGate` (and iOS `CovenantSheet`) into the decision sheet: WHO · WHEN · HOW (bands, cap) · FLOOR with its consequence · FINISH · STAKE ("you pay Casey directly; Cup Season keeps the tab, never the cash") · PRESET tappable → bylaws · D50's "The Pro rules…" paragraph · `How scoring works →`. Keep the two buttons.
4. For `viaInvite`, skip the D82 orientation (the code's own comment says the covenant is the invitee's teaching): door(card) → email/OTP → golfer card → covenant → welcome. One fewer screen.
5. Persist the decline: remove `cs_code` only on a join *attempt*; on "Not now" keep it (or move to `cs_code_declined`) and replace the "None yet / JOIN OR START" tile (9874) with "Invited · The Papago Grind · REVIEW", pre-filling `#jCode`/`#joinCode`. Mirror on iOS.
6. Make `boot()`'s signed-in join path (17506) call `covenantGate` like the other two (15459, 15575).
7. Cold door: one sentence under the h1 (2635), lifted from `GUIDE.games`, plus the money posture ("Free to play. Leagues set their own buy-in; the app keeps the tab, never the cash."). Frame this as a one-line D83 amendment for the owner, not a defect.
8. iOS: the rank card's line becomes a `Button` → scoring sheet (parity with `openScoringHelp`; `router.open(.scoringHelp)` already exists in `BylawsCard.swift:46`); name the field ("10 back of Galen · 2 of 2"); let "held" / "floors waived" carry a footnote.

**EXPECTED IMPACT.** The invite stops being a code relayed by text and becomes a self-explaining offer; joiner `conceptClear`/`stakesMeaningful` move at the decision point; the skeptic's named bail point gets its answer on the same sheet as the button; the "Not now" cohort is recovered instead of lost; the pot's purpose and payment path are stated pre-commit, which is the precondition for D106's collection to work.

**SECOND-PASS CLASSIFICATION — Confirmed UX problem (3/3).**
- *Unfamiliarity vs interface:* "Every fact the joiner lacks at the $50 decision point already exists in the database and is deliberately not delivered to the screen where the decision is made … an interface that explains the offer only after acceptance is failing at the moment the explanation is needed."
- *Reproduces / intended:* "Reproduces exactly and is NOT the intended design … the product's own GTM doc says the opposite of what shipped."
- *Root cause:* "Level: IA + onboarding sequencing … it is NOT a hard-to-explain mechanic — the app already has a two-sentence explanation and a bands table; they simply all sit AFTER the commit."
- *Refuted / trimmed sub-claims:* `v23 · __CS_VERSION__` on the door is the expected **local unstamped build** (Netlify stamps it; prod reads `v23 · 34d20b6`) — strike the skeptic's "unfinished software" point. The slogan-only cold door is an **owner decision** (D83 "the door sells with its own splash") — reframe M-143 as a D83 amendment. The "Not now" toast *does* fire (2.4 s; the screenshot missed it). No persona was blocked — all four joiners joined in ~4 min and could explain the game by session end, so post-commit teaching works; the failure is placement, not content.

### TOP-3 — Members are handed the organizer's controls, and no next step

**PROBLEM.** Every player's Home hero, for the entire pre-first-tee window, is the Pro's "Lock it in and invite your crew". It opens the create-league wizard on the friend's bylaws with a live "Lock the bylaws & form the squads" button and a Cancel that offers to discard the league. The league's status is stated five contradictory ways across surfaces. Nothing tells a member what to do next.

**OBSERVATION.**
- Player Home (`screenshots/cas/19-C02b-home-full.jpg`, `join/20-P-home-member.jpg`): "THE PAPAGO GRIND · FORMING · 7 days to first tee · 5 golfers in · The bylaws lock at the tee." and one full-width orange CTA `Lock it in and invite your crew`. Above it, three equal pills `Start a league · Start an event · Join a league`.
- Tap it (`join/39-U-lock-it-in.jpg`, `skep/50-lock-page.jpg`): "CREATE YOUR LEAGUE · LOCKS AT FIRST TEE · REVIEW THE BYLAWS, THEN LOCK IT IN" with the friend's bylaws and a live `Lock the bylaws & form the squads`. "← Back" walks *deeper* ("Competitiveness — pick once, argue never" → "League name · Pro — that's you · Marcus @marcus · you run this league · THE PRO", `join/56-AI-you-escape.jpg`). "Cancel" → native confirm "Cancel this league? It hasn't started, so this discards it completely." → toast "Could not discard. Something went wrong — please try again." Console 15:43:24: `commissioner only`. Escape cost: Back ×2 + Cancel + a failed discard.
- Four player personas (casual, competitive, skeptic, joiner) logged the hero independently; two declined to press the lock for fear of mutating the shared league ("a real member would hesitate, and it might have locked the league for the other testers").
- Status for the same league at the same moment: hero `FORMING`; room header `Squad formation`; Standings "SQUADS ARE FORMING · The Pro has the list · 5 PLAYERS IN THE POOL"; League tab "Squads · LIVE NOW — CAPTAINS READY"; Board "◆ The Papago Grind is live — post the first round"; Clubhouse banner "SQUADS LOCKED"; the Form-squads screen shows both squads "Empty" (`org/35`, `org/37`, `org/45`, `org/58`; `comp/21`, `comp/22`, `comp/24`). "Captains" otherwise exist only in the copy of the unbuilt captains-pick draft (`index.html:2948–2958`, demo data at `:3780`) and the always-empty "CAPT. —" standings column — no blind-draw league has one.
- "See the squads" opens the Pro's formation screen with developer copy ("THE HAT SHUFFLES SERVER-SIDE"), inert chips and no back control (`join/33-S-see-squads.jpg`).

**INTERPRETATION.** The biggest button on every member's Home is one they are afraid to touch, and it nearly deleted the friend's league — only the server said no. "What do I do now?" — the question the vision's "App Should Feel Alive" principle exists to answer — is answered with an admin tool for the 4–11 people per league who are not the Pro. D40 was written to prevent precisely this: "a member must never see the Pro's configuration tool."

**IMPACT.** Engagement: no member gets a concrete first action on the day they join, which is the precondition for the first-round loop working at all; a near-destructive path is one server refusal away; the status contradiction was logged by all six web personas and erodes trust in every other number.

**LIKELY ROOT CAUSE.** IA/mechanics: the client has no single "league stage × viewer role" state, so every surface hand-derives its own label and verb.
- `renderHomeHero` forming branch (`index.html:10072–10116`) fires for any league not in `season`, distinguishes only `starter = state.phase==='season'` (10076) and builds `nextStep = { label:'Lock it in and invite your crew', go:toWiz(2,null) }` (10102) from name + member count — **no role check, no setup/draft distinction**. D40's backstop lives only in `enterLeague` (14494–14501); D96 added this second door a month later reasoning only about solo Pros.
- `switchView` (4153–4200) has no role gate on `'wizard'`; line 4163 even redirects `draft` → `wizard` in setup for anyone.

### 2.1 `staged is not defined` — the lock throws after the server has committed (P0)
- **Repro:** Sign in → Start a league → name it → Next → Next → `Lock the bylaws & form the squads`. Toast "Lock failed. Something went wrong — please try again." Reload: the league is locked / live.
- **Evidence:** `raw/agent5-organizer.md` 13:32:14 (6 attempts, 2 entry points, reload); `raw/agent3-league-novice.md` 13:31:21–13:34:06; `screenshots/nov/33-lock-fail-toast.jpg`, `34-reload-home.jpg`; `screenshots/org/31-after-lock.jpg`, `35-home-after-failed-lock.jpg`; console `[cs] error: Lock failed. staged is not defined` ×12; prod `client_events`: `lock_fail` 11 (all 2026-08-29), `lock_ok` 0 since 2026-08-04.
- **Cause:** `index.html:15218` `return { emails: emails.length, invited: staged.length, nextPhase };` — `staged` was removed by D97 (`1fd47e1`); the other `staged` identifiers in the file are unrelated locals (a block-scoped Set at 13656, the Ryder roster code from 16202) plus the stale copy "1 golfer staged" at 12024. Writes at 15127–15149 (settings + `locked_at`), 15189–15196 (`seasons`), 15203 (`form_squads`) and 15211 (`leagues.phase`) all commit first; handler 15494–15505 catches → `humanError(err,'Lock failed.')`; `openLockShare` (15501) unreachable. Retaps re-run non-idempotent writes (Desert Dogs: `squads2 / season / 2 empty squads / 1 member`).
- **Fix:** `invited: 0` today (client push only); split commit from success UI and re-read league state in the catch; then a `lock_league` SECURITY DEFINER RPC (decision-log entry first); regression test + a no-undef lint in preflight.

### 2.2 Blank date → "Post failed. That didn't go through — please try again." (P0 for the tester who hit it)
- **Repro:** Post a round → fill scores → `Start over — clear this card` → fill scores again (date now `mm/dd/yyyy`) → `Post round`. Toast "Post failed. That didn't go through — please try again." No field is flagged; the retry fails identically.
- **Evidence:** skeptic SK-01 (`persona-results.json`, run 1; `screenshots/skep/…`), console `null value in column "played_on" violates not-null constraint` plus 4×502, 1×400. Recovered only by reading the console.
- **Cause:** `index.html:6846` "Start over" clears `inDate` along with the other fields (`e.value=''`); today's date is set only at boot (12973); the payload sends `played_on: $('#inDate').value || null` (6501); the server NOT NULL fires; `humanError`'s `/violates|constraint|not-null|null value/` branch (4116) yields the generic sentence under the `'Post failed.'` prefix (6640).
- **Fix:** never clear the date on Start over (re-default to today); disable Post or show an inline "Pick the date you played" when the date is empty; map constraint errors to the field that caused them.

### 2.3 `[live-resume] server query failed: Could not embed because more than one relationship was found for 'live_rounds' and 'live_round_players'` (P1)
- **Repro:** Any Home load on an account whose league has ever had a live round (observed on every boot by all seven web personas, including the observer's read-only account and a signed-out cold load). Start a live round, leave it, return to Home: the promised "Continue your round" banner never appears; the round is resumable only via ⊕ → Play now.
- **Evidence:** `raw/agent1-casual.md:49,171,354` (`screenshots/cas/73-I06-group-phones.jpg` — "a Continue your round banner is waiting on Home"; `92-L02-final-home.jpg` — no banner); `agent4-skeptic.md:32`; `agent3:422`; `agent7:60`; `agent5:364`; `agent6` J-32 (15:27:49).
- **Cause:** `index.html:7800` embeds `live_round_players(...)` inside the `live_rounds` select. `live_scores` and `game_results` each carry FKs to *both* `live_rounds` and `live_round_players`, so PostgREST sees a junction path besides the direct FK and refuses the ambiguous embed. All three skew-retry rungs (7805–7807) fail the same way; the catch at 7812–7815 warns and returns, so the server-side resume (and the Home banner it feeds) never runs.
- **Fix:** disambiguate the embed: `live_round_players!live_round_players_live_round_id_fkey(...)` (name the FK in the hint); add a check to preflight that the resume query returns without error against the linked DB; then verify the banner renders.

### 2.4 Course search: five 502s from the `courses` Edge Function, swallowed silently (P1)
- **Repro:** Post a round → type "TPC Scottsdale" or "Papago". No suggestions, no spinner, no error. "Ken Mc" returns a cached course with 7 tees. In another session "Papago" returned two identical rows, and the first tap logged a 502 with no tees; a second tap listed 13.
- **Evidence:** competitive C2-05 (console 502 ×5 at 14:29 UTC; `screenshots/comp/34-course-search3.jpg`, `36-course-papago.jpg`, `37-course-ken.jpg`); joiner J-22 (15:38:01; `join/43-X-course-search.jpg`, `44-Y-tees.jpg`); skeptic SK-18 (duplicate rows, then no results). 502s also logged on Post round (×3), Tee off (×3), Finish round (×2), On the tee sheet (×1) — the failing endpoint for those is unidentified.
- **Cause:** `index.html:6957` `sb.functions.invoke('courses', {action:'search'})`; on error the catch (6964–6968) hides the dropdown when the local cache is empty — by design ("cached results stand") but with no message. The tee fetch after a pick (6909, `action:'cache'`) is `.catch(()=>{})`. Why the function returned 502 (upstream GolfCourseAPI timeout, cold start, key/quota) is not established by this audit.
- **Fix:** inline "Course search is down — type your course, rating and slope" with a retry when the remote call fails and the cache is empty; dedupe results by course id; read the `courses` function logs for the 14:29 and 15:38 UTC windows and add a timeout + cached-fallback inside the function; put the other 502s (post / tee-off / finish / tee sheet) under a single telemetry event so the endpoint is named next time.

### 2.5 'Share' / 'Copy' failures: "Could not make the link. Please sign in again." while signed in (P1)
- **Repro (desktop / any context without clipboard permission):** League tab → Share the season → Link; or the "Welcome to the season" sheet → `Share a link — no account needed`; or a guest's `Copy link` on the live setup. Results: "Could not make the link. Please sign in again." (twice, 18 minutes into a signed-in session) / "Copy failed — try again" / "Invite code: THEPTCQ5" (every invite share control, ×4 for the organizer).
- **Evidence:** `raw/agent5-organizer.md:212,248,336` (`screenshots/org/60-season-share-tap.jpg`); skeptic SK-11 (`skep/56-share-link.jpg`, 14:35 UTC); console `Failed to execute 'writeText' on 'Clipboard': Write permission denied`; `agent3:237`; M-003 evidence list.
- **Cause:** `index.html:6052` (season/round share) and 6836 (claim links) wrap the RPC *and* `navigator.clipboard.writeText` in one `catch(e){ toast(humanError(e,'Could not make the link.')) }`; the clipboard error text contains "permission denied", which `humanError`'s auth regex (4112: `/…|permission denied|row-level|…/`) maps to "Please sign in again." — a wrong diagnosis that sends users to sign out. `shareInvite` (14113–14128) has no link-as-text fallback at all: `navigator.share` → clipboard → `toast('Invite code: ' + code)` (14127). `openLockShare`, the only surface that prints the URL, is unreachable (2.1). - **Fix:** separate link creation from copying — once the token/URL exists, always render it as selectable text with a Copy button, and treat a clipboard failure as "copy failed, here's the link", never as an auth error; exclude clipboard errors from the auth branch of `humanError`.

### 2.9 iOS: season length 14 on Home vs 13 in the Clubhouse (P1)
- **Repro:** Sign in to a league running Mon Aug 3 → Mon Nov 2. Home: "WEEK 4 OF 14". Clubhouse: "WK 4 / 13", "13 wks", tile "W4 / 13".
- **Evidence:** `raw/ios-screen-survey.md:35,55,60,237` (`screenshots/ios/01-door.jpg` vs `03-clubhouse.jpg`).
- **Cause:** two formulas. `SeasonPhase.of` (`Packages/CupSeasonKit/Sources/CupSeasonKit/Models.swift:184`) computes `total = ((days + 1) + 6) / 7` — an inclusive day count, so a 91-day season becomes 14. `LeagueDates.totalWeeks` (`League/LeagueDates.swift:31–34`) computes `ceil(days / 7)` = 13. Spec §14.0 seasons run N whole weeks; 13 is right, Home is wrong.
- **Fix:** `SeasonPhase.of` calls `LeagueDates.totalWeeks` / `currentWeek`; one source for both screens; a unit test on a 91-day season.

### 2.10 iOS: the calendar drops Aug 1–5 (P2)
- **Repro:** Schedule → August 2026. The first row is empty; the second row begins 6 · 7 · 8 under T · F · S.
- **Evidence:** `raw/ios-screen-survey.md:93,98,245` (`screenshots/ios/05-schedule.jpg`).
- **Cause:** `apps/ios/CupSeason/Schedule/ScheduleScreen.swift:123–124` — `ForEach(0..<vm.month.leadingBlanks, id: \.self)` for the blanks and `ForEach(1...vm.month.daysInMonth, id: \.self)` for the days share the same `Int` id space inside one `LazyVGrid`; for a month starting on Saturday (`leadingBlanks` = 6) ids 1–5 collide, and SwiftUI renders the day cells 1–5 as the blank views. That yields 11 blanks then day 6 on Thursday — exactly the screenshot. Reproduces whenever `leadingBlanks ≥ 2` (Sep 2026 will lose day 1).
- **Fix:** give blank cells a distinct id (`"blank-\(i)"` or negative ints); dim past days rather than omitting any.

## Part 4 — The zero-instruction test

> If the developer disappeared and a new user downloaded Cup Season tomorrow, could that user run a season with five friends without asking another human how it works?

**Answer: NO.** The exact reasons, from evidence only (this block is identical in `blind-ux-audit.md` §14, `critical-findings.md` Part 4 and `README.md`):

1. **The organizer cannot complete setup and know it.** Both organizers saw "Lock failed" on every tap (`staged is not defined`, `index.html:15218`); one learned the league was live only by reloading; the post-lock share sheet never opened. (M-001)
2. **The five friends cannot be invited from the app.** No email/SMS invite exists; "Add golfers" finds only existing accounts; the join URL is never displayed — only a code in a vanishing toast. The organizer must text "download Cup Season, tap 'I have an invite code', type THEPTCQ5" — which is asking another human. (M-002, M-003)
3. **A friend who opens the link cannot tell what they are joining** before "Join — I'm in for $50" (no roster, Pro, dates, scoring or payment path) — so they text the organizer to ask. (M-023, M-024, M-025)
4. **Once in, every friend's Home hands them the organizer's lock button** and no member-facing next step. (M-030, M-033)
5. **Their first round promises 5/6/12 league points and delivers zero with no explanation**, described with three different signs; nobody could say what they earned. (M-040, M-045, M-044)
6. **Nobody can answer "how do we win."** The Cup Final, seeds, ties and the floor are undefined or contradictory in-product; the rules sit four taps deep behind a collapsed panel. Every persona said, in those words, that the organizer would explain it in the parking lot. (M-055, M-056, M-057, M-054)
7. **Money is a ledger nobody knows how to settle**, with a $75 default nobody chose. (M-110, M-004)

**What WOULD pass:** an already-formed, already-running league where members only post rounds and play live side games. The post form, course → tee → rating/slope autofill, the live bands, the receipt's differential arithmetic, the live scorer's stroke line ("Marco gets 4: holes 3, 6, 16, 18"), the running skins ledger, guest play without an account, and the settlement card were praised by every persona (side-game verdicts 7–8/10). The season wrapper around them — setup, invite, consent, status, endgame, money — is what fails the test.

## Appendix — method limits and test footprint

**State honestly what this audit could not see.**
- The headless browser has no native share sheet and denied clipboard reads/writes: every "Share" / "Copy" outcome was judged on visible feedback only. A real phone usually gets the OS share sheet first; the desk (where the wizard is meant to be used) is where those degrade.
- No in-season play could be observed — both test leagues defaulted the first tee to Sat Sep 5, a week out. Counting rounds, month closes, week snapshots, the floor/bye firing, squad standings and the Cup Final were judged from copy, code and the owner's real leagues, not from play.
- No finished season was available; Journeys F/G (finale, wrap) are partly inferred from what the live app says about endings and from the engine.
- The owner's two real leagues have two players each, which flattens "biggest threat" and makes "EVERYONE ADVANCES — 2 CONTENDERS, 2 SEATS" trivially true.
- The App Review sandbox was unavailable; the iOS survey saw static landing screens via a developer launch hatch (no taps, no scroll, nothing below the fold); the Live screen's missing chrome may be the hatch bypassing a presenting container (2.11).
- Run-2 personas (casual, competitive, skeptic, iOS) landed on already-used accounts; they signed out, redid the cold paths, and flagged the contamination in their blockers. The first joiner attempt's "code never arrived" P0 was a test-harness artifact (Supabase recorded the send; the mail connector hid messages past the fifth in a thread) — only its door/Terms observations are used here.
- `v23 · __CS_VERSION__` on the door is the expected unstamped local build; prod reads `v23 · 34d20b6`.
- Console 502s on Post round, Tee off, Finish round and On the tee sheet were logged by three personas; the failing endpoint for those was not identified by this audit.
- The read-only observer on the owner's real account made no writes (DB check: 0 rounds, 0 posts).
