# Terminology audit — Cup Season blind UX pass, 2026-08-29

**Scope.** Every product-specific term the eight blind testers met (seven web personas + the iOS screen survey), cross-checked against what the product actually means by it (spec v1.0, product vision, decision log, the Guide, `index.html` copy, the iOS sources, and the scoring SQL). Observation, interpretation and intent are kept apart: *what users thought* is quoted from the raw reports; *actual meaning* is cited to a spec section, a decision, or a line of code; *confusing?* is the audit's judgement.

**Sources.** Raw reports in `docs/audit/blind-ux-2026-08-29/raw/` — `agent1-casual.md` (A1), `agent2-competitive.md` (A2), `agent3-league-novice.md` (A3), `agent4-skeptic.md` (A4), `agent5-organizer.md` (A5), `agent6-new-joiner.md` (A6), `agent7-retention-observer.md` (A7), `ios-screen-survey.md` (iOS). Screenshots are cited as `shots/<session>/<file>`; the root is `screenshots/` (sessions: org=A5, nov=A3, obs=A7, join=A6, cas=A1, comp=A2, skep=A4, ios). Product sources: `spec/spec-v1.0.md` (§), `spec/decision-log.md` (D-numbers), `spec/gameplay-modes-working.md`, `Cup-Season-Guide.md`, `index.html` (line numbers), `apps/ios/**`, `supabase/migrations/`.

**The rule applied throughout:** if the spec explains a term and the UI did not, that is the finding. A tester who eventually reconstructed the meaning from a collapsed panel four taps deep still counts as confused at the point of use.

---

## 0. The headline

Across 8 testers, **rulesClear averaged 4.0/10 and conceptClear 5.1/10**, and six of the eight 30-second explanations end on an admission of not knowing — most often how the winner is decided (A2, A7, iOS) or whether the round they just posted counted (A1, A3); A6 ends on not knowing how to pay. The product's own name is the least-defined word in it. The vocabulary problem is not that the words are bad (most are charming) — it is that **the app introduces ~120 product-specific terms and defines almost none of them where they are first met**, uses several of them in two or three senses, shows the user a handful of terms its own decision log had already retired, and in three places prints internal engineering language on screen.

Seven systemic pathologies, each documented in the table:

1. **Undefined at point of use.** The definitions exist (the scoring sheet, the five How-it-works cards, the wizard (i)s) but sit behind a collapsed disclosure, an optional Customize panel, or the bottom of the You tab. Cup, Cup Final, seed, floor, squad, the Pro, attested, allowance, tee sheet, bylaws — all met before defined.
2. **Overloaded nouns.** "Card" (five senses), "cup" (four), "stake" (two, opposite: money vs never-money), "tee sheet" (calendar vs live scorer), "lock" (three moments), "squad" (team / phase / penalty unit), "the books" (money / pride bets).
3. **Three label sets for one concept.** The point bands wear different names on the scoring sheet, the post form, the preview message and the receipt; the verification tier is "GHIN rounds" on one screen and "Attested" on the next; the invite code has four labels.
4. **Golf-borrowed words used against their golf meaning.** "The Pro" (a club professional), a negative "vs index" (minus is good in golf), "short game", "receipts required", "tee sheet", "No. 2".
5. **Spec-retired or spec-forbidden terms still on screen.** The allowance % (D2/D8/D48 said never show), "attested" (D13 said Vouch only), "Pro-approved bye" (D14 made it automatic), "event" as a label (D12 said schema-only), "counting cap" (D51 said never say it), "invite code / league code" both (D47 said one noun), unqualified "card" (D47).
6. **Internal language leaking.** "PROJECTED UNDER A GENEROUS CEILING" is D24's honesty-rule phrasing printed on the standings; "the stepper", "the pilot", "mints a trophy", "bylaws §4" (a citation to a repo document that is never served), "staged", "THE HAT SHUFFLES SERVER-SIDE".
7. **The number the user sees is not the number that scores.** The post preview and any pre-season/league-less receipt compute "vs your index" at 100% (`index.html:6315`, `:11577`; `round_card` falls back to `index_at_post − differential`), while league scoring applies the preset allowance (`v_rounds_ranked`: `index_at_post × handicap_allowance / 100 − differential`, baseline `:1359–1363`). The bylaws advertise "95%" and no surface shows it acting. This is the root of "I cannot reproduce the number by hand" (A5) and "the 95% never appears in a calculation" (A2, A3, A4, A6, A7).

## 5. The ten terms that most damage comprehension — ranked

Ranking weighs (a) how many testers hit it, (b) whether it sits on the core loop (post → points → standings → win), and (c) whether the misreading is *confident* (a wrong mental model is worse than a blank).

1. **Cup / Cup Final / "scored fresh" / seed / LOCKED / advance / seats — the endgame cluster.** 8 of 8 could not say how a season is won; the product's name is undefined; the flagship mechanic is one bylaw row behind a collapsed disclosure and a wizard (i). D4 named this on 2026-07-15 and D105 (2026-08-28) says the race surface still doesn't exist. Every "why would I start another season" answer (A7: 4/10) traces here.
2. **"vs your index" as a bare signed number.** 7 of 7 web testers read the sign backwards or watched one round get three verdicts (−9.8 / 9.8 over / +0.0). D1 already ruled the phrase form; the form, receipt and tables ignore it. This is the verdict on every posted round.

4. **"The Pro" (and "Pro Shop").** Every persona paused on it; three said "a club professional." D15 chose the collision; the testers paid for it. "Commissioner" costs nothing.
5. **"Handicap allowance 95%."** Advertised on two screens, applied on none the user can see, and the preview/receipt math runs at 100% while the league scores at 95%. D2/D8/D48 said hide it; it is shown *and* unexplained — the worst of both.
6. **Squad / "sqd pts" / captain / four formation states.** A team noun met before it is defined (Home's floor sentence shows it to users with no league), stretched to a phase and a penalty unit, printed ×4 in solo leagues, with a captain column that references nothing.
