# Cup Season — Blind UX / Gameplay / Retention Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Build audited:** `34d20b6` (production `cupseason.app` serves this exact `index.html`; every client defect below is live) · **iOS:** `apps/ios` at the same commit, static-screen survey only.
**Audience:** the product owner. **Voice:** plain, ruthless, evidence-only. Every finding keeps OBSERVATION / INTERPRETATION / IMPACT / RECOMMENDATION apart.

## 0. Method & scope

### Read this first (six lines)

1. **Seven blind personas drove the real product on real prod accounts** (headless iPhone-viewport browser), plus a look-only survey of the native iPhone app; nobody was told the rules. Synthesis agents then read everything with the spec in hand; the top five findings were each attacked by three independent validators (15 verdicts). **All five survived as Confirmed UX problems**, with specific sub-claims refuted (§12).
2. **The organizer cannot finish.** "Lock the bylaws & form the squads" throws a client `ReferenceError` (`staged is not defined`, `index.html:15218`) *after* the server has already locked the league. Both organizers saw "Lock failed" six times; the invite sheet — the only surface that ever prints the join URL — never opens. Prod telemetry: `lock_ok` = 1 (2026-07-27, pre-bug), `lock_fail` = 11 (all 2026-08-29). Live since 2026-08-04.
3. **The first round contradicts itself.** Six of six posting testers were promised "LEAGUE POINTS THIS ROUND 5/6/12" a week before first tee and got 0 in standings; the same round was described with three different signs; nothing says "the season starts Sep 5".
4. **Nobody can say how the Cup is won** (8/8). The Cup Final — the product's name — is one bylaw row behind a collapsed disclosure four taps deep. The money that rides on it has no payment path.
5. **Every member's Home leads with the organizer's lock button**, which opens the Pro's wizard with a live lock and a "discard this league" cancel (server refused; the client did not).
6. **The parts everyone praised** — the post form with live bands, the receipt arithmetic, the live scorer with its stroke line and settlement card — are the same parts the season wrapper then contradicts. Zero-instruction test: **NO** (§14). Median `wouldPay`: **3.5/10**.

### TOP-1 — The organizer cannot finish: "Lock" reports failure after the server has already committed, and the invite never appears

**Stage:** activation. **Master issues:** M-001, M-002, M-003, M-004, M-017 (+ M-006, M-007, M-008, M-011, M-018, M-022).

- **OBSERVATION.** Both organizers tapped "Lock the bylaws & form the squads" six times (A5 13:32–13:36, A3 13:31–13:33). Each tap: toast "Lock failed. Something went wrong — please try again." (`nov/33-lock-fail-toast.jpg`) or nothing visible (`org/31-after-lock.jpg`); console `[cs] error: Lock failed. staged is not defined` ×12. The novice reloaded and found "DESERT DOGS · SEASON LIVE — The season's on. Rounds count from today." (`nov/34-reload-home.jpg`) — the first tap had succeeded; her later switch to Solo was applied to settings while attempt one's two squads stood. "Add golfers" with a friend's email → "No golfers found. Invite links still work for everyone else." (`org/42-add-golfers-email.jpg`). Every "Share the invite link" control → a 1.5-s toast "Invite code: THEPTCQ5", never a URL (`org/38-share-invite.jpg`, `nov/45-share-link.jpg`, and identically for all five sharing testers). "Draw squads" → "Draw failed. Something went wrong — please try again." while the console carried the server's own sentence "Not enough golfers to cover every squad — 1 in, 2 squads. Share the invite link first." (`nov/53-after-draw.jpg`). Buy-in read "$75" behind "Customize" (`nov/19-wizard-customize-full.jpg`).
- **INTERPRETATION.** The single most consequential tap in the product lies to the person who pressed it, and the one sheet that hands the Pro a shareable link sits behind the lie. Everything downstream — joiners, rounds, the pot — waits on this tap.
- **IMPACT.** 0 of 2 organizers saw a success state. Organizer verdict 3/10: "Fix the lock bug and add a real invite and this is a 7." Prod telemetry (read-only): `lock_ok` = 1 all-time (2026-07-27, pre-bug); `lock_fail` = 11, all 2026-08-29, every payload `{"msg":"staged is not defined"}`. The bug shipped in D97 (commit `1fd47e1`, 2026-08-04) and has been live 25 days; prod holds **seven setup-phase leagues, all with buy-in 7500 and none locked** (Orchestrator check, read-only, 2026-08-29: all seven were created 2026-07-17 → 07-21, *before* D97 shipped on 08-04 — they are July pilot/test leagues, not evidence of Pros hitting this bug. `client_events` holds no lock attempt of any kind between 08-04 and this audit; the 11 `lock_fail` rows are the audit's own.) Desert Dogs now sits in prod as `structure=squads2, phase=season, 2 empty squads, 1 member` — a state spec §15 forbids.
- **Likely root cause (validator-verified).**
  - `index.html:15218` `return { emails: emails.length, invited: staged.length, nextPhase };` — D97 deleted `const staged = state.wizInvitees || []` and its loop but left this line. The throw fires after `league_settings.update` with `locked_at` (`:15127–15149`), the `seasons` insert (`:15189–15196`), the `form_squads` RPC (`:15203`) and the `leagues.update` phase flip (`:15211`) have all committed; the handler at `:15494–15505` catches it, `humanError()` (`:4106`, default branch `:4117`) yields the generic sentence, and `openLockShare()` (`:15501`, the only call site; the only surface rendering `cupseason.app/?join=CODE` as text, `:14135–14170`) never runs. `tests/preflight.mjs` runs `node --check`, which cannot see a free identifier (the `memName` crash, F-007, was the same class).
  - **Mechanics underneath:** "lock" is four direct client writes plus one RPC, not a `lock_league` RPC — the class CLAUDE.md says must be an RPC. Retap is non-idempotent in the dangerous direction: RLS `settings_write` (`baseline:2313`, `is_commissioner AND locked_at IS NULL`) silently matches zero rows, `leagues_update` (`baseline:2156`) has no lock guard, and `nextPhase` is derived from *local* `state.structure`. The solo path writes `phase:'season'` directly and never calls `start_season`, bypassing D58's minimum-four gate by construction. `WizardService.swift` ports the same five-write sequence to iOS.
  - **Share fallback:** `shareInvite()` (`:14113–14127`) tries `navigator.share`, then `clipboard.writeText`, then `catch(e){ toast('Invite code: ' + code) }` (`:14127`). No reachable surface prints the URL as copyable text.
  - **Invite-by-email** does not exist: `#emailSlots` is absent from markup, `state.emails` is permanently `['']`, no function or Edge Function mails from `public.invites`; `Cup-Season-Guide.md:107` still promises "or invite by email".
  - **$75:** `#stakeVal` is static `$75` markup (`:3263`) never re-rendered after `resetWizard()` sets `state.stake=0`; `league_settings.buyin_cents DEFAULT 7500 NOT NULL` (`baseline:1082`) and `create_league` inserts bare defaults (`baseline:237`), so any reload mid-wizard (`applyBylaws`, `:14351`) reloads $75 — and `join_covenant_info` reads it for any pre-lock joiner. `state.stake:75` at `:3793` is the demo seed, not the leak.
  - **Errors:** `humanError` (`:4106–4118`) has no pattern for D58's user-written message, maps a clipboard `permission denied` to "Please sign in again." (`org/60-season-share-tap.jpg`), and maps a `not-null` violation to "That didn't go through — please try again." (`:4116`).
- **RECOMMENDATION.** (1) Today, client-only: `invited: 0` at `:15218`; split commit from celebration in the handler so any post-commit exception re-reads `leagues.phase`/`locked_at` and still opens the share sheet; inline persistent error under `#lockBtn`, not a 1.7-s toast; pass user-facing RPC messages through verbatim; add an acorn/eslint-scope no-undef pass to preflight. (2) This week, log then build a `lock_league(p_league, p_bylaws)` SECURITY DEFINER RPC (one transaction; idempotent on `locked_at`; phase from the *stored* structure; D37 grant/revoke) and have both `lockBylaws` and `WizardService.lock` call it. (3) Promote `openLockShare` into an invite sheet reachable from every share control, always printing the URL as selectable text with Copy link / Copy message (prewritten: league, Pro, first tee, buy-in or "bragging rights", link, three things to know). (4) Home hero: branch on phase — a locked-and-alone league says "Share the invite link", never "Lock it in". (5) Money: new migration `alter table league_settings alter column buyin_cents set default 0` (log the §7 "$75" vs D46 "$0" CONFLICT; recommend D46 wins); call `renderSetup()` in `resetWizard()`; lift Buy-in out of Customize. (6) Fix the Guide (no email invite) or build it as its own decision. (7) Alert when `lock_fail` outnumbers `lock_ok`.
- **Expected impact.** Restores 100% of client-side locks (today 0 can succeed) for the seven stalled prod leagues; organizer's own estimate 3 → 7, `setupClear` 4 → 7, `wouldInvite` 4 → 6–7; ends the "$75 nobody chose" covenant before it reaches the friends the season pass depends on.
- **Second pass: Confirmed UX problem (3/3 lenses).** Refuted or trimmed: "the product's only invite moment" (overstated — Standings hero, Members sheet, Add-golfers footer and Home hero all carry Share; `openLockShare` is only the sole *URL-as-text* surface); "a code in a vanishing toast" is partly a harness artifact (a real phone gets the OS share sheet first); "no way to invite friends who lack accounts" is wrong — the signed-out `/?join=` link works for account-less friends by D97 design, the email gap is a doc mismatch; "$75 hidden from the lock decision" — the step-3 review shows BUY-IN and both testers changed it (but $75 *is* the effective default via the static label + DB default, which is the real defect); "seven pilot Pros stalled here" (`index.html:10080`) is misattributed — that comment records D96's measurement before D97 introduced this bug.

### TOP-2 — Nobody can tell what they are joining until after they have committed to $50

**Stage:** activation (with a monetization trust cost). **Master issues:** M-023, M-024, M-025, M-026, M-133, M-143, M-144 (+ M-028, M-029).

- **OBSERVATION.** Cold door: "Rally your crew. Post real rounds. Take the cup." + two buttons (`skep/02-cold-door.jpg`). Invite landing adds one line: "You're invited to The Papago Grind. Enter your email and you're in." (`join/02-B-join-link-landing.jpg`). Real flow: email → code → golfer card → orientation → consent sheet "Before you join The Papago Grind · THE FINE PRINT, UP FRONT" with four rows — `BUY-IN $50 / player · on the pot sheet`, `PRESET Standard`, `PARTICIPATION FLOOR 2 rounds / mo`, `FINISH Cup Final · final 4 weeks` — and "Join — I'm in for $50" (`join/11-J-after-take-me-in.jpg`). No roster, no organizer's name, no dates, no scoring, no payment path. "Not now" drops the invite; the code must be retyped (`join/12-K-not-now.jpg`). On iOS, "2nd · — held · 10 points back of the lead." with no definition of a point on any of 11 screens (`ios/01-door.jpg`).
- **INTERPRETATION.** The invite carries no pitch; the door carries no mechanism; the consent sheet discloses the stake but not the league. The teaching (welcome sheet, "How scoring works") all lands *after* "I'm in for $50".
- **IMPACT.** Joiner verdict 3/10. Three of four joiner personas scored `conceptClear` ≤ 5 and all four `wouldPay` ≤ 5. The skeptic named the sheet as the bail point: "a real Sam replies 'wait, fifty bucks for what?' — and the app cannot answer." Growth is foursome-by-foursome with no paid acquisition (decision-log:722), so the invite must sell itself; today it depends on the friend's nagging. `spec/gtm-year1.md:97–99` already asked for "make the invite carry a preview of the league (name, crew, pot)" with Invite→accept as a leading metric.
- **Likely root cause (validator-verified).** `covenantGate()` (`index.html:15422–15440`) renders exactly what `join_covenant_info` returns, and that anon RPC (`supabase/migrations/20260722211500_covenant_pulse_pairings.sql:29–42`) selects only name/buyin/preset/floor/finish/structure from `leagues` + `league_settings` — no `league_members`, no `seasons`; it was scoped to setup-QA S3-01 ("money never surprises anybody"), a disclosure, not a decision surface. `league_by_code` (`20260714040000_join_polish.sql:14–17`) returns only the name. The landing copy (`:17832`, `:17914`) was written for the pre-covenant instant-join flow. `cs_code`/`cs_code_name` are removed from localStorage **before** `covenantGate` runs (`:17564–17565`, an anti-loop guard), so a decline is indistinguishable from a failed join and leaves only a toast (`:17572`). **New, found while tracing:** `boot()`'s signed-in join path (`:17506–17512`) calls `join_league` with **no** `covenantGate` at all — a returning user tapping a `/?join=` link is put on the pot sheet with zero disclosure. iOS `CovenantSheet` (`JoinLeagueFlow.swift:131–138`) mirrors the same four rows; `HomeHero` (`HomeView.swift:488–556`) has no tap route to any scoring explainer.
- **RECOMMENDATION.** (1) New migration: `join_covenant_info` also returns `pro_name`, `member_count`, first ~6 names+markers (fail-closed, names only, honour `discoverable`), `starts_on`, `ends_on`, `weeks`, pot split, counting cap, allowance — all nullable for skew; log it as an IA decision extending S3-01 + D57. (2) Render a league card on the invite door *before* email ("Casey (THE PRO) invited you · 5 in · Sat Sep 5 → Sat Jan 2 · 17 wks · $50 → $250 pot, 60/25/15 · every round you post, anywhere, scores against your own number · How scoring works →"); fix the landing line to "Sign in to review The Papago Grind — you'll confirm before you join." (3) Turn the covenant into the decision sheet: WHO, WHEN, HOW (bands), FLOOR with consequence and bye, FINISH defined, STAKE with payment note ("you pay Casey directly; Cup Season keeps the tab, never the cash"), PRESET tappable, plus D50's parked "The Pro rules…" paragraph. (4) For `viaInvite` skip the D82 orientation (one fewer screen). (5) Persist "Not now": keep the code, render a Home card "Invite pending · The Papago Grind · Review" with the code pre-filled. (6) Make `boot()`'s signed-in path call `covenantGate`. (7) iOS: rank card → "How points work" sheet; name the leader ("10 back of Galen · 2 of 2"). (8) Cold door: one sentence under the slogan (the meta description already has it: "Season-long golf with the people you already play with — points, pot, pressure.") — as a D83 amendment, not a defect.
- **Expected impact.** The invite becomes a self-explaining offer instead of a code relayed by text; the "fifty bucks for what?" question is answered on the same sheet as the button; the "Not now" cohort is recovered instead of lost; the money posture is stated pre-commit, which is the precondition for D106 and the season pass.
- **Second pass: Confirmed UX problem (3/3).** Refuted or trimmed: the cold door being a slogan is an **owner decision** (D83 "the door sells with its own splash"; D82 puts depth post-auth) — reframe M-143 as a one-line D83 amendment; `v23 · __CS_VERSION__` on the door is the expected **unstamped local build** (CLAUDE.md rule 2; prod reads `v23 · 34d20b6`) — struck from evidence; the "Not now" toast does fire (screenshot timing missed it); **no persona was blocked** — all four joiners could explain the game by the end of their sessions, so post-commit teaching works; the covenant was scoped to money by S3-01, so part of the ask is a scope extension, not a defect in what was built.

### 3.2 The common core

Synthesized from the eight, with frequency (`synthesis-rules-mental-model.md` §5.1):

> Cup Season runs a months-long golf league for a friend group [8/8]. Everyone posts real rounds from anywhere [8/8]. Each round is scored against your own handicap into 5–12 points, so a high handicap and a low one compete evenly [7/8]. Three of eight reached the product's own category unprompted ("fantasy-league-style"; spec §12: "fantasy sports where your foursome are the athletes"). Six of eight explanations end on an admission of not knowing — how the winner is decided (A2, A7, iOS), whether the round they just posted counted (A1, A2), how to pay (A6). Every one includes some form of "the organizer will have to explain it."

### 3.3 What it is supposed to be

- `spec/product-vision-v1.0.md:14`: "Cup Season exists to make every round of golf matter because it belongs to a season." Golfers "build rivalries, win championships, create traditions." Success metrics (`:146–147`): join a league in under 30 seconds · post a round in under 60 seconds · understand standings in under 10 seconds · **never need a tutorial**.
- `spec/spec-v1.0.md` §1: one pipeline, five stages — **POST** a round → **SCORE** it (bands vs your number) → **COUNT** it (best-N, floor, bye, partial months) → **RANK** squads (the race, the Climb, seeds) → **CROWN** a champion (Cup Final scored fresh or the points table; the §14.3 ladder; the ceremony; per-person pot). Side games and events are parallel ledgers that never touch cup points (modes §5). The pot is a ledger with two numbers (D106).
- `Cup-Season-Guide.md` line 3 has the one-breath version no screen shows: "Season-long fantasy golf for your regular group … ends in a Cup Final. The app keeps score and keeps the pot's books."

### 3.4 The gaps

The testers rebuilt stages 1–3 (POST → SCORE → COUNT) almost verbatim and unaided. Stages 4–5 (RANK → CROWN) are blank in all eight. **The model is not wrong; it is truncated** — a season with a head and no tail — and no sentence anywhere says why today's round matters (D51's stake line is decided and unbuilt). The lens model (rounds belong to your card, leagues read them) never landed; "card" carries five meanings. The fantasy-league analogy is doing the work the UI should: it supplies "squads", "points", "a final" as slots, and testers fill each slot with a guess.

### 3.5 What everyone understood (8/8 or 7/8)

- Post the real rounds you already play, from any course.
- Each round is scored against your *own* handicap, so different handicaps compete evenly.
- Five bands, 5 to 12 points; a bad day still scores; "the only way to hurt your team is not to play" (quoted back by four testers).
- Best four a month; two a month or the squad loses points (the best-taught rule in the product — the counting-cap (i) and the "BUMPED" receipt).
- The app keeps the money on a tab; you pay each other.
- Live scorecard for skins / match play / Wolf, settled hole by hole (6/8).

### 3.6 What nobody understood (0/8 confident)

- How the Cup Final decides the winner and what "scored fresh" does to points already on the table.
- How ties break — anywhere (standings, seeds, Points King, skins).
- What the 95% allowance does to a round, or that it is why two screens show different numbers for one round.
- What "Attested" asks of a player at post time (D13 says the word should be "Vouch"; 0 occurrences vs 15 "attested").
- How, when, and to whom the buy-in is paid; how a squad's 60% becomes a person's payout.
- Whether a round posted this week counts (until inferred from zeros).
- What "the cup" is, in one sentence. The product's name is its least-defined word (four senses: the title, cup points, the Cup Final, "Cups & events · 1 · Played in").

## 8. Retention audit

Source: `synthesis-retention-monetization.md`. The retention curve **peaks mid-season and collapses at both ends**: Day 0 return ~5 (nobody can say what a cup/season is from the door; the first thing a joiner is told is a $50 buy-in), Week 1 ~4 (a broken points promise), Week 4 / mid-season ~6 (rank + gap + floor bar + a rival's name are legible in 3 seconds), late season ~5, finale ~5, season+1 day ~4, **season+30 days ~2** (by D23/D27 policy there are no re-engagement nudges and the only off-app emails are sign-in codes, a buddy request and one season-end email). Nearly every absent emotional element has a built or decided answer in the decision log (D24 magic number, D52/D108 clash, D66 ceremony, D67 career record, D68 email, D105 race view, D41 run-it-back) that no tester could observe — no completed season, forming leagues, a chip suppressed by a nearest-deadline sort, or shipped the day before.

## 9. Monetization readiness

| Dimension | /10 | Reasoning (SEEN → READ) |
|---|---|---|
| **Perceived value today** | **4** | What is visible on day one is "a chat, a calendar, a pot ledger, gross scores, a $50 ask, a Pro's lock button" (A4) — things the group already has. | **Social lock-in** | **3** | A league is 5–16 people who all have to be here, and the audit shows the graph is not yet captured: no invite email, the invite link never displayed, buddies are a second graph "with nothing to do with leagues", the group text is where the banter still lives (zero replies on any board). |
| **Switching cost** | **3** | Pre-season: zero ("my 91 lives in 18Birdies too"). Mid-season: the counting/floor arithmetic ("a formula I'd hate to maintain by hand") and the round history. No multi-season record yet to leave behind (D67 built; nothing to aggregate). |
| **Annual renewal motivation** | **3** | The renewal moment is designed (D41, D66, D68, D101's "run it back goes back to being a celebration") and unproven: no completed season anywhere, no title to defend, the pot never collected, the observer's 30-day projection is 2/10. |

**The skeptic's own $79/year justification** — "It runs the whole season so nobody has to be the spreadsheet guy — it scores every round against your own number so the 8 and the 20 are in the same race, it tells you when you're about to miss your two rounds and cost the team five points, it shows who owes the pot and who gets paid at the end, and it's the place the trash talk lives because the scores are already there." Every clause of that sentence is a built mechanic. None of it was visible to him on the day he joined. Against the competitor set he named (group text, spreadsheet, 18Birdies, Venmo), Cup Season is *worse* for banter, *same* pre-season for standings, *better* in-season for the arithmetic "the app never sells", *same-ish* for posting, and *replaceable* for the pot and the calendar.

**What must happen before pushing monetization, in order:**

1. **A season has to be seen ending well.** The ceremony (D66), the email (D68), the career record (D67) and run-it-back exist; nobody outside the sandbox has watched them fire. The first PIGL close is the real test; until then every "would pay" number is a guess about a product the tester has not seen.
2. **The race has to be visible for the whole season, not the last week.** Fix the foreshadow sort; seeds/magic number on Home; the clash chip on every live league (verify `week_clashes` opens a row on the next tick).
3. **Pre-season honesty.** Six of seven testers were promised league points that did not exist. A product asking for money cannot open with a broken promise.
4. **The pot has to be true.** D106 (owed vs collected) plus a payment note/deadline before any league is asked to pay a pass "out of the pot" — the pass is paid from money the Pro has, and today the app cannot say how much that is.
5. **The invite has to work.** An organizer who cannot send a link cannot form the league that would pay.
6. **State the price where the wizard sets the buy-in** (D56 surfaces) with D101's numbers; testers explicitly could not tell whether "membership" would be charged to them, to the Pro, or mid-season.
7. **Then** the focus-group instrument (D56's deck) has real surfaces to test against. Stripe stays parked until 1–6 are true.

## 13. Critical product question — what is Cup Season actually selling?

**What the sources say it sells:** *season-long competition* as the frame, *rivalry with friends* as the emotion, *a recurring golf tradition* as the renewal. `product-vision-v1.0.md:14`: "Cup Season exists to make every round of golf matter because it belongs to a season"; "Not another score tracking app. Not another handicap app." Spec §12: "fantasy sports where your foursome are the athletes." D101's own Pro sentence: "The app's $89 for the year out of the pot — call it seven bucks a man — and it covers every season we run."

**What it currently looks like it is selling, screen by screen (SEEN):**

| Surface | What it presents | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Door | "Rally your crew. Post real rounds. Take the cup." + email + invite code | scorekeeping with a prize |
| Home (member, forming) | Start a league · Start an event · Join a league · **Lock it in and invite your crew** · MONTH CLOSES · floor penalty | **league administration** |
| Welcome sheet | "You're on the pot sheet: $50 buy-in." first | **a money ask** — the bail point |
| Clubhouse header | Code · Add golfers · THE PRO · Cancel & delete this league | league administration |
| League tab | Members & invites · Share the season · Squads · ▸ LEAGUE RULES & PRO SHOP · "COMING AT LAUNCH" | admin + an upsell |
| Pot tab | $250 · $0 collected · 5 still owe · buy-in checkboxes · "Post a stake" | a ledger |
| Board | joins, match results, one chat line, 🔥 ⚑ | social golf, thinly |
| ⊕ → LIVE | strokes off the low man, skins riding, "JORDAN PAYS PRIYA $60" | **side bets** — rated 8/10 by two personas |
| Standings (mid-season) | 1st · 22 over Jade · LOCKED · EVERYONE ADVANCES · Points King / Iron Man | **season-long competition** — the only surface that reads as the category |
| You | index, badges, "No silverware yet", rivalries 2–0, SEASON I | identity + a record with nothing in it yet |

**Choice, from the list:** Cup Season is *selling* **season-long competition / rivalry / a recurring tradition**. It currently *looks like* it is selling **league administration and a money ask**, with **side betting** as the part people actually enjoyed.

**The gap, stated plainly.** The product's own one-breath sentence ("Season-long fantasy golf for your regular group … ends in a Cup Final. The app keeps score and keeps the pot's books.") appears in the Guide and in the page's meta description and nowhere a tester can see before or during joining. What a new member sees first is administration (a lock button, a code, a Pro) and money (a $50 line, a $0-collected pot); what a mid-season member sees is competition without an ending (a lead, a floor, a Cup Final in a collapsed row); what an ex-member sees is nothing (no email until a season ends, no title to defend). The layer testers praised most — the live settle-up — is the one the spec treats as the acquisition surface and a side product, and it is the only layer that showed a tester who won, who lost and what it cost, in the same session. Two testers independently wrote the same diagnosis: the scoring model is "smart and fair" / "genuinely good", and "the app just doesn't tell you any of that until you dig."

**Closing the gap, one sentence per hierarchy level:** *Vision → IA:* the season's ending is a first-class object on every live-season screen, not a bylaws row. *IA → mechanics:* the engines that already exist (magic number, seeds, clash, ceremony, career record) render *before* they resolve, so the season is watched, not discovered. *Mechanics → UI:* the hero says one gain ("post a 9 by Sunday and you pass Galen") and one rival, every week, for every league of any size. *UI → copy:* the door says what the Guide says; the welcome sheet says the game before the money; the standings say "seed" and "scored fresh" in a sentence. *Implementation:* the invite link renders as a link; the share buttons say what they did; the foreshadow strip stops sorting the Cup Final out of view.

## 14. Zero-instruction test

> If the developer disappeared and a new user downloaded Cup Season tomorrow, could that user run a season with five friends without asking another human how it works?

**Answer: NO.** The exact reasons, from evidence only (this block is identical in `blind-ux-audit.md` §14, `critical-findings.md` Part 4 and `README.md`):

1. **The organizer cannot complete setup and know it.** Both organizers saw "Lock failed" on every tap (`staged is not defined`, `index.html:15218`); one learned the league was live only by reloading; the post-lock share sheet never opened. (M-001)
2. **The five friends cannot be invited from the app.** No email/SMS invite exists; "Add golfers" finds only existing accounts; the join URL is never displayed — only a code in a vanishing toast. The organizer must text "download Cup Season, tap 'I have an invite code', type THEPTCQ5" — which is asking another human. (M-002, M-003)
3. **A friend who opens the link cannot tell what they are joining** before "Join — I'm in for $50" (no roster, Pro, dates, scoring or payment path) — so they text the organizer to ask. (M-023, M-024, M-025)
4. **Once in, every friend's Home hands them the organizer's lock button** and no member-facing next step. (M-030, M-033)
5. **Their first round promises 5/6/12 league points and delivers zero with no explanation**, described with three different signs; nobody could say what they earned. (M-040, M-045, M-044)
6. **Nobody can answer "how do we win."** The Cup Final, seeds, ties and the floor are undefined or contradictory in-product; the rules sit four taps deep behind a collapsed panel. Every persona said, in those words, that the organizer would explain it in the parking lot. (M-055, M-056, M-057, M-054)
7. **Money is a ledger nobody knows how to settle**, with a $75 default nobody chose. (M-110, M-004)

**What WOULD pass:** an already-formed, already-running league where members only post rounds and play live side games. The post form, course → tee → rating/slope autofill, the live bands, the receipt's differential arithmetic, the live scorer's stroke line ("Marco gets 4: holes 3, 6, 16, 18"), the running skins ledger, guest play without an account, and the settlement card were praised by every persona (side-game verdicts 7–8/10). The season wrapper around them — setup, invite, consent, status, endgame, money — is what fails the test.
