# Post-round feedback — five questions, two minutes

Ask these the same way every time. No leading words. Record the answer as
given, then the build number and the date.

1. **Walk me through what happened from arriving at the course to putting the phone away.** (Listen for: where they paused, what they asked someone, what they skipped.)

2. **Right now, where is your score?** (Listen for: "posted", "on my phone", "I don't know". Then: **How sure are you?** — as a number from 1 to 5.)

3. **Was there anything you did for the group outside the app today?** (Text messages, a spreadsheet, a group chat, telling someone the tee time.)

4. **If your group plays next week, what would you do with the app, if anything?**

5. **We are thinking about charging the organiser about $6.50 to $7.80 per golfer per year — $59 to $109 a league depending on size, first year free — paid out of the pot. Would your group pay that for what you used today?** Yes / no / not for this. **What would have to be true for a yes?**

Optional, only if they raise it: what was confusing, and where.

Record on the [session log](session-log.md) whether anyone from Cup Season was present or on a call during the round.
