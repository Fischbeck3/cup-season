# The staged pilot — operating materials

Written 2026-09-15. The goal is evidence, not features: prove that groups can
complete rounds and competitions without the founder in the room, and collect
what is needed to decide when to widen. Cohort sizes below are planning
hypotheses, not benchmarks.

| Stage | Who | Size (hypothesis) | What it must prove | Where it is measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · Owner verification | the owner, two phones | 1 | the two-phone lifecycle and recovery checks in [`owner-checks.md`](owner-checks.md) | this checklist, by hand |
| B · Friends | Galen and Jade, plus one regular group | 4–8 | task-based completion without a walkthrough | `pilot_sessions` (assisted vs not), scorecard sections 2, 4, 5 |
| C · Independent pilot | groups the founder does not play with | 3–5 groups, ~20–40 golfers | unassisted completion AND repeat use | scorecard sections 2–6 by cohort `independent` |
| D · Competition pilot | groups running a league, a Ryder, a Major | 5–10 groups | at least one full competition lifecycle, organizer starts another | scorecard section 3 by cohort `competition` |
| E · Limited public | a capped founding cohort | capped; decide the cap at the D gate | unassisted completion at scale with the support load measured | cohort `founding` |

## The files

- [`owner-checks.md`](owner-checks.md) — Stage A: two-phone lifecycle and recovery checks, with the sandbox evidence that already exists.
- [`tester-task-sheet.md`](tester-task-sheet.md) — what a tester is asked to do, without a walkthrough.
- [`post-round-feedback.md`](post-round-feedback.md) — the short form after a round.
- [`organizer-interview.md`](organizer-interview.md) — the interview guide for whoever runs the group.
- [`session-log.md`](session-log.md) — the founder's session and support log, and how it lands in `pilot_sessions`.
- [`weekly-review.md`](weekly-review.md) — the weekly checklist and the scorecard command.
- [`gates-and-stop-conditions.md`](gates-and-stop-conditions.md) — proposed expansion gates and explicit stop conditions.
- [`outreach-drafts.md`](outreach-drafts.md) — drafts only. Nothing is sent from this repository.
- [`telemetry-events.md`](telemetry-events.md) — every event, its denominator, and where the truth lives.
- [`scorecard-2026-09-15.md`](scorecard-2026-09-15.md) — the baseline read from production on the day this was written.

## What is deliberately not here

Names of real golfers, leagues, money, or league politics. This repository is
public. The private half of the pilot — who is in which group, what was said on
a call — lives in `pilot_sessions` and `pilot_cohort_members` in the database,
founder-readable only, and in the founder's private notes.
