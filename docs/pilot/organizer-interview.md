# Organizer interview — twenty minutes, after the second round

For whoever runs the group. Ask, then be quiet. Do not explain a feature in
the middle of an answer; note it and come back at the end.

## How the group actually runs

1. Tell me how this group decides when and where to play. Who does what?
2. What did you do this week to make the round happen — before the app, in it, and around it?
3. Where does the money, if any, live? Who keeps track?

## What the app took over, and what it did not

4. What did the app do for you that you used to do yourself?
5. What are you still doing somewhere else — a chat, a note, a spreadsheet? Why there?
6. When someone in the group did not have the app, what happened?

## Confidence

7. After a round, how do you know everyone's score is in? What do you check?
8. Has the app ever told you something you did not believe? What did you do?

## Next time

9. What would make you set up another season, or a one-off event, for this group?
10. What would make you stop?

## The offer

11. We are considering a league pass, paid by the organiser out of the pot, for a year of seasons: $59 for nine or fewer golfers, $89 for ten to thirteen, $109 for fourteen or more — about $6.50 to $7.80 a golfer a year, and the first year free (D101). Would you pay that for this group? What would you expect for it?
12. Is there anyone you would recommend this to, and what would you tell them it is?

Close: is there anything I should have asked?

Record the answers privately, not in this repository. Log the interview as a
`support` session in [`session-log.md`](session-log.md).
