# Tester task sheet

You are helping us find out whether Cup Season works without anyone explaining
it. Please do these in order, on your own phone, without asking us how. If you
get stuck, that is the finding — note where, and move on. Nothing you do here
affects a real league unless you are already in one.

Fill in the short form after each round: [post-round feedback](post-round-feedback.md).

## Before you play

1. Install the build you were sent and sign in with the code emailed to you.
2. Set up your golfer card.
3. If you organise the group: book a round for the day you are playing and tag the others. If you were tagged: answer the booking from Home.

## On the day

4. The organiser: open the booking and start the round from it, with the group seated.
5. Everyone else: open the same booking and join it.
6. Score every hole. At least one of you should enter someone else's score at least once.
7. Somebody should lose signal on purpose for a few holes (airplane mode), keep scoring, then come back.
8. One person should kill the app and reopen it mid-round.
9. Finish the round from the organiser's phone.

## After the round

10. Find your own round. Open its receipt. Add a photo if you like.
11. Look at Home the next morning and tell us whether it still thinks you have a round to play.
12. If someone in your group had no account: they should open the link the organiser shares, then decide whether to create an account and claim their card.

## A second time

13. Play again within two weeks, the same way, without re-reading this sheet.

## What we are NOT asking

- Do not report the score you *think* you should have. Report what the app says, and whether that is right.
- Do not tell us what you would build. Tell us where you paused, re-read, or guessed.

Known, on purpose: the organiser (or a league member) is the one who finishes; a golfer with no league cannot finish someone else's round. Birdies and eagles are only called on a card whose pars came from the course.
