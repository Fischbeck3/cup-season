BUSINESS MODEL (2.1(b))
Cup Season is free. No In-App Purchases, subscriptions, paid tiers or purchases of any kind, in the app or on cupseason.app; accounts are free. Dollar amounts in the app are a ledger only. A league's organiser ("the Pro", a free role that comes with starting a league) records the buy-in each golfer agreed to, who has paid, an optional text note on how to pay them, and how the pot splits at season's end. "Join — I'm in for $75" records that agreement and charges nothing. A live game can record an optional amount per point, skin, side or bank unit. Every money field stops at $200: league buy-ins, Major buy-ins and live amounts. Pride bets are words only, with no amount. Friends settle with each other outside the app. Cup Season and Fischbeck3 LLC never collect, hold, transfer or pay out money and take no fee; the app has no payment processing and no payment links. Being marked paid unlocks nothing. This build removes never-shown pricing placeholder cards and the Settings "PLAN FREE" row; nothing is or was for sale.

SIGN-IN
Use reviewer@cupseason.app (App Review Information) and tap "Send code": nothing is emailed; a Password field appears (its hint says "from the notes"): use the password in the Sign-in Information fields. Everyone else signs in with an emailed 8-digit code. No third-party or social login.

THE ACCOUNT
Four seeded leagues of fictional golfers; you are a player in each, never the Pro. Please use Ridgeline Cup (active until December 19, 2026; 8 golfers, two squads; $75 buy-in). Sunset Match is finished; its pot lines record what friends settled outside the app.

WALKTHROUGH
1. Compete > Ridgeline Cup: "The squads", then "Every golfer". Tap a golfer for the rounds behind the points, then a round for its receipt.
2. Same league > "The pot" > "Who has paid": $600 ($75 each), 5 of 8 marked paid (a record only).
3. Play (centre tab) > "Add a round you played" > "Add my round" (posting is fine).
4. Play > "Score it live", pick a format, "Tee off", "Finish the round".
5. Golfers > Marcus Reyes > the ... menu: "Block Marcus", "Report".

SAFETY (1.2)
- Names, posts, comments, plans, league and course names and pride-bet words are checked by our database for slurs, sexual terms and threats before they save.
- Report posts, comments and golfers, each with a reason. Block hides their posts and comments and stops their requests, invites and notifications.
- The operator reviews reports within 24 hours and can take down posts, comments and photos and remove accounts; a removed account cannot sign in.
- Photos are not pre-screened. Boards and round photos are seen only by golfers in the same seasons and accepted buddies. A reported photo leaves storage within minutes of takedown (a private copy is kept up to 90 days). Public links exist only when a golfer taps Share.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
You > Card & settings > Settings > "Your account" > "Delete my account" > "Delete permanently". It removes name, email, profile, posts, comments and shared links, queues photos for removal and closes the login. Posted rounds stay as "Former member" so standings hold. Please do not confirm; we can provide a throwaway account.
