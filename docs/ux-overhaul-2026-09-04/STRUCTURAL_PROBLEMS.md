## 2. The problems

### SP-1 · The league season is the only unit of engagement (rank 1 · verdict 3/3)

**Amended statement.** Every *story*, *signal*, *reaction* and *invite link* in Cup Season is keyed to membership in a league season (or, for the first three, an event). A golfer without one — and every member between seasons — can post a round and it will appear silently in their buddies' Home feeds, but it produces no `posts` row, so it reaches no lock screen, carries no reaction strip, and lands on no board. Nothing they can send brings a *newcomer* into their circle: the only account-to-account link in the product is a league's. The brief's funnel (casual play → competition → recurring competition) has a play-BEFORE rung (the tee sheet, buddy tags, RSVP) and no play-AFTER rung: the round happens and nothing follows from it.

**Four rails, named so the design phase can take them one at a time:** **story home** (a posted round outside a league has no `posts` row) · **signal** (push recipients are `league_members`; no friendship branch exists for a round) · **reaction** (`post_kudos.member_id` is an FK to `league_members`, so a reaction cannot key off a person) · **invite** (no link lands a non-user in the sender's circle).

**Level.** Mechanics — four rails. *Not* IA: Home's NOW lane already unions memberships (D218, `HomeView.swift:104-107`) and People is already membership-independent; the membership-dispatched hero and Clubhouse are SP-2 and SP-7, and the friend-to-friend competition object is SP-4.

**(c) Unruled — the structural core.**
- **Story home.** `posts_home_check` requires `league_id` or `event_id` (`20260716160000_ryder_slice3.sql:26-28`, never dropped; prod: 0 posts with both null, **20 non-voided rounds with no post row at all**). `round_to_board` writes one post per `league_members × active season` covering `played_on` (`20260727160000_board_voice.sql:56-75`); `finish_live_round` guards board writes with `if lr.league_id is not null` (`20260831120000:906, :930`); `round_moments` writes only `posts(league_id, season_id, …)` (`20260831130000:89-90, :192-193`); `achievements` is profile-scoped and unposted (`20260716020000:23-31`).
- **Signal.** `push/index.ts:485-521` fans league posts to `league_members` minus author; `:464-483` fans event posts to `event_players`; there is no friendship branch for a post, and `push_nudges` kinds are constrained to `nudge/invite/request/rsvp` (`20260827210000_push_wave7.sql:37-38`; `docs/ios/push-contract.md:84-89`). A round with no post cannot fire the webhook at all, so editing the recipient list would not help: the producer must move to the `rounds` insert path.
- **Reaction.** The FK above, plus `HomeSocial.swift:65-71` building targets only from posts carrying `league_id` and `:99-101` throwing "not a member of that league" on write.
- **Invite.** The phone claims only `?join=` and `?claim=` (`CupSeasonApp.swift:28`); the web boot reads `unsub/share/join/claim/exit` (`index.html:20163-20431`). A person link is a new anon endpoint under L-45.
- **Friend-scope competition record** (shared with SP-4): `my_rivalries`'s `shared` CTE joins `league_members × seasons` (`20260716210000:76-83`); `create_forfeit` raises 'crew only' (`20260724120000:59, :70-73`; re-declared `20260727160000:344`).
- **The wrapped-league pool** (`Models.swift:300-303`) drops wrapped leagues from Home whenever a live one exists — a client decision with no log entry (see SP-2).

**Root cause.** Vision `product-vision-v1.0.md:14-15` makes the season the unit that gives a round meaning, and everything below inherited it — but the same document sanctions the missing rung: persona 3 is the buddies trip (`:59-60`), the stories it wants are "a lifetime record against friends" and "see friends' rounds" (`:95, :101`), and its own notification examples are "Friend posted · Milestone reached · League lead changed" (`:123-125`). The fix restates the vision rather than breaking it. Below it, **D28** and **D107** are the two rulings that *chose* the league as the reaction and story home; "buddies have nothing to do with leagues or points" is guide copy (`GuideCopy.swift:75`), not a ruling — D80 (`:2725-2790`) is a noun ruling.

**Why structural.** The strongest copy/default/single-screen bundle available — porting the web's rung 7/6/5 CTAs onto the phone hero, rewriting rung 6 to the truth ("your buddies see every round"), naming the champion on the wrapped hero, hoisting the existing `my_rivalries` record onto Home — leaves Persona F unable to answer Q3 ("what can I do right now": react to Dev's round — impossible by FK and by the absence of a post) and Q4 ("who am I competing with": no object relates two buddies outside a shared season), and leaves Persona B in the same place after her season wrapped, because `round_to_board`'s season gate means her and her league-mates' post-season rounds produce no post either. Each fix is a migration, an RPC, an FK or an AASA change.

**Laws that bound the fix.** L-13 (a round outside a window builds your number and posts to the Tour Card — the wall already grants a sub-season rung) · L-40 (the free door is the tee sheet with anyone; guests need no account) · L-42/L-22 (reactions stay the six-emoji crew vocabulary in service of the round's story; never likes-as-currency, no follower counts, no leaderboards of attention) · L-20 + D23/CC-22 (a buddy-round push is an *activity* notification of the vision's kind, not a nudge — but any push escalation still needs its own level-2 entry; once per condition, no shame) · L-37 (a profile-homed post's RLS must use the Tour Card gate) · L-45/L-36 (a person invite is a new anon endpoint, fail-closed, with the db-check edit) · L-21/L-23 (empties are curated, never fabricated) · L-03 (a buddies ladder is a server read, not a client sort) · **D69** consent stands: do not loosen RSVP — Persona F's "I'm in" becomes an ask-for-a-seat request, not a write on someone else's round.

**What a solution must do.**
1. Give a round a story home that does not require a league — a profile-homed post (or a synthesised `home_stories()` over `achievements` + `home_feed`) — and make the league post a *derivative* of it, not the only form. The Tour Card gate is the RLS.
2. Make the signal follow the same circle the feed already uses (accepted friendships ∪ league mates ∪ event players, `20260723090000:24-35`), produced on the round's insert path, deduped per recipient, and named for one of D23's eight emotions.
3. Key reactions to the round, not to a league post — which means the `post_kudos` FK moves off `league_members`.
4. Ship one link that lands a person who has never heard of Cup Season inside the sender's circle, honouring L-45 (a new anon endpoint + db-check) and L-12's covenant rule if it ever also joins a league.
5. Do all four without inventing a second social product: no follower counts, no attention leaderboards, no fabricated empties, and every rung must still funnel toward a season (vision `:14-15`).
6. Cite D28's reopen trigger, D107's accepted tradeoff and D177's "needs a decision" by number in the entry that supersedes them.

**Success metric it moves.** Friend connection (a sendable link and a reaction that lands) · activation and D7/D30 for a golfer with no league and every between-seasons member · empty-state engagement (the empties become the product's first rung) · WAU/MAU (a round by someone you know is the one event that reliably reopens the app).
