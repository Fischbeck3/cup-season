### D222 · Four places become five — Home · Compete · ⊕ Play · Golfers · You — **PROPOSED**

*(2026-09-05. **Level 3 (IA).** Carries **O-01** and **O-06**. Sources: the owner's first-principles brief (§"Navigation: ~4–5 primary destinations mirroring the user's mental model"); `docs/ux-overhaul-2026-09-04/INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §3; the six persona walks; `ia-blueprint.html:168` (the slot's own name was Compete); D11 `:173-175` (retired "clubhouse" from prose the day the tab kept it); T-08 (crew is a register word, never a list label).)*

- **Current mechanic:** four places — Home · Clubhouse · ⊕ · You — with the ⊕ as a verb, not a place (IA P4). Ruled four times: the preamble `:13-15`, D82 `:2862-2865`, D93 `:3307-3314` ("NAV UNCHANGED — and that is the point"), D94, IOS-011 `DECISIONS.md:125-127` (a fifth tab and "the ⊕ as a menu page" both rejected), `IOS-002 §2:47`.
- **Problem observed:** COMMUNITY and HISTORY share a tab with ME (D177's two group heads on You), and COMPETE is a single league room. Every walked persona paid for it: the rivalry record is "three screens away" (persona F), the 0–2 against the champion is "three screens away" (persona B), the opponent is one tap plus a scroll (persona C). Splitting COMMUNITY out is the single change that answers "who am I competing with" without a scroll.
- **Recommendation (proposed):** five slots — **Home · Compete · ⊕ Play · Golfers · You**. The blueprint's four *questions* survive and are answered better (What happened? → the wire and the VERDICT kind; Where do I stand? → the ME strip; I'm playing → ⊕ Play; How am I doing? → You/Your record); its four *nouns* do not. D82's one surviving rule — **the ⊕ is a verb, not a place** — is upheld verbatim, including the snap-back and the haptic (`MainTabView.swift:336-339`). **Clubhouse → Compete** (O-06 rides here: the blueprint's own name for the slot, and a restoration as much as an override). **People → Golfers**, because T-08 rules *crew* a register word and never a list label, and the tab holds three tiers "buddies" does not cover. **Post → Play**, because the tab is labelled Post and does not post. **Home keeps its name**, so `HomeRoute`, the `.home` push route and the App Review notes keep theirs.
- **Principle served:** the brief's hierarchy (ME → NOW → COMPETE → COMMUNITY → HISTORY) and its "~4–5 destinations mirroring the user's mental model, never the backend"; vision #1 (golf first).
- **Expected user benefit:** Q4 is answered at one tap from anywhere instead of three screens; the season stops being a room you must be inside; the leagueless golfer gets a destination that is about people rather than about an absence.
- **Tradeoffs:** the most expensive change in the programme. Every deep link, both league-naming push routes, the widget surface, `openLeague`, `preferredLeague`, the Guide and `docs/ios/app-review-notes.md` retarget. It cannot be A/B'd cheaply. **If the owner declines the fifth slot,** the fallback that preserves most of the value is: keep four, put Golfers inside Compete as its first segment, and accept that Q4 costs one tap.
- **CONFLICT (named):** the IA blueprint's four rooms · D82 `:2862-2865` · D93 `:3307-3314` · D94 · IOS-011 `DECISIONS.md:125-127` · `IOS-002 §2:47` — **superseded at level 3 by owner authority.** D93's *reasons* survive: the ⊕ stays a verb, the feed stays whole, no quadrant is introduced (see D228).

### D223 · No league room — a season is a page — **PROPOSED**

*(2026-09-05. **Level 3 (IA).** Carries **O-02**, **O-13** and **O-10**. Sources: `INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §7; `LeagueRoomScreen.swift`; `ClubhouseView.swift:45-134, :110-113`; D93 `:3355` ("one authoritative surface per question"); D203 `:5518, :5528`; D218 `:5664`.)*

- **Current mechanic:** the Clubhouse *is* the league room (D203), rooted on Standings for the current league (`IOS-002 §5:104-113`), with a six-segment strip (STANDINGS · BOARD · SCHEDULE · POT · ALBUM · LEAGUE, two of which are doors) and swipe-paging between memberships that writes `store.preferredLeague` as a side effect.
- **Problem observed:** the room is the only place the competition lives, so a golfer between seasons or in a moment with no league has nowhere to go; the segmented strip mixes panes with doors; the paging's side effect makes one stored value do two jobs (the room's current page **and** Home's lens); and `openLeague` lands every door on the table, so the story never gets a first screen.
- **Recommendation (proposed):** **Compete** is a peer list of every competition I am in, with finished ones folded under "Finished" and the champion named. Each row pushes a **season page** — one scrolling page with a story spine, where the six segments become sections and doors: the story line, THIS WEEK, THE TABLE with the endgame permanently beneath it, THE POT, and doors to the Board, the Schedule, the Album and the Rules, with the Pro's verb row at the foot. **D93's "one authoritative surface per question" is upheld** — standings, board, pot, schedule and rules each keep exactly one home. D203's paging survives as a gesture **between season pages** and loses its `preferredLeague` write (see D229). **Every `HomeRoute.league(id)` / `.pot(id)` / `openLeague(id)` retargets** to the season page (O-10): the hero's own door reads "Open the season →" and opens the page; a door named for a table (`See the table →`) anchors `pane: .table`, so **D218's rule survives restated, not broken**.
- **Principle served:** L-35 (story first, table second); the brief's "seasons are the magic layer… never League → Season → Event → Standings → Round".
- **Expected user benefit:** the first thing a golfer sees about their season is a sentence about a person, not a segmented control; a moment with no league finally has a door; the record's season rows open a story instead of a dead table.
- **Tradeoffs:** the biggest single lift on the phone (`LeagueRoomScreen` plus five panes). Sequenced **after** the season page has proven itself as a pushed destination, because no walked persona failed *because* the tab is called Clubhouse — this is the change with the least direct evidence and the most blast radius.
- **CONFLICT (named):** `IOS-002 §5:104-113` ("Root = Standings for the current league") · D203 `:5528` ("the Clubhouse is *the* league room") · D93 `:3323-3324` — **the container is superseded at level 3; D93's one-surface-per-question rule (`:3355`) is upheld.** D218 `:5664` is **kept, restated**: doors say where they go, and where they go changes. O-13 falls with O-02; the peers-side-by-side pattern is reused in Compete's list.

### D225 · Creation starts from intent — the doors name what I want, not what the engine has — **PROPOSED**

Six of six `setup` leagues in prod are a founder alone — the wizard mints before anyone has agreed to anything. Behind it, three questions in order — **Who? · When? · What's on it?** — then Start. **Nothing is minted until all three are answered**, the **name is asked last** and pre-filled from the roster, and the `leagues` row is minted at that tap **in one transaction with `lock_league`**, so an abandoned wizard leaves nothing behind. - **Expected user benefit:** an organiser says a sentence and gets a competition; a golfer who owes $50 can always find out how to pay it; $20 becomes possible; nobody creates a ghost league by typing a name.
- **Tradeoffs:** the intent sheet is a new choke point and must refuse "Run a season" configuration to a non-Pro of an existing season (D40, unchanged). A member's version of "start something" inside a season is **Post a forfeit** or **Call someone out** — real acts, not a disabled button. **R18 is a migration this entry did not originally carry**, and it is named here because "required at publish" was otherwise unbuildable as one transaction. **Step 1 has an empty branch and it is part of the ruling:** a first-time organiser has no buddies and `search_golfers` matches an exact @handle or an existing relation only (L-37), so the step offers **Find your friends** (contacts, D251), **the person link**, and **Just me for now** — otherwise the sheet's first screen is a dead end for the exact golfer it was written for.
