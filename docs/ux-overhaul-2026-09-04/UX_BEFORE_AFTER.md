*Written 2026-09-05 against `INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `UX_PRINCIPLES.md` and `DECISIONS_TO_LOG.md`. Repo `/Users/fischbeck3/cup-season` at tip `3bba87e`; read-only but for this folder. Screenshots are the fresh captures in `scratchpad/ux/shots/`; every BEFORE cell carries a screenshot or a `file:line`. Every AFTER string is a proposed string and obeys the voice canon.*

## Spine amendments proposed

**None.** This document proposes no change to the five destinations, the reads, the words or the sequence in `INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Everything below is the same design said as a diff, so an owner can see what actually changes for a golfer. Two clarifications that are *restatements*, not amendments, and are marked where they appear:

1. **§3 Home** presents the thirteen states as five *felt* states, because a golfer does not experience thirteen. The thirteen inputs to the sort are unchanged (IA §4.5).
2. **§16's ten questions** are answered against the IA as written; where an answer depends on an owner ruling (IA §19), the line says which.

**One open question this document surfaces and does not resolve** is listed at the end — D247's starter index, filed as IA §19 item 1. The two it used to carry (the fifth slot, and how the two clients relate) are answered by **R-A** and the revised **R-C**, and the rows are written to those answers.

## 1 · The mental model

| BEFORE | AFTER |
|---|---|
| The user is asked to hold the schema. Six nouns before golf: **league → season → event → match → standings → round**, plus **the Pro**, **bylaws**, **preset**, **structure**, **counting cap**, **participation floor**, **allowance**, **the endgame**. The App Store listing sells them in that order (`docs/ios/app-store-listing.md:21,42-43,47-59`), the wizard's presets recite four dials each (`WizardState.swift:66-72`), and the tab bar itself names a room, not an activity (`MainTabView.swift:128`: `case home, clubhouse, post, you`). | The user holds **five nouns and never meets the sixth** (IA §2.1): **a round · a season · a moment · a golfer · my record**. *League* survives only as the crew's standing name — "the Fellas" — never a button, never a tab, never a thing you join. You join a season. The backend keeps League → Season → Event → Match behind a wall, and every screen crosses it through a named producer (UX_PRINCIPLES P-7). The one-paragraph pitch a golfer could say in a parking lot: *"Cup Season turns the golf you already play into a season with your friends. You add a round — course, score, done — and it posts to your rounds, into your friends' feeds, and onto whatever you have going with them."* |

> **Persona A, reading the store listing before he ever opened the app:** *"'The Pro sets the bylaws once — squads or solo, the handicap allowance, how many rounds count a month, the endgame — and locks them at first tee.' — the Pro? bylaws? allowance? endgame? Four words I don't own, one sentence."*

**Metric:** time-to-aha · activation. The mapping table (IA §2.2) is what makes this checkable rather than aspirational: the terminology lint (IA §15.5) fails the push if a schema noun reaches a user surface.

## 2 · Navigation

| BEFORE | AFTER |
|---|---|
| Four slots: **Home · Clubhouse · ⊕ Post · You** (`MainTabView.swift:128`, and the bar in `shots/00-launch-default.png`). Two of the four are wrong on their face: the tab called *Post* opens a cover that does not post, and the tab called *Clubhouse* is a room the product's own prose retired from the vocabulary (D11). There is no COMMUNITY destination at all — People is a pushed screen inside two different stacks (`HomeRoute.people`, `YouRoute.people`), which is the whole D178 class of bug. Inside the Clubhouse, a season is a six-segment paged room (`ClubhouseView.swift:45-134`; STANDINGS · BOARD · SCHEDULE · POT · ALBUM · LEAGUE, two of which are doors), and swiping the pager rewrites `store.preferredLeague` (`:110-113`), so a navigation gesture silently changes what Home is about. | Five slots: **Home · Compete · ⊕ Play · Golfers · You** (IA §3, D222). Each answers one of the brief's five questions and is named for the golfer's model, not the backend's: Compete holds every season and moment I am in **as peers, nearest clock first**, with finished ones folded under **FINISHED** and the champion named — never hidden. Golfers is the COMMUNITY destination, promoted from a pushed screen. ⊕ Play is a **verb, not a place** (L-25) and never becomes a list. A season is **one scrolling page**, not a six-segment room (IA §7, D223). `preferredLeague` is demoted to **navigation memory only** (D229) — Home has no open season. |

> **Persona F, one month in, five buddies, no league:** *"Four tabs along the bottom: Home · Clubhouse · ⊕ Post · You."* … and, six screens later, on the calendar: *"This is the most disorienting moment of the walk — two adjacent screens disagree about whether Sunday exists."*

**Metric:** the five first-seconds questions at every open · friend connection (COMMUNITY gets an address). **Cost, stated honestly:** the fifth slot is a level-3 override made four times (O-01, IA §19 q1); if the owner declines it, the fallback is four slots with Golfers as Compete's first segment, and Q4 costs one tap.

## 3 · Home

| BEFORE | AFTER |

In `shots/00-launch-default.png` the largest object on the screen is **`2nd` of 2 — held** at ~130 px, under it a four-line rulebook paragraph about the Cup Final, and the league I *lead* is an 11-pt footnote below the one I am losing (`HomeView.swift:1033-1036`). "— held" is computed against a **Sunday snapshot** (`20260902200000:312-342`), so a Tuesday climb reads *held* and lies about time. The gap names the leader and never the golfer directly above me. The four doors: **Add my round · Start something · Join with a code · Find golfers**. |

## 7 · The season

| BEFORE | AFTER |
|---|---|
| A **league room**: a six-segment strip (STANDINGS · BOARD · SCHEDULE · POT · ALBUM · LEAGUE) inside a paged Clubhouse (`ClubhouseView.swift:45-134`, `shots/02-clubhouse.png`), whose hero is a database record — code chip, phase words, dates, "THE PRO · MARCUS", and a red **Cancel & delete this league** (SV-11, SV-33). The season has **no arc**: nothing anywhere says what has happened over weeks. The rules are ten all-caps rows naming COUNTING CAP, PARTICIPATION FLOOR, STRUCTURE, PRESET, VERIFICATION. A wrapped room contradicts itself — *"Season complete · settled"* over *"Post 2 more rounds this month"* over *"top 2 advance"*. The recap **exists and is good** (`SeasonCeremonyView`, re-openable at `StandingsPane.swift:139`) but Home's wrapped hero names **no champion** — `champion_member_id` is decoded (`Models.swift:50`) and unread (`HomeView.swift:869,980`), so a solo champion is told *"The cup's been lifted."* There is **no member exit on either client**; "Cancel" is Pro-only. | **One scrolling page with a story spine** (IA §7, D223). It opens on a sentence, not a table: *"Galen has led for four straight weeks. He has not been caught since the second Sunday. You are four back with nineteen to play."* → **The season's story →** — a week-by-week page from **R6 `season_story()`**, chosen by a **fixed ladder** over named reads, so the line is learnable rather than a mood, and **rung 7 reaches into history** (R-H): *"You and Galen have not settled a week since the twelfth of August. He is 6–5 up all-time."* — true and computable from `head_to_head`, `my_streaks`, `rivalry_weeks` or `standings_snapshots`, with the fence absolute: nothing invented, nothing inflated, no manufactured stake. When even history finds nothing, **rung 7b** still says *"Week seven of twenty-six. Nothing has moved since Sunday."* Each table row carries **one clause of why**: `▲ held four weeks` · `▲ up one since Sunday · 4 back` · `▼ down one · her best week was 3`. The endgame sits **permanently under the table** in full, with a short clause in the ME strip (the D126(2) split, D235). ## 8 · An event

| BEFORE | AFTER |
|---|---|
| An event is reachable **only from inside a league room** — `EventChips` is mounted at `ClubhouseView.swift:87`, the leagueless Clubhouse draws `LeaguelessDoors` and nothing else (`:137-157`), and **no `?event=` link exists**, so a Ryder has no shareable address. The picker is a menu of schema objects with a dead option and an emoji (SV-36). The room speaks the schema: *duel*, *session*, *W-L-H*, *SERIES LEVEL*, *DEFENDS*, *dead rubbers*. A **Major** is flag-closed in prod while **three Home occasion cards sell it** — a door advertised that does not open (L-44). | **A moment is a page with an identity**, same grammar as a season, different clock (IA §8): *"**THE DESERT SHOWDOWN** · SAT SEP 12 · DESERT MOUNTAIN · 6 PLAYING — Two teams, four sessions, and a jug that has been in Galen's garage since March."* Then **RED 3 — 1 BLUE · Red hold the Ryder** (`event_lineage`), **THIS SESSION · closes Sunday** — *"You v Tash. Neither of you has posted."*, **WHO'S PLAYING**, **WHAT'S ON IT** — *"$50 each · $300 · winner takes 60%"* — and **LAST YEAR · Blue took it 4–2**. > **Persona A, who wanted exactly one Saturday and found the right tool by accident:** *"The 'oh, that's actually cool' moment: the ⊕ cover — 'Play now — score the group … Friends without the app just play; their card is waiting when they want it.' … That is the whole product for a guy like me, and it took fifteen taps of wandering to find it."*

**Metric:** competition creation · competition repeat · empty-state engagement (a golfer in a moment and no season stops being a dead end).

**The seat nobody sat in — a guest on someone's tee sheet.** Unchanged and protected (L-40): no account, added by name and index in the live setup, and at the finish a claim link whose sentence is the best in the product and is kept verbatim — *"NAME — 84 at COURSE, Sat Jul 25. ## 9 · The round — add, score, plan

| BEFORE | AFTER |
|---|---|
| **The cover** (`shots/09-post-cover.png`): three rows and roughly a thousand pixels of dead space (SV-22), with *"counts on your card and in every league"* on the middle row — a T-01 violation on the ⊕'s own cover. **The composer** puts the fields last (SV-23), asks for five things where one would do, and its rating and slope placeholders read as **values** — `72.1` / `128` (`PostRoundScreen.swift:194-195`) — while a blank rating posts 0 into a check constraint. A **hand-typed course cannot post** (`PostCard.swift:238-244`, and the web's twin). The post is the phone's **one consequential direct write** (`PostService`'s raw `db.from("rounds").insert`), which L-03 forbids. There is no way to say **who was out there**. **Live setup** is a 20-concept form with seven eyebrows and no fold (`LiveSetupView.swift:24-184`, `shots/16-live-setup-nearby.png`), and it cannot be set up the night before. After the ceremony, `EpilogueSheet` closes the whole cover with **no next act**. | **⊕ → long-press → type `84` → Add my round.** The composer is **one box** (IA §5.2): the last course pre-filled as a chip, one focused numeric field labelled **YOUR GROSS** (sans, because a focused numeric input is a control — L-29), and everything else inherited and folded into **one editable line**: `Blue · 71.2 / 128 · today ⌄`. Plus one optional row: **Who was out there? ( Galen ✓ ) ( + )** (C-2 `round_players` + `confirm_round_partner`, a claim with a **state**, and **a tag is never a vouch** — L-19). The placeholders become `—`. A hand-typed course mints a `course_key`; a round with no rating **posts to your rounds and does not earn season points, and the receipt says so**. The direct write becomes **R11 `post_round()`**. Then the epilogue becomes **a page with exactly one ranked next act** (IA §5.4) — eight rungs, eight distinct sentences, one door each, of which the funnel rung is: *"Galen was out there too. Four rounds between you this month — four is a season."* → **Start a season with Galen →**. *"Make the next one count"* appears in **exactly one row**, so it means one thing (P-3). Live setup gets the composer's treatment: **Who · Where · What are we playing** above the fold; strokes, stakes, guests and Bluetooth below it. |
