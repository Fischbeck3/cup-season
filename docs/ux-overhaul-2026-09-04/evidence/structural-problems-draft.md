## SP-1 · The league season is the product's only unit of engagement

**Statement.** Every story, notification, reaction, invite link and head-to-head record in Cup Season is keyed to membership in a league season (or an event); a golfer without one — 14 of 39 profiles today, every member between seasons, and every event-only player — opens an app that can show them a friend's round but cannot let them react to it, invite anyone, be told anything, or have their own round go anywhere. The brief's funnel (casual play → competition → recurring competition) has no first rung, because the product only begins at rung two.

**Hierarchy level.** Mechanics (the schema's homing rule and the push fan-out), leaking upward into the IA (Home and the Clubhouse are both dispatched by membership).

**Evidence.**
- `posts_home_check` (`20260716160000:27-28`): a post must be homed in a league or an event — never on a person. A leagueless golfer's round produces no post anywhere; 26 of 212 rounds have no `v_rounds_ranked` row (DL-18, DL F1). `round_to_board` (`20260727160000:61-70`) writes one post per league whose season window holds the date; `round_moments` inserts only via `league_members` (`20260716020000:192-198`); `finish_live_round` skips the board when `league_id` is null (`20260829090000:38-39`).
- Push recipients are `league_members` minus author (`push/index.ts:485-521`); no buddy fan-out exists, so a buddy's round appears in a leagueless golfer's Home feed and never on their lock screen (EN-02, DL-12). The 14 leagueless profiles can only ever be notified by another person acting on them.
- Reactions render only when the round has a `posts` row in a league the viewer shares (`HomeSocial.swift:5-10, 65-71`; `HomeView.swift:502-511`) — a buddies-only circle gets no reaction strip and no explanation (HM-11). Persona F: "I could see Dev's best round but couldn't even fire-emoji it."
- The only invite link in the product is a league's: `PeopleScreen.swift:112-129` renders it only when a membership carries a code; `PeoplePickerSheet.swift:50-60` likewise; a leagueless golfer who searches and finds nobody gets a sentence and no door (YP-01, FR-26, WB-24).
- Head-to-head exists only inside a shared season: `my_rivalries` (`20260716210000:76-83`), `rivalry_weeks`, `tour_card.vs_you`; `create_forfeit` is crew-only (`20260724120000:60-70`); 6 of 12 friendships share no league (DL-09, DL-20, DL-28).
- The leagueless Home hero is not a button and carries no CTA (`HomeView.swift:801-813, 951-952`): "Your card · 0 of 3 · … Nothing else needed." / "Established. Nobody's seen it yet — you haven't joined a league." (HM-02, FR-01, EN-23). The web's twin has "Post your first round" / "Find your buddies" / "Start a league" (`index.html:11448-11474`) — the phone has no rung 5 at all (`Models.swift:305-310`, HM-10).
- Events are decoded and never rendered on Home (`Models.swift:233-234`; `native_home.sql:398-456`) — an event-only golfer is told "you haven't joined a league" (HM-03). A wrapped league beside a live one vanishes (`Models.swift:300-303`, HM-26); the wrapped hero says "Run it back." with no door (`HomeView.swift:981`, HM-07).
- The leagueless Clubhouse is three doors and "Add golfers" under an empty title (`ClubhouseView.swift:137-157`; CH-35, FR-14). Persona F: "An empty room with a sign that says build one … this tab has nothing for me."
- Prod: 14/39 no league; 27/39 no buddy; 11 carded golfers with no league, 10 of them with no round; `home_hero_state rung7` painted 12×, its CTA tapped 2× ever (web); 14 rounds in 21 days product-wide.
- Persona B (member between seasons): "It's a tombstone with a feed under it … Would I open it again tomorrow? No." Persona A: "Where did the three doors go? … My goal isn't on this screen anywhere." Persona D: "the screen thinks I'm here to build a handicap, not run a league."
- Reader ids: HM-01, HM-02, HM-03, HM-10, HM-11, HM-26, HM-07; DL-01, DL-03, DL-12, DL-18, DL-20, DL-28; EN-02, EN-23, EN-24; YP-01; FR-01, FR-14, FR-16, FR-26; CH-35; CJ-44; CC-21; PA §3.6 (D81/D94/D121 superseded).

**Root cause across the hierarchy.** Vision: "Cup Season exists to make every round of golf matter because it belongs to a season" (`product-vision-v1.0.md:14-15`) — the season is the vision's unit, and everything below inherited it. IA: the blueprint's "Where do I stand?" became one league's room (D93, IOS-002 §5, D203) and Home's hero "the open league" (D81/D94/D121). Mechanics: `posts_home_check`, `round_to_board`, `round_moments`, the push webhook, `my_rivalries`, `create_forfeit`, `post_kudos`-via-league-post, and the league-only invite code all key on `league_members`/season windows; D80 explicitly defined buddies as "nothing to do with leagues or points" (`GuideCopy.swift:75`). UI: `HomeMode.of` returns rung 7/6 by `rounds_count` alone (`Models.swift:305-310`); doors are league nouns. Implementation: `store.preferredLeague` is the lens for feed, You-this-season and standings (D123, CC-13).

**Why structural (what a copy fix would not solve).** Rewording "Nobody's seen it yet" or adding a button to the rung-7 hero does not give a leagueless round a home, a friend's round a push, a buddy's card a 🔥, or a golfer a link to send. Those need: a story home outside leagues (a profile-homed post or a synthesised `home_stories()` over `achievements` + `home_feed`, DL C-4/B-3), push fan-out by friendship (EN-02), reactions keyed to the round rather than a league post (HM-11), a person-level invite (YP-01, D177's "would need a decision"), and an IA in which ME/NOW/COMMUNITY are computed independently of membership. Each is a migration or an RPC plus a decision-log entry; none is a string.

**Costs.** Activation (the first-week golfer has nothing to do but wait for three rounds), time-to-aha (the aha — a friend seeing your round — cannot fire without a league), friend connection (no link to send), WAU/MAU and D7/D30 for 14 of 39 accounts and every member between seasons, empty-state engagement (the empties are sentences, not doors), season repeat (the wrapped state is a tombstone).

## SP-4 · The social graph changes nothing competitive — "I want to beat Jake" has no object
