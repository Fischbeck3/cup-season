# Completeness critic — what the UX audit did not open, only inferred, or never rendered

Read-only pass at tip `3bba87e`, 2026-09-04. Inputs: `docs/ux-overhaul-2026-09-04/UX_AUDIT.md` (1,353 lines), the twelve reader reports, the six persona walks, the synth/verify files, and the code and docs named below. Every line number is as of tip. **SAW** = read in code/doc/screenshot or returned by `supabase db query --linked`; **INFER** = concluded without running it.

Prod facts read for this pass (read-only, 2026-09-04): 13 leagues — `squads2/setup` 6 (the one-member "My Cup"s) · `squads2/season` 2 · `solo/season` 3 · `squads4/season` 1 · `squads4/complete` 1 (the Sandbox); **every league's `draft_type` is `random`**; `league_cancellations` 0 and `cancellation_votes` 0 ever; `events` = 1 Ryder complete; **0 profiles are in an event without a league**; 11 profiles hold 2+ leagues; `device_tokens` = 1 `ios-sandbox`.

## 0. Verdict

The audit is wide (≈580 findings, 68 empty states, 21 notification kinds, a six-mode Home matrix) and honest about its limits (§0.4, Appendix B). Its blind spots cluster in four places:

1. **Persona situations it never walked** — the multi-league golfer (the most common real state), the squads member and captain (the listing's first sentence), the Pro in season, the guest, the event-only golfer, and the App Review account that gates shipping. All six personas are single-league or league-less and every league they touch is solo.
2. **Rooms it described only in their first stage** — draft night, the Ryder in LIVE/COMPLETE, the Major in any stage, the finish/settlement, the ceremony, the cancel vote. The audit's central structural claim (the Ryder engine is the "challenge" primitive, CJ-02/DL-01/CC-11) rests on a room nobody looked at past FORMING.
3. **Rendering conditions it did not vary** — the light theme (treated as a capture anomaly, SV-01, though it is a shipped first-class setting and half the personas "saw" it), Dynamic Type/VoiceOver (deferred to a doc that itself excludes the Home hero and the Clubhouse), the web at desktop width, the phone with the app closed (push, Live Activity), offline.
4. **Surfaces outside the four tabs** — the widget target (0 mentions; it is a Live Activity only, and the absence of a Home Screen widget is a design fact the ME/NOW hierarchy needs), the App Store listing as a screen (no §2 row), the OTP email (the second screen of every first run, not in the repo), the sandbox (D65 — the owner's own instrument for forcing the phases Appendix B says were never seen).

Twenty-six gaps follow, ranked by how blind a redesign decision would be without them. Each says what it is, what the audit has, why it matters, and the cheapest way to close it (file to read, `-cs_dev_*` hatch, or state to force). §2 is the persona × situation matrix; §3 lists the hatches the audit needed and does not have.
