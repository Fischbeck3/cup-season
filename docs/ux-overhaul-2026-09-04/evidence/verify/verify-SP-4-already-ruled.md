## Verdict

**HOLDS.** Nothing in D111–D221, IOS-006…027, or the prior plan rules a friend-to-friend competition object, a head-to-head outside a shared league season, a friends ranking, or a stake between two golfers who share no league. Prod at tip: 0 tables and 0 functions matching `challenge|callout|head_to_head|friends_board`; `rivalry_names` 0 · `forfeits` 0 · `week_clashes` 8 with 0 winners · `game_results` 0 · 12 accepted friendships.

But the statement is under-cited and in four places wrong about *why* things are the way they are. The ground it stands on was ruled — mostly BEFORE D111 — and several of those rulings are deliberately the opposite of what the statement treats as an oversight. The design phase must cite them by number:

- **RULED, NOT BUILT** — the design phase's starting points: **D21** (the Callout, the ruled pair-wise object, never built), **D64**'s rivalry-facet archive and auto-hook (built league-scoped, the archive never wired), **D52/D108**'s "result feeds the faceted rivalry record" (ruled unnecessary "by construction" in the migration, and that construction has since drifted), **D93**'s cross-league schedule (built as a place, but both clients kept **D38**'s room filter, which is why persona F's calendar hid Tash), prior-plan **T5-12** (head-to-head where the rival is named, on the hero/standings — unbuilt on both clients), **D131**'s "forfeit" noun on the Pot sheet (unbuilt on the phone).
- **RULED AND BUILT the other way** — the statement must stop calling these gaps: **D69** (RSVP is for the invited — a deliberate reversal, with a stated rationale), **D19** (a rivalry can be named only with history — its own rule, with a misuse valve), **D12** (retired "duel" as a *user* noun only; the mechanic stands, and "Challenge" would be a fourth h2h noun), **IOS-008** (rejected a generic "next opponent" slot on Home as "a mechanic the product rejected"), **D17** ("I'm in" — built on both clients, but reachable only from a watch list that excludes pure buddies), **D63** (Last Round With — the one relationship-first, league-free mechanic that exists; not competitive).
- **UNRULED, and named as such in the log itself:** D177 ("a buddy-invite link is a different mechanic and would need a decision, not a tidy"); D150 (deferred "who else played here" as wanting "their own decision"); IOS-008 parked D52 "as a question, not designed around". Friends ranking (CC-10, DL C-9), the casual-meeting rule (DL C-2), a challenge push kind, and a COMPETE surface not gated on membership have no entry anywhere.
