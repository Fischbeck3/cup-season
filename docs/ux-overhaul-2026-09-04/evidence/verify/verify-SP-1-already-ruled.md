# Verify · SP-1 · lens "already-ruled" — 2026-09-04, tip 3bba87e

**Verdict: HOLDS.** SP-1 is neither fixed nor ruled as a whole. No entry in D111–D221, IOS-006–026 or plan t1–t7 gives a leagueless golfer (or a member between seasons) a story home, a round-push, a reaction on a buddy-only round, or a person-level invite. But the evidence list is three different things, and the statement overstates one of them:

- **(A) Ruled, built as ruled, and the ruling itself deferred the gap** — D28 (reactions through a shared-league post; round-scoped reactions "built when a real user hits the gap"), D107 §5 (no league → no board; "the share card is the story — accepted"), D177 (a buddy-invite link "would need a decision"), D122 (the leagueless phrase "join a league to score it"). These are the doors the design phase must reopen BY NAME; they are not undiscovered gaps.
- **(B) Ruled and NOT built on the phone** — the D81/D94/D119(4)/D151 leagueless ladder with its CTAs and rung 5; IOS-008(2) events on Home. Still a problem; the design phase must cite the ruling rather than propose it again.
- **(C) Never ruled** — push fan-out by friendship, a story home for a TYPED leagueless round, a friend-scope competition/rivalry/pride-bet object, the wrapped league vanishing beside a live one. These need decision-log entries, which is what SP-1 says.

One evidence bullet is partly wrong (HM-07: the run-back door exists), and the statement's "cannot invite anyone, be told anything" is too absolute — the buddy graph DOES carry three push kinds and two invitation rails today. Corrections in §5.

## 5 · Corrections to the statement and evidence (it still holds)

1. **"cannot react to it, invite anyone, be told anything" is too absolute.** A leagueless golfer today CAN: send a buddy request that pushes (`friend_request` → `push_nudges` kind `request`, `20260827210000:119-121`, category `CS_REQUEST`); put a buddy on the tee sheet with an In/Out push (`declare_round` `20260831170000:79-87` tags `friendships` OR league-mates, `:112-121` kind `rsvp`; `DeclareRoundSheet.swift:3` "Works league-less"; D219 built); seat a buddy in a live round with a push (`start_live_round` `20260829090000:162-170`, D107 §2); and hand a non-user a claim link (D87/D107; `GuideCopy.swift:77`). Persona F's own Q3 row (`persona-F…:221`) notes "can't say I'm in on Tash's round" — that is the RSVP path not being offered on the card, not its absence. **Rewrite the statement as the narrower, sharper claim:** a buddy's *posted round* — the aha — has no push, no reaction and no story home outside a league; there is no person-level invite LINK; there is no friend-scope competition object. The plumbing for "be told" exists (three kinds); the thing it never says is the round.
2. **HM-07 must be dropped or reworded.** The run-back door exists on the phone (`StandingsPane.swift:140`, `SeasonCeremonyView.swift:67`); the hero's own door lands on that pane. Keep only: the hero's door word ("See the table →") does not say it, and `LeaguelessDoors.swift:23-25`'s `RunItBackCard` is dead code.
3. **"leagueless hero is not a button and carries no CTA" — say "the hero"; Home itself has the `+` Menu** (`HomeView.swift:178-190`: Start a league / Start an event / Join with a code / calendar / Find golfers) and a date-windowed `OccasionCard` (`:84-92`; `HomeStream.swift:213` a leagueless-only "Start a league" occasion; dark today per EN-24). Persona A's "three doors" are on the Clubhouse tab and the orientation, not Home. Cite **D94 + D119 rec (4)** as the ruling that the doors should LEAD the leagueless Home — ruled, unbuilt on the phone — instead of arguing the point from first principles.
4. **Cite the rulings that already anticipated the gap, and their own reopen clauses:** D28 `:644-648` (trigger: a real user tries to react to a friend-only round — Persona F), D107 `:4031-4032` (owner-accepted tradeoff, live rounds only), D177 `:4941` (needs a decision), D168 `:4862-4863` (push invitation PROPOSED, two owner questions). The design phase reopens these by number; it does not discover them.
5. **Line refs to update:** `round_moments` latest body is `20260722100000:140-143` (the cited `20260716020000:192-198` is superseded); `finish_live_round` latest is `20260830220000:261-263` (`20260829090000:38-39` is a header comment); `create_forfeit` latest body `20260727160000:343-…` keeps the same `is_league_member` gate.

7. **Add IOS-008(2)** to the HM-03 bullet as the ruling (events on Home when a Ryder session is open — ruled, unbuilt), and mark HM-26 as unruled.
