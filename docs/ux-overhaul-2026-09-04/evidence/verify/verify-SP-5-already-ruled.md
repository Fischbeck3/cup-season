Restate as: "a hand-typed course cannot be posted without also knowing its rating and slope, and the phone neither says so nor disables Post before the server refuses (Q-22 unported)."
3. **"3 taps from a leagueless Home to the form"** — from any tab it is ⊕ (1) → "Post a round — after you play" (2) to the form. Restate as two taps to the form; keep the 6–10-tap submit count.

5. The "18-hole total in Front 9 posts a nine at half value" line reads as a bug; it is D34+D72 as ruled with the fine print stating it (`PostRoundScreen.swift:269`). Keep it as a consequence of the ruling to amend.
