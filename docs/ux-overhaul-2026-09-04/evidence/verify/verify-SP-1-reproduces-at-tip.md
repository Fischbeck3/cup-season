## Corrections (what the statement overstates or mis-cites)

1. **"cannot … be told anything" is too strong.** Three pushes are keyed to friendship, not league: buddy request (`friend_request` → `push_nudges` kind `request`, 20260827210000_push_wave7.sql:92-99; routed push/index.ts:440-442), buddy accept (push/index.ts:411-419 "is in your crew"), and tee-sheet In/Out to tagged buddies (`declare_round` 20260902203000:127-137; buddies are taggable per :188-191; routed push/index.ts:443-445). Precise claim: **no push about a buddy's round, movement or milestone** — only relationship and booking pushes.
2. **"cannot invite anyone / has nothing to send" is too strong.** A leagueless golfer can send: a public round page (`create_share('round')` 20260722190000:42-58; EpilogueSheet.swift:66-74), the live pencil link (LivePlayView.swift:492), a claim link (PostService.swift:195-197; GuideCopy.swift:77 "No league, no buddy — just the round"), and an in-app buddy request to an existing account (PeoplePickerSheet `.befriend`). What does not exist is a link that lands a **newcomer** in the sender's circle — the only account-to-account link is a league's (PeopleScreen.swift:105-107; index.html:15260). Reword to that.
3. **"cannot have their own round go anywhere" is wrong in-app.** `home_feed` unions accepted friendships into the circle (20260723090000:24-27) and `can_see_round` admits buddies (20260718192400:31-33), so the round lands in every buddy's Home feed — silently (no push), without a post row, un-reactable. Persona F saw Dev's round for exactly this reason. Reword: "goes to buddies' feeds only; no post, no push, no reaction."
4. **"every event-only player" cannot be told anything is wrong.** Event board posts push to `event_players` (push/index.ts:464-483) and posts can be event-homed. The event-only gap is on **Home** (HM-03: `events`/`open_duels` never rendered), not in push.
