## 2. Why it still holds

The fence is a principle-level ruling (D23, level 2 memory-layer guardrail, restated in D61's "threshold, never schedule") whose escape hatch — "a Year-2 decision with its own entry" — was never taken. D104 built the rail and the ask; D204/D176/D207 built day-of and last-day *league-wide, third-person* system posts. The between-opens loop for a leagueless golfer is still empty (recipients are `league_members`, `push/index.ts:485-521`; 14 of 39 profiles leagueless), a multi-league golfer still hears "August is in the books" once per league (6 such posts on 2026-09-01 in prod), and the rail has never delivered to a production build.
