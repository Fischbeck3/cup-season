## 11 · Verdict on the goal question

*"Where do I stand and what do I need to do next?" — can I answer it instantly on the first screen?*

**Where I stand: yes, instantly — 10/10 for that half.** "3rd of 8 · 12 back of Tommy · 4 back of 2nd", with the season week, the month's count and the pot, is the best first screen of any league app I've used.

**What I need to do next: no.** The first screen states every fact I need and turns none of them into an instruction. Posting a round is a tab, not a sentence. Paying is "ask the Pro" with no Pro named. Passing Dre is arithmetic I had to do after a five-tap dig for the scale. The one thing that worked like a normal app — Saturday's RSVP — took two taps and felt great.

**Score: 6 / 10.** Tap count to close every sub-question: 0 (stand) + 1 (find Dre) + 5 (learn what beating him takes) + 2 (money, then give up and text) + 2 (Saturday) + 0 (ending, two reads) = **10 taps and one leave-the-app**, for something a single "Next" line on the hero could have done in zero.

## 12 · The "oh, that's actually cool" moment

**The Climb.** Tapping the hero lands me on a table built around *me*: Tommy's row in gold, "Dre Wilson · 4 ahead of you", a line saying "CUT LINE · 4 BACK", my row highlighted with a sparkline of my season, "Kev Lam, 6 behind you", "··· 4 more ···". Then tapping Dre's name shows the six rounds that made his 76, with the ones that got bumped greyed out. That is exactly the "standings that show their work" the store listing promised, and it's the only place in the app that talks to me like a competitor. Runner-up: the Coming-up card with "WITH YOU", "2 in", the weather, and an "I'm in" button one tap away.
