## 0. The headline

The prior audit's diagnosis ("~120 product terms, almost none defined at first contact, several used in two or three senses") was ruled on in D111–D139 and a real fraction was built: one stage vocabulary with a lint (D120, `tests/preflight.mjs` check 20), one band producer (`CSBands`), the D1 phrase form on Home and the feed, "differential" and "Pro Shop" and "the pilot" gone, "Leagues & events" renamed (D208), the endgame said as a sentence on Home (D126), the `IN` badge (D136), marker and GHIN defined on the card gate, the ledger line as one constant (D201).

What is still true at tip, and what this report is about:

1. **Five noun collisions the log already ruled on are still shipping** — *card* (D131), *stake/the books* (D131), *tee sheet* (D131), *cup points* (D131), and *the Pro undefined at first contact* (D132). None of D131's assignments and none of D132's three definitions are in the phone binary. The join covenant (D115) and the D133 live how-tos are likewise unbuilt on the phone. These are not new findings; they are ruled findings with no build behind them.
2. **The engine's vocabulary still reaches the user where the log said it would not**: `HANDICAP ALLOWANCE 95%` and `COUNTING CAP` on the bylaws (D51/D128), `SEEDS LOCKED · A CUP SEED · EVERYONE ADVANCES — 2 CONTENDERS, 2 SEATS · CUT LINE` on the climb (D126), `LIVE NOW — CAPTAINS READY` and `The Pro has the list.` (D120), `THE HAT SHUFFLES SERVER-SIDE`, `One Pro-approved bye month` (D14), `Attested` ×8 (D13), `vs index` on the individual race and receipts (D209/D210).
3. **The two-level model the brief wants hidden — League → Season — is the UI's own grammar**: "Start a league" · "Season length" · "Run it back — Season 2" · "SEASON II" · "your league season is live" · "Leagues & events" · "league mate". A first-timer is asked to hold both nouns before the first round.
4. **The product's four objects (Crew · Cup · Rivalry · the Record, brand canon §1) are not the product's four nouns.** The UI's nouns are League · Season · Event · Squad · Board · Pot · Card · Tee sheet — the database's. The Record is never a place; Crew is a slogan word; Rivalry is a You section; Cup is the app's name and its least-defined word (still).
5. **The app has three door-names for its smallest useful action.** The tab says **Post**, the cover it opens says **Golf**, and the cover's first row is **Play now — score the group**; "Post a round — after you play" is the second row. The brief's "Add My Round must be trivial" runs through that.

Total findings: **59** (0 P0 · 12 P1 · 34 P2 · 13 P3). No term *blocks* a goal on its own; the two nearest to P0 are the money-vs-never-money "stake" on one pane (TM-05) and "ask the Pro how to pay" for a member who was never told what a Pro is (TM-02).
