**Who I am.** Dana Whitfield, 44, Tempe. I was a member (not the organiser) of an
8-person league on Cup Season called **Dew Sweepers**. The season ran 13 weeks, Mon

**When.** Tuesday evening, Sep 1, 2026, about 7:40pm, on the couch. I last opened

ceremony. I have had no notifications from it since.

**My goal.** *"I opened CupSeason. What should I do?"* — is there something to see,
