In beta with the founding league

The iPhone app is being tested by the founder's own league. When the public beta opens, its invitation link appears here; when Apple approves the listing, this becomes the App Store button. Until then the web app above is the same product on the same account.

Somebody sent you a link?

Install the app, then open the link again. A link to a round, a card or a season opens on the web first. Once the app is on your phone, opening the same link from the message lands inside the app. The app does not remember a link you opened before it was installed — keep the message it came in and open it once more.

No app yet? The link works on the web as it is. Sign in on that page and what you opened is waiting for you after the code.
