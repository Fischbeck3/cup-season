# Cup Season — where the app is on 1 October 2026, and on 1 October 2027

**Written 2026-09-20**, eleven days before a Thursday launch date the owner named
as a planning exercise. This is a companion to `product-vision-v1.0.md` (the
founding text and the 2026-09-12 expansion draft stand unchanged), `gtm-year1.md`
(the funnel and the four quarters), and `docs/pilot/` on
`claude/pilot-readiness-2026-09-15` (the staged pilot and its gates). It does
not replace any of them. It answers two questions those documents leave open:
*what, exactly, is live on launch day* and *what does one good year look like*.
The readiness metric and the work list to get there are in
`launch-readiness-2026-10-01.md`.

The promise has not moved: **Cup Season is where amateur golf counts.** The
expansion draft's line — *the golf you play with your people becomes a season
worth playing and a record worth keeping* — is the sentence this document
builds toward.

## 1 · October 1, 2026 — launch day, said honestly

### What "live" means that morning

Launch on October 1 is the **start of the staged launch**, not a cold public
opening. The pilot's own gates (`docs/pilot/gates-and-stop-conditions.md`) put a
capped public cohort three to five weeks past a Friends build that passes the
two-phone checks; a public door on October 1 would violate two ratified gates.
So the honest picture of the day is Stage B in flight and Stage C beginning:

| Layer | State on the morning of October 1 |
|---|---|
| **cupseason.app** | Serves the tip of what is now PR #6 — the pilot-readiness work, F1–F13, the note door, the counting explanation. The stamp in the sign-in caption matches `git log origin/main`. Signed-out visitors meet the door and the demo diorama; a claim link or invite link lands them in the right place. |
| **Database** | 248 migrations applied, the two pending revokes included; `deploy-status` reports every layer clean. |
| **iPhone** | A TestFlight build from the same commit is in the **Friends** external group, past Beta App Review. The Owner group holds the same build. App Store public listing is **not** live; the store is a Stage E decision. |
| **Who is in** | PIGL, the Friends group, and the first independent groups — three to five, Phoenix-area, groups the founder does not play in. Every league created before the paid offer exists is a **Founding League**, free forever. |
| **Money** | Nothing is charged (D183: free until 1,000 onboarded golfers). Stripe stays parked. The pot is a ledger between friends; the legal page and the product say that in one voice. |
| **Support** | One person, with the session log, the weekly review and the read-only scorecard. Every assisted session is logged so it can be subtracted from the numbers. |

### What a golfer can do on day one

The loop that exists today, current on every surface:

- **Get in without the founder.** Open an invite link or a code, read the
  covenant at any stake including $0, join, and be seated the way the door
  seats you (D348, D351, D361-era fixes).
- **Post a real round in under a minute** — two boxes, front and back — and be
  told, before posting, what the round can add (*worth up to 12 · your best 4
  count and you have 3*) and after posting what it did, once (D362). Every
  points figure opens the rounds that produced it (§16).
- **Play live** with the people on the tee sheet: Match Play, Wolf, Skins, from
  a booking or spontaneously, offline-safe by write clock, one round per
  booking however many times start is pressed (D350, D367). An account-less
  guest gets a claim link that shows only name, gross, course and date.
- **Watch a season move**: standings that show their work, the month meter,
  the counting cap, floors waived in edge months, auto-bye, month close on the
  1st, the endgame dial — Cup Final or the points table — trophies and the
  settlement post.
- **Run The Ryder** as a standalone event with sessions that open and resolve
  on the tick.
- **Keep a record**: rounds, seasons, trophies, courses, optional photographs;
  applause as the one appreciation act (D365).

### What it deliberately is not yet

Said out loud so nobody sells it: no payments; no captains-pick or snake draft
engines (the wizard shows the two built structures); email invitations are
written to a table and not sent; season two does not yet re-ask for consent
(`run_it_back` is unpushed by decision); an unclaimable guest link still dead-
ends on the signed-out door; no spreadsheet import; no Android native app (the
web is the Android app); no crash pipeline beyond `client_events`.

### The bar the day has to clear

From the gates, not from a feeling after a good round:

1. Zero integrity non-zeros: no duplicate live round per booking, no double-
   posted card, no acknowledged score that failed to land.
2. Every row of the two-phone checklist PASS on the build Friends receive.
3. Every finish posts every registered seat; every account-less guest either
   claims or is told how.
4. Assisted sessions do not outnumber unassisted completions in any cohort that
   is meant to be unassisted.

### What we measure from the first day

The GTM funnel, read through the pilot scorecard by cohort: invitation opened →
joined → first counting round; live game started → finished → every seat
posted → guests claimed; leagues locked → first month closed → season
completed. Counts, not percentages, until the sample passes about ten.
Retention is already the strongest signal in production — every golfer who
ever posted a round posted again (21 of 21 on 2026-09-16). The launch exists to
prove people can *get in* without the founder in the room.

## 2 · October 1, 2027 — one year on

The GTM plan maps the year to the golf calendar: beachhead and instrument,
ambassadors and content, referral and revenue proof, retention and renewal.
This is the product and business that plan describes, if the gates keep
passing.

### The product

**The complete week, on both clients, unremarkable.** A golfer books Saturday,
the people who said IN are seated when the host tees it up, scores land
offline, the finish posts every card, the eligible past plan asks quietly
whether it was played, and the round explains what it changed. None of this
needs an open app during golf. This is horizon one of the expansion draft,
finished and boring — the highest compliment.

**The Record as chapters.** A season reads as a chapter: the rounds that
counted, the month that turned it, the clash that decided it, the trophy and
the settlement, with honest coverage where facts are thin. A round can be
revisited through a person, a course or a season. "Season Wrapped" exists as
the Q4 organic moment, built from the chapter, not instead of it. This is
horizon two, entered only once the week loop held.

**Traditions that travel.** Run-it-back is the covenant again, with last
season's result on it — *you finished 3rd of 8; same stake, same rules; in?* —
recorded per member per season. A guest who played one Skins game can be
brought into a league by the person who seated them. Email is either a real
channel with a consumer of `invites`, or the table is gone; the decision was
made, not deferred. This is horizon three, and it is what makes year two of a
league cheaper than year one.

**Formats where a real group asked.** Captains-pick and snake draft only if an
actual league chose them at lock; the Ryder and a Major as event add-ons a
Pro can run in the off-season. No new format is drawn ahead of a group.

**Two clients, one product, as ruled.** The phone at the turn, the desk at the
desk (D234). The App Store listing is public, with account deletion, the
privacy page and the pot disclaimer where App Review looks. The web remains
the Android app; a native Android build is not on the year's list unless the
funnel says a metro needs it.

**Course data that is boringly right.** The course cache refreshes atomically,
malformed provider numbers are coerced rather than fatal, a course the API does
not have can be entered once and is remembered (the 2026-09-09 inbox finding,
closed), and a plan never fabricates a scorecard.

### The business

- **Active Recurring Leagues is the one number.** The plan's arc is roughly 25
  by early spring, 100 by Masters season, 300 by late summer, with the weakest
  funnel gate named each week. Those are the plan's targets, not forecasts;
  the year is judged on whether the gates were honest, not on hitting a round
  number.
- **Season-two renewal is the number that decides the model.** Leagues that
  started in the launch window are finishing by summer 2027; whether they run
  it back — and whether they do so without a call from the founder — is the
  read that makes or breaks a paid year two.
- **The paid offer has been put to real Pros.** The unit is the league-year,
  paid by the Pro from the pot (D101: $59 for nine or fewer, $89 for ten to
  thirteen, $109 for fourteen or more; first year free), and D183 keeps
  everything free until 1,000 onboarded golfers — 33 today — after which
  D135's eight-item evidence checklist decides the flip. Before any price is
  printed it is put to at least five organizers with the interview guide, and
  the guide quotes D101's numbers, not July's. Founding Leagues stay free
  forever; individual identity and handicap stay free forever; live games and
  the guest-claim funnel stay free because they are the acquisition.
- **One metro owned, a second opened.** Phoenix/Scottsdale standing buddy
  leagues first; a second year-round metro or a spring cold-climate metro when
  the first shows renewal.
- **Ambassador Pros carry onboarding.** The white-glove call is codified into
  an in-app checklist; the Pros who ran good seasons recruit the next.
- **Ops is still small and still honest.** The scorecard, the session log and
  `deploy-status` are the operating system; a crash pipeline exists; the
  support load per completed round is falling and is measured.

### How we will know

| Measure | Read where | What "good" looks like a year on |
|---|---|---|
| Unassisted completion (invite → first counting round; start → finish → every seat posted) | pilot scorecard by cohort | Rising through every cohort; assisted sessions per completed round falling |
| Active Recurring Leagues | `gtm-year1.md` §1 | On the plan's arc, with the weakest gate named weekly |
| Season completion, then season-two renewal | season story, `run_it_back` records | The renewal number exists and was earned without founder calls |
| Referred leagues | growth events | A Pro or golfer starting a second league; the plan wants k approaching 1 by Q4 |
| Claim-link conversion | claim walkthrough events | Finished rounds mint cards; cards convert; unfinished rounds stop minting dead links |
| The friction targets | timed tasks with real testers | Profile under 2 min · join under 30 s · post under 60 s · standings understood in 10 s |
| Integrity | scorecard `integrity` | All zeros, or every non-zero explained the same week |

### What stays out

The boundaries the vision already set hold for the whole year: no GPS, swing
coaching, shot tracking, synthetic competition, engagement bait or a generic
feed; no public course-review marketplace; no reselling of the handicap index;
no clubs/B2B until two real leagues ask to play each other; no paid
acquisition beyond a warm retargeting test. A quiet week may stay quiet.

| When | Stage | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|
| **Sep 20 – Oct 1, 2026** | A → B | PR #6 reviewed and merged; the two revokes pushed; a fresh build through Beta App Review to Friends; the two-phone checklist PASS; cohorts named; task sheets sent. |
| **Oct – Nov 2026** | B → C | Friends complete two rounds each, the second unassisted; three to five independent groups recruited; one competition group toward a lock; the unclaimable-link dead end given words or prevented. |
| **Dec 2026 – Feb 2027** | C → D | Three independent groups complete a second game unassisted; a full competition lifecycle closes and settles for a group the founder is not in; season-two consent decided and built before the first PIGL-style renewal. |
| **Mar – May 2027** | D → E | The paid offer interviewed with five organizers; the capped founding cohort opened; App Store public; Masters-timed "start your season". |
| **Jun – Sep 2027** | E | Referral mechanics; renewal read on the launch cohort; second metro; "Season Wrapped" built for the first season endings. |

Dates are the golf calendar's, not delivery promises. A gate that fails moves
the row, not the bar.

## 4 · What decides a conflict

The hierarchy of truth stands: vision → principles → IA → mechanics → UI →
implementation; a lower level never silently contradicts a higher one, and
every mechanic change is logged before it is built. The five principles do the
work — golf first, low friction wins, real golf, memory over statistics, the
app should feel alive — and the Cup Season Test closes the argument: *if this
disappeared tomorrow, would golfers miss it because it made their golf life
richer?* When the launch date and a gate disagree, the gate wins; the date is a
name for a week, and the gates are what the year is judged on.
