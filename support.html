Get help

Cup Season is built and run by one golfer in Tempe, Arizona. Write to jerecho@fischbeck3.com and say what you were trying to do, which phone you were on, and roughly when. You will hear back within a day.

If a score did not land, a round posted twice, or you can see something that is not yours, say so first — those are the things we drop everything for.

Install the app

Everything you need is at cupseason.app/get: the iPhone app, and the web app for any other phone.

If somebody sent you a link — to a round, a card or a season — install the app, then open the link again. The link you were sent opens on the web first; once the app is on your phone, opening the same link lands inside the app. The app does not remember a link you opened before it was installed, so keep the message it came in.

A link that did not open

  • It opened in the browser instead of the app. That is expected before the app is installed. Install it, then open the link again from the message.
  • It says the round was never finished. There is no card to keep — whoever ran the round can tee off again and send you a new link from that round.
  • It says the card was already claimed. Your card is on a record already. Sign in with the same email and it is there.
  • It asks you to sign in. Sign in on that screen. A card or an invitation you opened while signed out is waiting for you after the code.

Your account

Signing in. There is no password. You type your email and we send a code; the code is what you type back.

Deleting your account. In the app or on the web: Settings → Delete account. Your photos, your device tokens and your email address go at once; rounds that other golfers' standings were built from stay on the record with no name on them. The privacy policy says exactly what is kept and why.

The pot. Cup Season keeps the ledger; the money moves between friends. Nothing is paid through the app. See the prize pool disclaimer.
