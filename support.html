Get help

Write to jerecho@fischbeck3.com with what you were trying to do, which phone you were on, and roughly when. Cup Season is built and run by one golfer in Tempe, Arizona; you are writing to him.

If a score did not land, a round posted twice, or you can see something that is not yours, say so first — those are the things we drop everything for.

Install the app

Everything you need is at cupseason.app/get: the web app for any phone, and the iPhone app as it opens up.

If somebody sent you a link — to a round, a card or a season — install the app, then open the link again. The link opens on the web first; once the app is on your phone, opening the same link lands inside it. The app does not remember a link you opened before it was installed, so keep the message it came in.

A link that did not open

  • It opened in the browser instead of the app. Expected before the app is installed. Install it, then open the link again from the message.
  • It says the round was never finished. There is no card to keep — whoever ran the round can tee off again and send you a new link from that round.
  • It says the card was already claimed. Your card is on a record already. Sign in with the same email and it is there.
  • It asks you to sign in. Sign in on that screen. A card or an invitation you opened while signed out is waiting for you after the code, and the app asks before it adds a buddy, takes a seat or puts a round on your record.

Your account

Signing in

There is no password. You type your email and we send a code; the code is what you type back.

Deleting your account

You can do it yourself, on the phone or the web: open You, then Card & settings, and choose Delete my account at the bottom of Settings. It asks once more before anything happens.

Deleting removes your name, handle, photo, email, photos and share images, and the text of your posts and comments, and your notifications stop. Your posted rounds stay in the leagues you played as "Former member", so standings stay true. The privacy policy says exactly what is kept and why.

If you can't get into the app, write to jerecho@fischbeck3.com from the email on the account and we will delete it for you.
