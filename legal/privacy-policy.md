# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 25, 2026

Cup Season is operated by Fischbeck3 LLC. This page says what we collect, why,
who else touches it, and how you leave.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** Your name, email address and handle; your marker; your city
  and home course if you add them; a profile photo if you choose one.
- **Your golf.** The rounds you post (score, course, tees, date), your
  handicap index and how it was set, the leagues you join and what happens in
  them (points, standings, board posts, comments, applause), the live games you
  score, and any photos you attach to a round.
- **Your device.** A push token, only if you turn notifications on. Basic
  diagnostics — crash and error reports and which screens were opened — so we
  can fix what breaks.
- **Your contacts, only if you ask.** When you tap to find friends, we check
  your contacts against golfers who are already here. We send one-way SHA-256
  hashes of the numbers and addresses, never your contacts, and we keep
  nothing that doesn't match.

## What we use it for

To run your account, your leagues and the standings; to send sign-in codes and
league updates; to fix the app. We do not sell your information and we do not
use it for advertising.

## Who else touches it

- **Supabase** — the database, sign-in and storage for photos and share
  images.
- **Netlify** — hosts the web app and these pages.
- **Brevo** — delivers sign-in codes and season emails.
- **Anthropic** — reads a scorecard photo you choose to scan, and only after
  you agree, so the scores can be typed for you. Your photo is not used to
  train its models. You can turn scanning off in Settings.
- **GolfCourseAPI** — course names, ratings and slopes, fetched by our server;
  it never sees you.
- **Open-Meteo** — the forecast for a planned round, looked up by our server
  from the course's location only; it never sees you.
- **Apple** — delivers notifications to the iPhone app, only if you turn them
  on. On the web, your browser's own push service carries them the same way.
- **Google Fonts** — serves the typefaces the web pages use, so it sees your
  IP address and browser when a page loads.
- **esm.sh** — delivers part of the web app's code when it loads, so it sees
  your IP address and browser; never what you type.

Each holds only what its job needs.

## How long we keep it

While your account exists. You can delete your account yourself, on the
phone or the web: open You, then Card & settings, and choose Delete my
account at the bottom of Settings. Deleting removes your name, handle, photo,
email, photos and share images, and the text of your posts and comments; your
notifications stop and your account is closed at once. Your posted rounds
stay in the leagues you played as "Former member", so standings stay true.
Deleted data leaves our live database immediately, photos and share images
leave storage shortly after, and our backups follow on their own rolling
schedule.
