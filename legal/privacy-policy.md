# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 21, 2026

Cup Season is operated by Fischbeck3 LLC. This page says what we collect, why,
who else touches it, and how you leave.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** Your name, email address and handle; your marker; your city
  and home course if you add them; a profile photo if you choose one.
- **Your golf.** The rounds you post (score, course, tees, date), your
  handicap index and how it was set, the leagues you join and what happens in
  them (points, standings, board posts, comments, applause), the live games you
  score, and any photos you attach to a round.
- **Your device.** A push token, only if you turn notifications on. Basic
  diagnostics — crash and error reports and which screens were opened — so we
  can fix what breaks.
- **Your contacts, only if you ask.** When you tap to find friends, we check
  your contacts against golfers who are already here. We send salted hashes,
  never your contacts, and we keep nothing that doesn't match.

## What we use it for

To run your account, your leagues and the standings; to send sign-in codes and
league updates; to fix the app. We do not sell your information and we do not
use it for advertising.

## Who else touches it

- **Supabase** — the database, sign-in and photo storage.
- **Brevo** — delivers sign-in codes and season emails.
- **Netlify** — serves the web app.
- **GolfCourseAPI** — course ratings and slopes, fetched by our server; it
  never sees you.
- **Anthropic** — reads a scorecard photo you choose to scan so the scores can
  be typed for you. Your photo is not used to train its models.

Each holds only what its job needs.

## How long we keep it

While your account exists. You can delete your account from Settings on the
web or the phone: your photos and device tokens are deleted, your email
address is removed from your profile, and your account is closed at once.
Rounds you posted into a league stay in that league's record as "Former
member", because the standings built on them stay true. Deleted data leaves
our live database immediately and our backups on their own rolling schedule.

## Age

You must be at least 13 to use Cup Season.
