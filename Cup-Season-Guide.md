# Cup Season

Season-long fantasy golf for your regular group. You post the rounds you
actually play, from any course, and they count toward a season that ends in a
Cup Final. The app keeps score and keeps the pot's books. The money moves
between friends.

---

## What it is

Most golf apps track your handicap or a single round. Cup Season tracks a whole
season with your friends. Everyone logs real, handicapped rounds wherever they
play. Points build month over month. Captains draft squads, the months pass, and
the endgame settles it.

Two rules make it work:

- **Every number shows its work.** No points total appears without a path back
  to the rounds that produced it. Tap any figure for its receipt.
- **The pot is a ledger.** Buy-ins and payouts move between friends the way you
  already settle up — Venmo, cash, a tab at the turn. Cup Season keeps the
  ledger; the money moves between friends.

## Two ways to play

**Leagues — the long game.** A full season: squads or solo, monthly counting
rounds, a running table, and an endgame the Pro picks when the league is
built — a four-week Cup Final, or the points table crowns the champion. Months
long. This is the franchise.

**Events — the short game.** A weekend or a few weeks, self-contained, its own
trophy. Two are live: **the Ryder** (two teams, weekly one-on-one duels, first
to the clinch) and **a Major** (one window, every card on one board, one name
on the jug). An event stands alone or attaches to a league.

**The tee sheet — one round.** Score the group live on one phone or all of
them: **Match Play**, **Wolf**, **Skins**, with the settle-up at the end. Guests
need no account — a claim link is their pencil during the round and their recap
after. Live rounds start from your league's Clubhouse.

## How scoring works

Every round is measured against your own number. Beat your handicap and the
round says so — **beat your number by 2.1** — and lands in one of five bands:
*Torched it · Beat your number · Played to it · A little loose · Posted anyway.*
Those bands are the currency behind the season table, the duels and the
leaderboards, so a 20-handicap and a scratch player compete on even terms.

You don't need a handicap to start. Give the app a starter index if you have
one; three rounds set your number, and it keeps adjusting as you play. GHIN is
an optional reference on your card, never a requirement.

Each month has a counting cap and a floor. Miss a floor once and the season
forgives it; the Pro can grant a bye for vacations and injuries. Disputes go to
the Pro, whose ruling is logged for everyone to see.

## The pot

Set a buy-in when you start a league. The app prices the pot, the Pro marks
who has paid, and everyone sees the payout split — champion, runner-up, points
leader — from the wizard's dial. You move the actual money yourselves. A league
with no buy-in shows no pot at all.

## User guide

### Getting in

Open **cupseason.app** or the iPhone app and continue with your email. You get
an 8-digit code; type it in. No passwords. On iPhone you can also use **Sign
in with Apple**. Have an invite code? There's a door for it right under the
email.

Then make your card: your name, your **@handle**, your ball marker, and a
starter index if you have one. The handle is yours — it can change once every
60 days, and the league is told when it does.

### The four places

- **Home** — everything you're in, one feed: your standing up top, your
  buddies' rounds under it, and the doors to start something new.
- **Clubhouse** — one league's room: Standings, the Board (chat and round
  posts), the Schedule, the Pot, the Album, the League itself. Switch groups
  with the chips up top.
- **⊕** — one door for before, during and after a round: post after, score the
  group live during, put a tee time up before.
- **You** — your card and index, your record, your trophies, your buddies. The
  ⚙ on your card runs everything else. *You › How it works* reopens this
  walkthrough any time.

### Post a round

Tap **⊕ › Post**. Front nine, back nine, pick the course — twenty seconds.
Nine holes is a real round, not eighteen with blanks. The app computes the
rest and sends the round to every league you're in. Rounds live on your card
for good — leagues just read them.

Have the paper scorecard? **Scan the card** and the app reads it, the whole
group at once. Hole-by-hole scoring lives on the tee sheet, during play, not
in the post form.

### Start a league

From **Home**, tap **Start a league**. Name it, add golfers (find them by name
or @handle, or invite by email), set the buy-in, the season length, the format
and the endgame, then **Lock it**. Squads form, everyone joins with the league
code or an invite link, and play begins at the first tee.

### Start an event

From **Home**, tap **Start an event** and pick the Ryder or a Major. Name it,
set the window, add players, and go.

### Score a round live

From your league's Clubhouse, tap **Play now**. Pick the game, add the group —
league mates or guests — and everyone scores from their own phone. When it's
over, your card posts, attested by the group, and the settle-up hits the
board.

### Buddies

A buddy is mutual. The magnifier up top finds golfers by name or @handle;
**You › Your buddies** keeps them. Buddies see each other's rounds and share a
tee sheet — nothing to do with leagues or points. Once someone is a buddy you
can pull them into any league or event with one tap, no email needed.

### Trophies

Win a Cup Final, a points crown or an event and a trophy lands in the case on
your card. It stays there, season after season. That is your record.
