# Cup Season Ember logo explorations — v1

Ten unapproved visual sketches derived from the Ember Logo System spec. Open `contact-sheet.html` for the review board. These files are staged outside production brand paths and do not replace the current mark.

## Concepts

1. **Ember Marker** — physical ball-marker silhouette, flag/cup relief, and three visible heat steps.
2. **Cup, Held** — trophy cup and cup-on-the-green become one destination; the flag carries live ember.
3. **Rollout Loop** — motion-native putt route moving through season checkpoints before settling.
4. **Four-Round Pennant** — accumulated rounds create a planted, progressive finish.
5. **Crew Convergence** — opposing crew paths share one season ring and converge at the cup.
6. **Ledger Flame** — the durable season record stacks toward a live result.
7. **Season Dial** — a marker-like four-phase dial records progression around the cup.
8. **Cup Season Knot** — geometric C-shaped season track and S-shaped putt resolve at the cup.
9. **Flag Flame** — one strong ember silhouette contains the flag, golf cup, and progression notches.
10. **Legacy Ember Orbit** — the required legacy-evolution control, translated to D76 semantics.

## Recommended comparison set

Advance **01, 02, 03, and 09** against **10** as the control. That narrows the exploration to four new territories plus exactly one legacy control, includes a motion-native route, and spans marker, cup/trophy, rollout, and ember-silhouette directions.

## Status and constraints

- Exploration only; no artwork is approved by gates or human studies.
- Uses only charcoal `#0C0D0F`, paper `#EFF2EE`, slate `#8E979E`, amber `#E9A23B`, and ember `#FF5A2E`.
- Earned champagne gold is intentionally absent because none of these static marks depicts an earned outcome.
- Flat vectors only: no gradients, shadows, textures, raster images, text, or runtime font dependencies.
- All SVGs include accessible titles/descriptions and a finite `0 0 240 240` view box.
- Small-size, contrast, crop, monochrome, engraving, embroidery, distinctiveness, association, Hat, and Archive gates remain untested.
