# The Marker — candidate mark (2026-09-11)

**Status: CANDIDATE.** Nothing in `brand/` has moved. These are the masters the
family would regenerate from if the owner picks it (`tools/make-icons.py`
would be re-pointed at this geometry; the two favicon data-URIs in `index.html`
and `legal.html` and the iOS `AppIcon.appiconset` follow).

**What it is.** Territory 1 of `spec/brand-canon.md` §6 — the direction
`spec/mark-execution-brief.md` ADOPTED on 2026-07-17 and no attempt since has
executed: the mark *is* a ball marker, a stamped coin, champagne on dusk. The
ball marker is already the in-app identity token, so brand mark, profile
identity and the one piece of merch a golfer would use become one object.

**Why it belongs to the app now.** The flag is the tab band's own pennant
(`CSDesign/Chrome.swift`, the glyph LINT-28 reserves for the tab band and the
app icon) at 2.67×, filled as relief. The coin is `CSMedallion` — the ceremony
ground, a gold ring, a drawn mark in ceremony gold — which has been on every
golfer's credential since the overhaul. The mark is the medallion's obverse.
